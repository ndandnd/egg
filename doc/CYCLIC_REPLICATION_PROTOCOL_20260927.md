# Prospective exact cyclic-fleet replication check

Status: derived candidate formulas and fixed verification design, before execution.
This extends the replenished construction at 7bf913a. It is distinct from PR54's
finite-menu aggregation laboratory: it embeds the scaling question in continuous
charging and complete, fully replenished fleet schedules. No novelty priority
is claimed for the general distinction between efficiency and incentives.

## Question and scope

As service demand and electricity supply capacity scale together, can the
absolute physical-versus-hull cost gap vanish while the whole operator's regret
at its physical optimum's own marginal price does not? This is a single
price-taking operator. Its deviation may reassign all mandatory services;
it is not an individual bus's or an independent firm's incentive. Regret per
bus and relative regret must also be reported to prevent a normalization error.

## Replicated physical model

For integer n>=1 there are n identical A services in hour [0,1] and n identical
B services in [2,3], each consuming 15 kWh. Each of at most 2n used buses starts
and ends at capacity 20 kWh, with zero reserve and no deadheads/auxiliaries.
Every used bus covers at least one service. Identical simultaneous services
cannot share a bus; thus any partition has m two-service buses, n-m A-only buses
and n-m B-only buses, where 0<=m<=n. Buses cost 7 each.

Charging is continuous and lossless. The early [1,2] window has n connectors
at 10 kW each and shared power 10n kW; the terminal [3,4] window has n connectors
at 30 kW each and shared power 30n kW. Connector assignments can change without
time overhead between charging sessions. These are synthetic resources scaled
with demand, not an empirical infrastructure model.

The exact aggregate early range for partition m is [10m,10n]. Necessity follows
because each A+B bus needs 10 early kWh, each A-only bus can receive at most10,
and a B-only full bus cannot accept early energy. Sufficiency: allocate10 to
each A+B bus and u=(x-10m)/(n-m) to each A-only bus when m<n. Then their terminal
charges are20, 15-u and15 respectively. Pair each A-only/B-only bus on one
terminal connector and charge sequentially; combined energy30-u<=30. Allocate
one terminal connector to each A+B bus. This uses n connectors, stays within
power and SOC limits, and restores every battery. At m=n, x=10n and only the
A+B construction is needed. All partitions buy exactly30n kWh.

## Economic scaling and candidate closed forms

Use F_n(E,L)=4E+(E^2+L^2)/(10n) on nonnegative supply. Thus F_n=nF_1(E/n,L/n),
which keeps marginal prices at fixed per-copy demand constant as n grows.
Changing n with unscaled curvature would be a different experiment.

For each integer m, independently minimize

    H_n(m,x)=14n-7m+4x+[x^2+(30n-x)^2]/(10n),  10m<=x<=10n.

The branch minimum is x=max(5n,10m). The complete projected hull is the
triangle generated by the all-paired point and the two all-single endpoints.
Its cheapest intrinsic cost at x is14n-7x/10, so

    CH_n=7591n/80, x*=27n/4, m*=27n/40.

For m>=n/2 the physical objective is CH_n+20(m-m*)^2/n. For m<n/2 the
branch-minimal objective is99n-7m, strictly decreasing. For n>=3 the nearest
integer to m* is at least n/2; n=1,2 can be checked directly. Hence every
physical optimizer is a nearest integer to m*, with both ties retained.
For delta=m-m*, the candidate gap is20delta^2/n (at most5/n).

At such a physical optimizer, p=(4+2m/n,6-2m/n). Since p_early>=p_terminal,
the complete price response chooses each partition's minimum early charge.
Its linear objective is194n-60m+(40m/n-27)k for candidate paired count k.
The minimizing response has k=0 if delta>0, k=n if delta<0, and any k if zero.
Thus the candidate whole-operator own-price regret is

    delta>0: 40(m/n)delta;
    delta<0: 40(1-m/n)(-delta);
    delta=0: 0.

For n=40k+1, delta=13/40: gap=169/(80n), relative gap=169/(7591n^2),
and regret=351/40+169/(40n). The first two vanish while absolute whole-operator
regret approaches8.775. Regret per used bus and relative regret still vanish.
For n divisible by40, gap and regret are exactlyzero. Do not present a monotone
regret limit over all n. Tie sizes n=20 modulo40 have two planner optimizers
with different own-price regrets; record both rather than favoring one.

At the common hull price(107/20,93/20), every physical planner optimizer has
fleet LOC0 and supply LOC equal to its gap. Its supplier conjugate is23445n/400
and the complete fleet response value is307n/2. This is a different price from
each schedule's own gradient, with no budget-balance mechanism asserted.

## Frozen verification and reporting

Use exact Fraction arithmetic, no solver and no existing experimental input.
Fixed grid: every integer n=1..80, plus120,200,400,401,1000,1001 (86 cases).
This covers two complete residue cycles, exact-zero sizes, ties and the stated
subsequence. It is an explanatory identity check, not sampled prevalence.

The driver enumerates all integer partitions and minimizes each continuous
quadratic branch. It derives a lower hull from all branch endpoints and
minimizes every hull edge independently of the candidate closed forms. It
enumerates all partition endpoints again for each physical optimum's linear
response. Every reported optimum carries aggregate SOC/charging templates,
common-price accounts and all tied optimizer records. Candidate formulas are
checked against those calculations, with all cases retained.

Commit source and this protocol before execution. Use a new attempt directory,
record Git/source/protocol hashes and preserve any failure. One local process,
30-second external cap, no cluster resources. No success-based parameter edits
or retries within the attempt. A non-author review must reconstruct the
aggregate feasible set, price-taking interpretation and results before any
claim enters the manuscript. The original 24-case result remains immutable.
