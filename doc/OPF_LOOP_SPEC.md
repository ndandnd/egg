# Matched two-window dispatch on a German feeder

Frozen design for the first matched dispatch comparison, 3 November 2026. This measures duty commitment, physical dispatch and whole-schedule convexification. It does not test the convergence of the four earlier loop hypotheses. Source implementation, analysis and command will be pinned in the launch record before reading evaluation outputs. The user explicitly requested this run.

**Assignments.** Use all combinations of both previously chosen native SimBench 20-kV sites (near 78 and far 68), both admitted DE price days (17 January and 17 July 2024), and the previous 10/40/80% charger designs. Use their fixed original n and whole 125 kW plugs. All comparisons within a row have identical services, native feeder, availability, fee, capacity and background. Site-to-site differences include different fleet sizes. Do not select active hours, high prices, days, buses or binding cases after evaluation. Record all assignments, infeasibility, inaccurate returns, budget stops, missing checks and denominators. No threshold tuning or stress replacements.

**Award rules at a grid limit.** Primary institution: hard native voltage/current limits and no load shedding, substitute supply, grid reinforcement or ad hoc price at an infeasible request. Sensitivities: the same limits plus a hypothetical dispatchable 1 MW local active-power unit at the depot, continuously available at unity power factor. Use two flat offers, €414.7235/MWh and €510.5304/MWh, equally in every quarter, site, season and fleet. They are fuel-only engineering scenarios from one 50 Hz 1 MW standby generator using 251.5 L/h at full or 77.4 L/h at 25% load, valued at Germany's 2024 average retail diesel price €1.649/L. Both offers use average output-specific fuel cost as an explicit linear proxy. These are neither measured marginal costs at these depots nor prices of unserved bus trips. Exclude startup, capacity, wear, taxes adjustment and distinct emissions charges. Include generated MWh and cost in G. Only an interior active offer pins the local price. Procure residual energy at the actual period DE spot price, linear with signed export settlement. Preserve negative prices and reverse AC flows. [Caterpillar 3512 data sheet](https://emc.cat.com/pubdirect.ashx?media_string_id=LEHE1291-) · [ADAC retrospective](https://presse.adac.de/meldungen/adac-ev/verkehr/diesel-im-november-um-mehr-als-fuenf-cent-teurer-als-im-oktober.html)

**Time and fleets.** In the primary comparison use 12:00–13:00 and 22:00–24:00 Berlin local time. Within each window sustain constant depot MW while independently constraining every 15-minute grid snapshot. Assign the actual spot price for its delivery hour. The load/PV proxy uses the same ISO week and weekday in the 2016 SimBench profiles (20 January and 20 July); these are constructed operating days. Fixed tap and switch states, unscaled native profiles and source voltage, native thermal and voltage limits, no discretionary DER curtailment. Costs include only the listed operating windows, and a €50 fee for each used bus-day. Annual savings and actual German depot hosting capacity are outside scope. Test (a) heterogeneous published Sistig-derived energies from the first single-depot operator in archived order, Euskirchen, and (b) per-batch mean energies from the same selected runs, matched on total morning and afternoon demand. Use deterministic equal-rank midpoint quantiles resampled to n runs per batch, no trimming failing runs, complete possible successor graph, no additional transition energy. This is a derived depot-scale two-peak schedule, not an observed assignment. Battery 350 nominal/320 usable kWh, charge efficiency .95, preemptive power, start/end full. Raw source: draft1 peak_runs.json at paper-worktree revision 908ba228210f2f0150443100ca964bf27758433c, original Sistig et al. [dataset](https://doi.org/10.6084/m9.figshare.26088190.v1).

Secondary windows are 12:00–13:00 and 22:00–23:00; retain the original 140/90 kWh homogeneous fixture and the same selected heterogeneous services. Archive feasibility already reveals at least one incompatible afternoon run in every one-hour heterogeneous size: this secondary group remains assigned. The extension of the terminal window was chosen from technical charging feasibility before inspecting feeder study outcomes. Primary and secondary are reported separately. Each design has 72 assignments including the three market rules, for 144 total.

**Baselines and estimands.** Price-independent duty commitment chooses a fixed hash permutation of within-batch energy ranks at minimum chargeable bus count. Original run identifiers were discarded in the archive. This tie rule ignores the magnitudes and grid prices (if that committed cover is not chargeable, record failure). Strong fixed-cover control chooses the widest feasible interval at that same minimum count. Optimize charging and the grid within each cover. Joint physical dispatch may change the number or identity of paired duties, with a reconstructible whole-bus witness. Hull dispatch optimizes over the exact convex hull of the same energy–fee intervals. No mixing grid outcomes before imposing common AC limits at the mean. Report total resource cost, D−H, fixed−D, buses/pairs, early/late kWh, smallest and largest same-count forced charge, physical grid load and backup, grid marginal price, grid and charging feasibility. A wholesale-only result is a separate analytical control using the same two windows. Mean-price spot arbitrage and the complete-day range are different. Report fraction of cells and fraction of distinct quarter-dispatches with native voltage within 1e-5 p.u. or terminal current within 1e-4 of its rating; exclude the fixed slack voltage. Report voltage and thermal separately, separate local resource bounds from grid congestion. Unresolved cells cannot be used as non-binding observations.

**Prediction and inference.** Prespecify 0 ≤ D−H < €50 for finite feasible cases meeting the consecutive, convex nested-layer assumptions, proved in [the rounding note](opf/SUPPORT_BOUND.md). A lower bound proving a positive gap and a positive numerical point difference are different statements. Crossing a forced level or finding a binding constraint alone predicts neither a cycle nor strictly positive gap. Report whether grid conditions move prices enough to justify extra buses, and whether changing the pairing improves on the price-independent cover while fleet size is fixed. Preserve a null or grid-infeasible result. The spread €527.78/MWh belongs only to the original homogeneous, two one-hour fixture and €50 scenario. It is not transferred to other fleets/windows. Use paired differences in EUR for these scenarios. No population/significance or general ML claim.

**Numerical deviation, declared before date runs.** Static runs had different failures. The earlier independent relative threshold was 1e-5 (not 1e-4); 1e-4 was the physical-unit AC agreement tolerance. Preserve all original raw verdicts. New criterion: on each branch separately require absolute cone discrepancy, transformed to MVA², ≤ 1e-5 MVA² + 1e-5 × max(|sending voltage² × series current²|, |series complex power|²) in MVA². This fixes the absolute scale to the earlier 10 MVA audit base and remains stringent on materially loaded branches. Require native optimal status, balances and operating limits within 1e-7 on the 10 MVA reference base (voltage in p.u.), and independently solved AC flow within 1e-4 in p.u., MW, Mvar and kA at compared terminals. No upgraded optimal_inaccurate statuses. Report any Gurobi dependency/license limitation. Verify physical schedules and mean-load network dispatches separately. Marginal loss and congestion effects are both possible. Record a binding flag as activity, not proof of a positive shadow price. A €0.01 comparison resolution is a display threshold, not a proof of an exact zero. Numerical optimizer reports are distinct from rigorous lower bounds. Any nonphysical conic optimum is reported unqualified even if the AC physics check at another point passes.

On the four prespecified primary 80% Sistig hard-limit rows with qualified joint dispatch, perturb each window's grid load separately by ±1 kWh holding the other constant. Report feasibility/status and one-sided finite price secants from strict mixed-screen conic points. These are local numerical diagnostics, without an additional AC admission or proof of price uniqueness. Retain missing probes and all four rows.
