## Physical network, costs and units

Fix a balanced, radial, connected MV circuit and orient every in-service edge from its parent i to child j. Fix the slack squared voltage. In one interval of duration h hours let d and q be nodal consumption net of uncontrollable production. Convert depot energy e_t in kWh into d at the depot by adding e_t/(1000 h) MW. Positive e is import, negative e is AC-side V2G export. Map battery energy through the charging/discharging efficiencies once on the fleet side. Unity depot power factor is the primary convention. Costs use EUR/MWh. One per-unit power is S_base MW at unity power factor. Use p.u. impedances, squared magnitudes for voltage v and series current l. For a series-only line the sending-end equations are

    P_ij = d_j − g_j + sum_child P_jk + r_ij l_ij
    Q_ij = q_j − gQ_j + sum_child Q_jk + x_ij l_ij
    v_j  = v_i − 2(r_ij P_ij+x_ij Q_ij) + (r_ij²+x_ij²) l_ij
    norm((2P_ij, 2Q_ij, v_i−l_ij)) <= v_i+l_ij,   l_ij >= 0.

Bus powers in these equations are per unit. Sum powers at the root, including root consumption. Include both voltage bounds, actual thermal ratings and transformer/connection caps where known. Zero MATPOWER rateA denotes an absent rating. It cannot be used as a physical zero or invented nameplate. At shaped public-feeder study sites use [0.95,1.05] p.u. as a declared study assumption. For validating the unmodified MATPOWER/pandapower base PF use its benchmark operating point with the published numerical convention and broad feasible voltage bounds. Forcing 0.95 on the native base would change the reference problem. Report import P,Q, active losses and minimum voltage at the original bus ID.

Minimize upstream procurement plus local generator resource costs, multiplied by interval duration. A linear or convex piecewise-linear offer and a quadratic offer are separate arms. Keep grid procurement out of fleet operating fees. The full physical objective is c_s + V(e_s) for a complete schedule s. Subtracting a common V(0) changes only reported levels. The real day-ahead substation price is exogenous and can be negative. An OPF on those prices may prefer artificial losses under relaxation. Keep these observations and screen exactness. Qualify an ideal lossless connector separately by eliminating its redundant current variable and recovering physical current from P,Q,v. A lossless calibration in the original laboratory's currency per kWh entails no currency conversion or empirical euro claim.

**Scope of the branch model.** Begin with fixed topology and balanced series lines. Native SimBench MV cables, taps, transformer and shunt currents need explicit translation to a common SOCP/AC model with the selected switch state. A native feeder with parallel transformers is not automatically the simple tree above. Disallow unsupported elements or record the reduction, then measure its difference on the native model. Every unverified case remains pending. LinDistFlow is the lossless comparison. Use selected independent AC Newton power flows at the conic dispatch, plus explicitly identified local AC OPF as an additional baseline. Pandapower runopp uses PYPOWER, not IPOPT. ## What price does the operator receive?

Define V as the extended-value grid cost (infinite for infeasible loads). A conic optimal multiplier is a supporting price under strong duality, primal and dual attainment, and a balance written with the right sign. For a MW perturbation in a slot, divide its currency derivative by h to get EUR/MWh. For a p.u. perturbation divide by h S_base. To invoice legacy kWh, divide the per-MWh price by 1000. At fixed nonunity power factor include the Q-balance contribution to the total derivative. A binding limit is compatible with a unique marginal value. Convex piecewise linear costs only kink where the active slope changes. Unequal duals on redundant constraints may induce the same depot price.

At each study dispatch evaluate left and right value differences with independently solved neighbors on a prespecified shrinking physical scale. Store objective error relative to step size, admissibility, and active constraints for every solve. Convexity orders the outer secant slopes. Smooth curvature itself separates finite secants. A gap at one step is insufficient evidence for nonuniqueness. Certify slopes where possible by minimizing and maximizing the *depot price* over optimal dual witnesses, with tolerance accounting. At the boundary an infeasible outward perturbation is not a second finite price. For separable periods the joint subdifferential is the Cartesian product of per-period sets. With any ramp/storage/other temporal constraint require a *joint* supporting dual or a conjugate-residual check. For V=max(x,y) at the origin, (1,1) passes both coordinate intervals but is not a supporting price. [Papavasiliou](https://doi.org/10.1109/TSG.2017.2673860) · [Gong et al.](https://doi.org/10.1109/TCNS.2019.2920587)

## When can an AC result be reported?

Check the hypotheses as well as the cone. With passive fixed loads, no generation below the root and no export, upper downstream net injections are nonpositive; the positive parts in the C1 path matrices of Gan et al. vanish in their orientation. Positive line r,x satisfy the resulting path test. The lossless upper-voltage envelope can verify C2 for those passive injections. Record qualifications for binding ampacities, source-objective monotonicity, reverse flow, voltage rise and shunts. For an ideal zero-impedance connector use the analytical equality instead. The original theorem does not certify every grid in this brief.

For every solution check native status, feasibility of power/voltage balances, physical limits, cone slack, and objective. Report absolute cone error and abs(P²+Q²−v_i l)/max(v_i l,floor), with floor identified. Solve independent AC PF on selected dispatches and compare node voltages, imports and line flows. A converged PF can be physically infeasible. A recovered feasible physical point gives an upper bound, not proof of global optimality without a valid lower bound. Pointwise agreement in cost does not establish agreement of one-sided AC prices. Distinguish (a) conic relaxation error, (b) fleet indivisibility gap on the chosen grid value function, and (c) restricted-column or solver uncertainty. A lack of conic exactness at an iterate disqualifies that AC price interpretation. No blanket transfer of convex subgradients or hull lower bounds into physical support claims. ## Certificate that must accompany the coordination experiment

Use consistent energy units throughout this derivation. Let S contain entire feasible schedules including continuous dispatch alternatives, their grid energies e_s and resource fees c_s. On the conic grid let

    D = inf_s c_s + V(e_s)
    H = inf_{λ,L} sum_s λ_s c_s + V(L),    L = sum_s λ_s e_s, λ in simplex.

Bounds use the convex hull of joint (e,c) records. Introduce p times (sum λ e − L) in the Lagrangian. Taking the infimum over λ gives min_s(c_s+p·e_s); over L it gives −V*(p). For ANY p,

    H >= bound_fleet_min(p) − bound_grid_conjugate_max(p).

The first bound must be a lower bound, the second an upper bound on sup_L(p·L−V(L)). A maximizing grid incumbent alone has the wrong sign. An AC-feasible mixture-mean grid dispatch plus a convex combination of valid columns gives a restricted-master upper bound for H; a feasible complete fleet and grid dispatch gives a distinct upper bound for D. A conic relaxation can lower-bound the corresponding physical optimum, with the model scope recorded. Infeasible restricted masters require a separately priced feasible reference or a declared phase-I feasibility search. A failed start stays in the assigned denominator. Report the gap interval [max(0,LB_D−UB_H), UB_D−LB_H] for like formulations, only with valid ordered bounds. A small upper endpoint certifies a tolerance, not equality. The positive operated-plan regret equals c_s+p·e_s−min_s'(c_s'+p·e_s'), in addition to any grid-side lost opportunity. Do not describe all planner difference as an observed payment. A finite collection of exactly priced columns does not automatically give finite termination with a curved network master or continuous fleet actions. Check against complete tiny enumeration before any certification claim. [Hua & Baldick](https://arxiv.org/abs/1605.05002) · [Andrianesis et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/2012.13331)
