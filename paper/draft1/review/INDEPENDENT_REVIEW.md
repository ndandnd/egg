## Matching and price formation

The principal matching proof survived. After a matching fixes the duties, each bus admits an interval of net battery exchange. At fixed matching size the sums of lower and upper bounds and the vehicle fee are invariant. Only the total mandatory positive battery gain depends on the pairing. The reviewer independently reconstructed the interval allocation, the finite neutral cycles needed for losses and exports, the clipping of the first threshold, and the argument for unequal batch sizes. A new comparison with a general assignment solver agreed at all 64 checked cardinalities within 6e−12 kWh. That comparison tests the matching calculation in the stored charge-only study. The constructive proof supplies the continuum result.

The original endpoint-recurrence claim was false. With three unit services per batch, unit batteries, fee one and a valid interior tie selection, prices alternate between 1 and 1/3 while loads alternate between 1 and 3. The leftmost exposed load, zero, never appears. The revised theorem distinguishes the two strict sides from the exact tie. It gives the step condition at strict vertices, including a vertex exactly at a price switch. It specifies the hypotheses for recurrent extremes. A separate example supplied by the novelty reviewer had loads 1,0,0 on a period-three orbit with a constant half step and a flat-face optimum at 1/4. The arithmetic mean is 1/3. The paper restricts exact load and fee recovery to diminishing-step weighted averages with affine marginal spreads. Daily costs still apply to the physical schedules operated on each day.

The load coordinate and the joint load–cost point had also been conflated. All loads between the minimum and maximum can occur in the lossless charge-only model. Support fails when no fleet attains the hull operating cost at the optimal load. An uplift at the hull tariff is contingent on dispatch and need not make that tariff the marginal cost at the dispatch's own load.

Two additions answer the reviewer’s question about the consequence of the reduction.

- For a marginal spread consisting of an intercept plus a strictly increasing function of early load, each adjacent pair of physical thresholds gives one open interval of failure intercepts. Adjacent bands can touch only at a supported breakpoint. Their total width on the real axis telescopes, independently of the positive bus fee. Intersecting the bands with the study window exposed four additional operators at 300 kWh. The second reader checked the derivation for clipped and empty layers.
- Pair-specific transition draws allow one representative matching per group with the same number of pairs and the same total transition draw. In a complete, equal-size, lossless charge-only perfect layer where every perfect matching is physically feasible, a single representative exists exactly when every two-by-two exchange preserves the sum of edge draws. This is equivalent to additive row and column potentials. The exchange characterization is classical. Its physical implication is the precise boundary for one-matching replacement in this layer.

## Novelty and venue assessment

The novelty reviewer identified established assignment/charging formulations, cost-aware aggregation of prescribed resource sets and a 2026 fleet-size Pareto-frontier study. A weighted sweep that selects optimal schedules and an exact replacement of the whole attainable joint set answer different questions. The reviewer found no direct prior statement of the narrow replacement theorem in the primary sources inspected. Priority across the broader literature remains open. [Müller et al., Sections IV–V](https://arxiv.org/abs/1705.02815) · [Dabčević et al., Section 4.2](https://doi.org/10.3390/en19030867)

The existing EV update in Ma et al. already moves posted prices toward marginal generation cost. For an affine spread and feasible initialization, the draft's update is the load component of ordinary Frank–Wolfe. Generic convergence and ergodic recovery consequently carry little novelty here. The application-specific results concern the exposed physical choices, admissible tie selections and the exact geometry of support failure.
