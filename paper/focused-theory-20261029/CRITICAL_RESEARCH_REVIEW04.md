## Priority1: establish the specific contribution's significance against overlapping theory

The strongest rejection remains “correct composition of classical tools under tailored assumptions.” The load-only projection is simple; common windows and uniform acceptance make serial feasibility depend on total throughput, while zero transition energy makes service consumption assignment-independent. Classical matching convexity then organizes the fleet-cost layers. Common losses add meaningful content: despite two load coordinates and gross connector use, one cardinality statistic still produces at most two efficient segments. That is a stronger lead than accumulating more special-case examples.

Current related work now acknowledges materially overlapping operational V2G bus models and exact lossy storage reformulations. Thus the distinction must be the explicit assignment-dependent cost-labelled classification and its consequences, not full inventories, signed storage, endogenous vehicle counts, a physical counterexample in isolation or a first transport/grid coupling. The paper should explain what a reader can decide from the classification that a generic MILP or fixed-population aggregation theorem does not directly reveal. The public certificate supports the mechanism's existence in a richer declared problem; it does not by itself make the closed-form theorem deep or unprecedented.

Bounded useful action: write one precise contribution comparison keyed to the exact theorem statements and already inspected closest sources. Keep the two pieces of original content visible—physical projection/efficient-envelope reduction and the resulting support regimes—while treating matching and support duality as tools. Do not expand the survey or manufacture a new method claim. If no stronger distinction is supported by the primary text, retain the modest classification claim and accept the venue/significance limitation.

## Priority2: separate mathematical existence from calibrated relevance and institutional interpretation

The public separation is rigorous, but its small lower bound is neither the exact gap nor a measured welfare loss. A larger gap is not established or ruled out. Synthetic energy/economic coefficients, one altered service block, zero reserve, ideal charging and full terminal inventory do not establish a deployed tariff failure, repeat-day feasibility, prevalence or an operator's monetary savings. Fee stability is useful evidence against dependence on one fee value; it is not robustness to conversion losses, charging access, reserve, delays or terminal requirements in that public model. The analytical lossy theorem covers a different common-window class.

The fixed-price whole-owner diagnostic is coherent. It does not model a strategic owner anticipating a tariff reset, and the reserved-pair replication is not a unilateral-equilibrium model for separately owned buses sharing an unrestricted connector. Current framework wording preserves these distinctions. The positive-gap result excludes all physical own-load-supported plans in its declared set, not support at every arbitrary posted tariff or failure of all pricing mechanisms.

Bounded useful action: keep a compact model/scope comparison adjacent to the public result, explicitly distinguishing the lossless classification, common-loss monotone reduction, and varied-time charge-only certificate. Interpret the public result as an exact existence demonstration under stated assumptions. Do not launch outcome-seeking cases to make the numerical separation look larger. Operational significance needs independently justified calibration or a separately scoped physical-parameter study; it cannot be fixed editorially within this draft.

## Priority3: make the exact result independently inspectable without overselling independence

The final public inference has the correct direction: an all-physical lower strictly exceeds a feasible complete-plan hull upper. Two-bus branching alone would be insufficient, but the physical one-bus obstruction and objective-specific three-plus floor complete coverage; hull mixtures remain unrestricted. The new fee argument correctly shifts only the two-bus subset and uses an explicit nonnegative-fee affine floor for higher counts. Common losses are presented as an efficient reduction under monotone economics, not equality of full physical sets or a new scalar support criterion. These correctness boundaries should survive compression into the abstract/conclusion.

The remaining reproducibility risk is reader trust in a long internal transfer chain. An independently formulated rational checker and physical replay are stronger than native agreement but are still internal work. Source-bound statuses, historical failure preservation and paid resource closure are valuable provenance; they do not replace a mathematical statement of the model-to-certificate map. In particular, a reader needs to see why every feasible declared schedule is represented or relaxed by the lower-bound model, why branch children exhaust its original binaries, and why each upper component is genuinely physical. No need exists to know either exact optimum or to run an additional price query for the sign.

Bounded useful action: provide a single certificate index mapping each displayed public inequality to immutable source/input/output identities, checker entry points, exact constants, declared model scope and a short verification recipe. Distinguish an optional expensive reproduction of native search from verification of retained rational evidence. If the latter is not yet packaged for an external reader, say so; do not call it external replication. The in-progress lossy suite can qualify its finite implementation only after closed receipts; source review or ten passing literals would not independently prove the continuum theorem, generic tractability, capped-idle runtime coverage or practical relevance.

## ICML fit and first-draft judgment

There is currently no substantive learning contribution. No statistical estimation problem, learned decision rule, learning-theoretic guarantee, data-dependent generalization claim or evaluated learning method is established. A rational certificate is a verification result, not machine learning; a synthetic parameter study and a public timetable do not change that. My scientific-fit judgment is that an ICML submission would face a fundamental scope objection unless a genuinely central learning question were developed as separate research. Adding an ML label, a small predictor benchmark or learning-to-select-node experiment would dilute this paper rather than repair that objection.

The natural contribution is optimization/energy-market/scheduling theory with exact computational evidence. It can be an impressive first draft on those terms, but correctness plus exactness does not guarantee sufficient novelty for a particular venue. The best remaining work is a sharper original-result hierarchy and a short externally legible certificate chain, followed by clean scope-consistent presentation. Further toy checks, more failure archaeology or arbitrary gap enlargement are lower priorities. Do not force a learning story or describe the manuscript as submission-ready before an outside domain expert assesses novelty and significance.
