# Model-to-lower-certificate coverage review

Source-only mathematical coverage review. Read EVIDENCE_MAP.md, ENERGY_FLOW_FORMULATION.md, the necessary per-leg rows in energy_flow.py/exact_dual.py, descriptor validators/window partition in native_recharge.py, the narrow public-case adapter, and the paper's public model/certificate appendix. No actual descriptor, source data, plans, rows, compressed packets, certificate vectors, imports, tests, hashes or numerical audits were inspected. This is a proof of a model inclusion under explicit assumptions, not verification that a larger operational opportunity set was exhaustively generated from the timetable.

## Verdict and material scope clarification

**Every physical schedule in the finite declared-movement model maps into the exact energy-flow model with the same load and operating cost. No invalid added row/cut was found within that model.** This establishes the needed lower-bound direction independently of builder/checker agreement.

However, the public paper description should explicitly identify that finite model. The source requires graph_scope=`declared-movement-modes-only`: each selectable movement has fixed leg departure/arrival times and energies, and charging access is attached only to one specified depot gap or a terminal pull-in window. The public prose currently names a complete directed deadhead matrix without stating all these restrictions. A complete matrix is not itself proof that all possible departure-time choices, extra depot visits, discretionary detours, or other charging opportunities are represented. No such broader completeness theorem has been established here. Treat this as a material claim-scope clarification before saying “all physical fleets,” not as a reason to silently invalidate or extend the already certified declared-instance result.

The declared opportunity set must therefore mean: choose complete service-covering paths from exactly the fixed movement modes in the pinned descriptor; use its fleet cap, leg times/energies and access windows; schedule charging continuously only within that access. Services have fixed times, nonnegative battery consumption and no in-service charging. Stored leg energies include whatever waiting consumption the declared instance assigns; there is no extra implicit draw between checked leg boundaries, and depot-parked draw is zero. No V2G or additional storage is admitted. One physical bus is one complete path; returning to the depot does not create a new full initial battery or a free unlisted connection.

## Constructive physical-to-row map

Take any such feasible physical fleet.

1. **Routing and binaries.** Set y_m=1 exactly for its chosen movements and zero otherwise. Each mandatory service has one predecessor and one successor, so cover_in/cover_out equal1. Each used path begins with one pullout and ends with one pull-in, proving fleet balance and the declared fleet cap. Fixed service durations are positive and internal movements obey end-before-next-start chronology; hence service arcs form a DAG. There is no disconnected cycle that avoids the path interpretation. Parallel allowed modes are retained as distinct binary alternatives, not collapsed by endpoint pair.

2. **Perspective inventory.** On every selected movement, assign s_entry and the successive leg-arrival/depot-departure/terminal states to the physical battery inventory at those exact boundaries. On an unselected movement assign all states and charges zero. Physical capacity/reserve gives r*y<=s<=B*y, and global box0<=s<=B adds no restriction. The pullout equality is the genuinely full starting battery, not an aggregate fleet energy credit.

3. **Per-leg and service conservation.** Each noncharging leg satisfies s_next−s_previous+energy_leg*y=0 with its ORIGINAL exact stored coefficient. No newly rounded sum is used. At a selected depot gap, total grid purchase q raises inventory by eta*q. A pull-in's terminal charge ends at B*y. At every service exactly one selected incoming exit and one outgoing entry remain in the unconditional service_energy row; their difference equals that mandatory service's energy. The row does not multiply mandatory service demand by a fractional arc choice. These facts establish every conservation equality for the physical assignment.

4. **Continuous charging to interval allocations.** Partition time by all market/resource boundaries, terminal opening and every declared charge-window endpoint. These endpoints are exact stored half-minute times in the semantic model; charging decisions inside the intervals remain continuous. Integrate each movement visit's physical grid charging over each elementary interval to obtain q_mk. A window either contains that entire interval or is absent, so no valid physical session is omitted. One shared connector, homogeneous acceptance, and grid limit imply sum_m q_mk<=min(P_bus,P_grid)*duration. A single selected visit's q_mk obeys the same bound and q_mk<=capacity*y_m. With no connector or zero power the integral is zero. These rows are necessary for any physical charging trajectory; their necessity does not depend on claiming arbitrary fractional schedules are physically serializable.

5. **Load and objective.** Summing q_mk by market ownership equals actual hourly purchases. Load upper boxes are the interval capacity sums and therefore cannot cut off a physical load. Fee is charged once on each pullout, and monetary movement cost is the stored per-minute rate times declared leg durations. Thus objective coefficients reproduce the declared operating cost and original stored-binary quadratic supply objective exactly. Native floating versions can be inaccurate proposals without changing this semantic objective.

This constructs a feasible integer extended vector for every declared physical plan, preserving its projected(e,c). Relaxing y into[0,1] preserves inclusion; imposing a branch equality y_m0 or1 merely selects one of two exhaustive physical domains. At each complete binary fringe, the exact tangent/row-multiplier/finite-box lower therefore applies to every physical schedule assigned to that leaf. No integer-to-physical converse is needed for a lower bound. Under the stated one-connector/common-power assumptions, the formulation note's serial construction also establishes the converse for integer allocations, but the lower certificate does not rely on that stronger claim.

## Derived cuts and fleet-count scope

The semantic derived minimum fleet cut is valid on this set. Half-open mandatory-service overlap cannot exceed the number of used buses. For chronologically disjoint services, a single bus must take them in order; a missing declared consecutive connection makes that impossible. If every consecutive option is direct and service energy alone exceeds B−reserve, one bus cannot finish before any terminal recharge. This reasoning REQUIRES that a direct/pullout movement offers no charging access even if a place name or idle time might otherwise suggest it, and that no unlisted intermediate depot movement can be inserted. The source window functions enforce that policy. The new independent reconstruction derives the cut from the current declared graph rather than a historical native result.

Exactly-two-bus equality is a conditional root restriction, not a generally valid all-fleet cut. The final physical result completes it with the independently established zero/one-bus obstruction and the separately valid >=3 floor. With nonnegative fees/costs/consumption, full replenishment and eta>0, every >=3 physical fleet pays at least3*f+a*mandatory_service_energy/eta; omitting other nonnegative terms is a relaxation. Neither exclusion restricts components of arbitrary complete-plan hull mixtures. The old continuous unbranched energy-flow relaxation and physical branched tree must retain their different CH/D scopes.

## Limits of this review

In particular this source review does not establish that its max_vehicles includes every useful fleet in a less restricted operational interpretation, that every contemplated departure-time/depot-visit choice was generated, or that every omitted waiting interval is physically costless. Those are instance-definition/completeness obligations, not consequences of exact dual algebra. The certified result should explicitly use the pinned fixed-mode opportunity set and the stated fleet cap. Extending to other timing/routing/access choices could lower D and alter the sign; absence of a mode is not automatically dominance.

The original rational witnesses, numerical audit and source-bound provenance remain separately admitted evidence. This review adds the missing logical physical-to-relaxation map for the declared model; it does not rerun them or call internal agreement external replication.

## Narrow source check of canonical movement timing

The necessary generator source is `src/experiments/sistig_native_case.py`, build_depot_variant. Its construction explicitly sets pullout to the latest departure reaching the first service; direct travel begins immediately after the prior service and appends an explicit destination wait; via-depot inbound begins at prior service end and outbound departs at next service start minus the directed outbound travel time; pull-in leaves immediately after the last service. The via-depot pair therefore has the maximal possible charging gap FOR THOSE SAME fixed-duration directed inbound/outbound arcs. Its stored split is1. Source comments and emitted policy fields state this explicitly. Direct waiting at a source-flagged depot is rejected rather than silently assigned off-depot draw. This inspection establishes the generator's policy, not a new audit that every actual archived coefficient was generated as intended.

There is a useful limited timing-dominance argument. Fix a service sequence and the SAME time-independent admitted travel arcs. Any later inbound/earlier outbound via-depot timing has a depot charging interval contained in the canonical maximal gap. Advance the same inbound travel and postpone the same outbound travel; replace any eliminated off-depot waiting by zero-draw depot waiting. Travel energy/duration stays fixed and other consumption weakly decreases. Original charge sessions remain temporally available. Suppress charging only when the replacement would exceed capacity; the replacement's inventory can be maintained no lower at the relevant service boundaries, with no extra charge in any interval. Terminal full inventory allows exactly the remaining deficit to be replenished. Consequently fee is unchanged and hourly purchases need not increase. This proves objective domination for the present nonnegative, coordinatewise increasing supply objective, NOT equality of projected loads. It requires nonnegative waiting consumption, fixed arc energy/duration, zero parked-depot draw, no minimum charging amount and no additional constraints that value off-depot waiting.

For direct travel with no charging access, relocating the fixed total off-depot waiting between the service endpoints does not change total energy when the auxiliary rate is location/time-independent; consumption is monotone and final inventory is the minimum. The declared immediate-travel/destination-wait representative is feasible whenever such a retiming is, with the same purchases/service-boundary inventories. Latest pullout and earliest pull-in similarly maximize depot availability without needing a triangle inequality.

An excursion departing and returning to the SAME depot between two mandatory services, with no mandatory task or distinct charging opportunity on that excursion, is dominated by remaining parked: delete its nonnegative consumption/time, retain every original charging time, and curtail purchases to avoid excess inventory. Several such visits can merge into one gap. This local deletion does NOT use a triangle inequality. It does not justify replacing an arbitrary path from a service to the depot or depot to next service by the matrix's one direct arc. Those endpoint paths might be faster or consume less through an intermediate location in a nonmetric directed matrix; nonnegative arcs alone do not prove their domination. Multiple depots/other charging locations, discretionary relocations that alter future access, time-varying travel, unlisted paths, location-dependent auxiliary rates or free terminal inventory credits remain outside the argument.

Thus the narrow timing/excursion argument supports canonicalization only when the admitted inbound/outbound travel alternatives are held fixed. It does not establish unrestricted routing completeness. Parent should keep the declared-mode formulation in the paper and make any broader dominance extension a separately stated conditional proposition rather than silently substituting it for source completeness.

## Recommended paper wording

> The certified instance uses the pinned descriptor's finite declared movement modes. Each mode fixes its directed travel legs and their departure/arrival times; service assignments choose among all those modes, and charging is continuous only in their declared depot or terminal windows. Via-depot modes use immediate inbound travel and latest feasible outbound departure on their selected directed arcs, giving the maximal gap for those arcs. The result is global within this declared opportunity set and fleet cap. It is not a certificate over arbitrary unlisted routes, additional charging locations or travel-time choices inferred solely from the complete deadhead matrix.

This wording preserves the certified claim and makes its missing generalization explicit. No claim that the omitted arbitrary choices are harmless is needed for the declared-model lower bound.
