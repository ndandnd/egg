\section{How the public lower certificate is checked}\label{app:certificates}
This appendix explains the algebra behind the public certificate. It is a
classical convex lower-bound construction, not a new optimization algorithm.
The native solver proposes numbers; exact arithmetic establishes the bound.

\paragraph{A lower bound that tolerates imperfect multipliers.}
Write a node relaxation as
\[
 \min_x\ q(x)=c_0+c^\top x+\tfrac12x^\top Qx,
 \quad Ax\ge b,\quad Ex=d,\quad \ell\le x\le u,
\]
where $Q\succeq0$ and every variable has finite bounds. Row signs can be
normalized into this form; branch decisions narrow the box or add exact
equalities. Choose any rational tangent point $z$, nonnegative rational
multipliers $\lambda$ and unrestricted rational multipliers $\nu$. Put
\[
 g=c+Qz,\qquad r=g-A^\top\lambda-E^\top\nu.
\]
Convexity and the constraints give the valid node lower bound
\begin{equation}\label{eq:rationalbound}
 B=c_0-\tfrac12 z^\top Qz+b^\top\lambda+d^\top\nu
       +\sum_j\min\{r_j\ell_j,r_j u_j\}.
\end{equation}
Indeed the tangent lower plane is
$c_0-\tfrac12z^\top Qz+g^\top x$. Subtracting
$\lambda^\top(Ax-b)+\nu^\top(Ex-d)$ cannot raise it on the
feasible set, and minimizing the remaining residual over the finite box
gives~\eqref{eq:rationalbound}. Neither $z$ nor the proposed multipliers
need solve the relaxation exactly. A sign-invalid inequality multiplier
cannot be accepted; residual compensation makes approximate stationarity
safe without declaring it to be exact stationarity. For a linear objective,
take $Q=0$. All coefficients and comparisons in the accepted bound are
rational, including the original stored supply coefficients.

\paragraph{An infeasible node also needs evidence.}
For $\lambda\ge0$ and arbitrary $\nu$, every feasible point satisfies
\[
 (A^\top\lambda+E^\top\nu)^\top x
       \ge b^\top\lambda+d^\top\nu.
\]
If the maximum of the left-hand side over $[\ell,u]$ is strictly below
the right-hand side, the node is infeasible. This finite-box contradiction
is checked exactly. A native infeasibility status alone does not justify
deleting a branch. Failure to obtain a valid contradiction leaves its
domain represented by a valid lower bound or an unresolved state.

\paragraph{Coverage is a separate obligation.}
Branching on a binary movement variable $x_j$ produces the two domains
$x_j=0$ and $x_j=1$. Both children are retained, and the checker verifies
their inherited domains. The minimum valid lower over a complete covering
fringe bounds every integer plan in the declared root domain. Search can
stop before finding an optimum if every fringe bound already exceeds the
fixed comparison target. Conversely, complete domain coverage with weak
bounds is not a positive-gap certificate.

The public quadratic tree has the exactly-two-bus root domain. Its separate
one-bus obstruction and nonnegative-cost floor for three or more buses are
therefore essential to the global physical conclusion. Convexified fleets
are not restricted to two buses. A feasible complete-plan mixture supplies
an upper on $\CH$; only a global physical lower strictly above that upper
establishes $D>\CH$. The tree bound is never used as a hull lower.

The later physical refinement preserves the source-admitted 303-node
partition and all 152 frontier identities. Each new local branch begins
with its original leaf's inherited decisions. A retained base bound remains
valid throughout its descendants; new exact certificates can strengthen
that bound but cannot erase a domain. Complete coverage of all original
leaves, with 72 new nodes checked, therefore gives a tighter physical
lower without repeating the old numerical proof audit. Together with the
retained physical witness it yields the outward interval
$[441.532912,441.532913]$. Subtracting the retained up-rounded hull upper
$441.142238$ from the down-rounded physical lower gives the conservative
gap lower $0.390674$, not an exact gap or a new hull lower.

\paragraph{What independent verification means here.}
The checker reconstructs the declared semantic constraints and objective,
checks proposed rational bounds and contradictions, verifies every branch
domain and completes the fleet-count argument. It does not rerun the native
search or assume that a reported solver optimum is exact. Physical upper
witnesses and mixture admission have separate checks and source-bound
provenance. These are internal independent implementations and reviews;
they are not external replication or a claim that the declared physical
model is an empirically calibrated transit system.
