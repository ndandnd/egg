\section{An exact assignment-dependent physical projection}
\label{sec:core}
The central result retains vehicle cost together with grid load. It applies
to an ideal common-window class, rather than arbitrary operational timetables.
There are $n\ge1$ mandatory A services in $[0,1]$ and $n$ mandatory B
services in $[2,3]$, with battery demands $0<a_i,b_j\le B$ and common
capacity $B>0$. A fixed bipartite graph $\Gamma$ lists every allowed
A--B successor pair. Every service also has a standalone option; transitions
have zero time, energy and monetary cost. A partial matching of size $m$
uses $N_m=2n-m$ buses, each at fee $f>0$, under fleet cap $2n$.
Services are indivisible noncharging intervals with nonnegative battery draw
throughout.

All buses start full, finish full and are available throughout the early
window $[1,2]$. One shared connector has grid-side power $K\ge0$ in
that window, in either direction; terminal charging uses one connector of
power $H\ge0$ in $[3,4]$ and permits charging only. Every bus accepts the
corresponding powers.
Charging efficiency is common $\alpha\in(0,1]$ in both windows, and
export efficiency is common $\delta\in(0,1]$. Reserve and other auxiliary
draw are zero. Early actions may change direction finitely many times,
with no switching delay, minimum session, degradation or cycling penalty.
They are sequential, never simultaneous on the connector. These permissive
action assumptions matter for exactness.

Let $C,X$ be integrated early grid purchases and exports. The connector
counts gross use $C+X\le K$, while net early load is $E=C-X$.
Write
\[
 S_0=\sum_i a_i+\sum_j b_j,\quad P=\sum_i a_i,\quad
 Q=\alpha C-X/\delta.
\]
Here $Q$ is net battery exchange, and full replenishment requires terminal
grid purchase $L=(S_0-Q)/\alpha$. Appendix derivations use the local
symbol $S$ for this service-energy total; $S_0$ distinguishes it from the
framework's projected set. Supply and tariff objectives must be defined
on the resulting signed load domain, or a declared background translation.

\begin{theorem}[Complete common-window projection]\label{thm:coreprojection}
Let $\nu$ be the maximum matching size in $\Gamma$, and define
\[
 r_m=\min_{\substack{M\subseteq\Gamma\ {m matching}\\|M|=m}}
       \sum_{(i,j)\in M}(a_i+b_j-B)_+,
 \qquad r_0=0,\qquad m=0,\ldots,\nu.
\]
For each $m$, let
\[
\begin{split}
 W_m=\bigl\{(C,X):\;&C\ge r_m/\alpha,\quad X\ge0,\quad C+X\le K,\\
 &\max\{S_0-N_mB,S_0-\alpha H\}
       \le\alpha C-X/\delta\le P\bigr\}.
\end{split}
\]
The complete physical load--vehicle-cost projection equals
\[
 \mathcal P=\bigcup_{m=0}^{\nu}
 \left\{\left(C-X,\frac{S_0-Q}{\alpha},fN_m\right):
          (C,X)\in W_m\right\}.
\]
Each point has a finite-action physical realization. Empty polygons are
discarded; each remaining polygon has at most five vertices. Their affine
images generate $\operatorname{conv}\mathcal P$ with at most
$5(\nu+1)$ records, including degenerate and lossless images.
\end{theorem}

\paragraph{Why one matching statistic suffices.}
For a fixed matching, each bus's net battery increment has an interval:
paired buses use $[a_i+b_j-B,a_i]$, A-only buses $[a_i-B,a_i]$, and
B-only buses $[b_j-B,0]$. Their lower endpoints sum to $S_0-N_mB$,
upper endpoints to $P$, and positive lower endpoints to the matching's
requirement $R$. At any admissible $Q$, interval allocation realizes
positive increments totalling $A_+=\max\{R,Q\}$. One-direction actions
therefore use $C_0=A_+/\alpha$ and $X_0=\delta(A_+-Q)$.

Any proposed polygon point has residual purchases $z=C-C_0\ge0$ and
residual exports $\alpha\delta z$. One existing bus can realize them
by battery-neutral cycles before its baseline exchange. Since
$N_m\ge n\ge1$ and $B>0$, choose charge-first or export-first according
to its SOC; chunks of battery depth at most $B/2$ fit and return to that
SOC. Finitely many chunks realize the finite residual. Serialize all
actions: total early throughput fits $K$, and terminal refill fits $H$.
Zero power permits only zero energy, without division. Positive B-service
demands give $P<S_0$, so terminal purchases are nonnegative. At fixed
$m$, only $R$ depends on the matching; a matching attaining $r_m$
contains every other matching's entire polygon at the same $C,X$ and fee.
This is physical equality, not just economic dominance. The complete proof
is Theorem~\ref{thm:fulllossyprojection}.

\section{Decisions, boundaries and independently certified existence}
\label{sec:coreconsequences}
The exact set preserves arbitrary objectives on period loads and cost,
including negative posted-price response values. For convex supply $F$,
physical planning minimizes $c+F(E,L)$ over each polygon and takes the
smallest value; hull planning uses ordinary convex combinations of their
vertex records. This uses classical optimization, matching and support
theory, not a new convexification algorithm. Under the framework's
nonemptiness assumption (at least one $W_m$ is nonempty) and
qualification, $D=\CH$ characterizes physical own-load support; a strict
gap excludes it. Replacing complete-plan weights by a fractional fleet
cardinality and interpolated $r_m$ need not give the same hull.

Monotone supply objectives permit a smaller efficient description with
at most two segments per cardinality
(Theorem~\ref{thm:lossyfrontier}). It cannot replace the full polygons
for arbitrary negative-price queries. The charge-only lossless scalar classification
is given in Corollary~\ref{cor:sharedsupport}. Common unequal directional
powers and affine grid-throughput wear also admit exact polygons, but
occupancy becomes $C/K_c+X/K_d\le1$ and wear must enter the joint cost
image (Corollary~\ref{cor:directionalwear}). Its positive-power finite
action bound does not transfer the older efficient formulas or cover
state-dependent degradation and switching time.
Positive per-action setup time admits a conservative physical inner polygon
and a quantitative value/gap bound under explicit connector-time slack
(Proposition~\ref{prop:setupslack}). Without slack, an arbitrarily small
setup can remove a fleet-cost layer while other fleets stay feasible.

A common compulsory post-early transition task preserves the reduction
with $S_0+md$ and a restricted graph $b_j+d\le B$
(Corollary~\ref{cor:commontransitionfull}). Edge-dependent tasks can
break this sufficient statistic. A complete-graph construction admits
every perfect matching yet retains a positive support gap, including
with strongly convex supply and a relative open parameter neighborhood
(Proposition~\ref{prop:stricttransitionrobustness}). That neighborhood
fixes topology, timing, access and ideal physics; it supplies no uniform
radius or empirical robustness claim. The detailed boundary examples,
lossless/V2G classifications and ownership consequences remain in the
appendices.

\paragraph{One public timetable, two explicitly distinct energy laws.}
A separate 37-service case goes beyond the common-window class. Under
each of two separately certified models, the conservative outward bounds are
\[
 \CH\le441.142238<441.532912\le D\le441.532913,
 \qquad D-\CH\ge0.390674.
\]
The physical optimum is tightly enclosed, not known exactly; the hull
optimum is also unknown. A feasible complete-plan mixture supplies its
upper, while an all-physical rational branching certificate supplies the
lower. A mean plan is not an executable daily schedule.

The original binary-consumption result concerns its finite declared
movement graph. A separately defined exactly additive consumption law,
fresh target certificates and a canonical dominance proof extend the
second result to finite walks over specified paired OD arcs and continuous
linear waiting, under explicit triangle and common-access premises. No
certificate or feasibility conclusion transfers automatically between
these laws, even though the displayed decimal bounds coincide. These
are charge-only models with synthetic consumption/economics, $400$\,kWh
batteries, one $360$\,kW connector and used-bus fee $100$. They establish neither
public V2G nor calibrated savings, prevalence or empirical road completeness.
Section~\ref{sec:public} and Proposition~\ref{prop:exactenergydominance}
give the full model, exact certificate scopes and extension conditions.
