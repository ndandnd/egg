\section{A conditional extension to exact additive consumption}
\label{sec:exactenergyextension}
The public result in Section~\ref{sec:public} uses the original stored
binary consumption coefficients. It does not automatically cover a change
to unrounded energy formulas. Here we state a separate, conditional model
extension with a separately checked positive-gap certificate. No physical
bound or feasible point from \eqref{eq:publicbounds} is assumed to survive
the coefficient change. The argument is a classical triangle-inequality and
charging-curtailment argument, not a new generic routing result.

\paragraph{The new law and opportunity set.}
Each ordered pair of a fixed finite location set is an available primitive
arc with jointly specified time $\tau_{ij}$ and distance $d_{ij}$.
These are abstract modeled pairs; their simultaneous realization by an
empirical road path is not inferred. Assume both directed tables are
nonnegative, have zero diagonals and satisfy their directed triangle
inequalities. In hours and kilometres, define the following quantities
by \emph{exact rational arithmetic}:
\[
 s_v=\frac65\,\ell_v+16h_v,\qquad
 q_{ij}=\frac{24}{25}d_{ij}+16\tau_{ij},\qquad
 w_{ij}=q_{ij}-16\tau_{ij}=\frac{24}{25}d_{ij}.
\]
Services have fixed endpoints and times; $s_v$ is their prescribed total.
They are indivisible noncharging intervals with nonnegative draw throughout.
Every bus starts full at the selected depot,
serves at least one mandatory service and returns full by the common
deadline. Retain the same battery/fleet limits and charge-only,
unit-efficiency shared charging resources. There is no taper, switching
time or minimum session. Only the selected depot admits charging and
zero-draw parking; all other stationary waiting consumes exactly16 per
hour. A second source-flagged depot does not acquire free parking or
charging. Post-last-service charging opens at absolute time zero with the
same deadline and access policy for every visit label. All operating
costs other than grid supply are unchanged by the replacements below;
in particular, monetary movement cost is zero.

The broader set $\mathcal W$ permits arbitrary \emph{finite} primitive-arc
walks and continuous waits between the same fixed services, with no extra
task, regeneration, charging location or time-dependent arc attributes.
The canonical set $\mathcal C$ contains each time-feasible direct and
one-via-selected-depot representative: immediate inbound travel, latest
outbound departure, the entire intervening depot window, latest full
pullout and earliest final return. Its coefficients use this same exact
law, so $\mathcal C\subseteq\mathcal W$.

Completeness includes zero-length depot-endpoint arcs and their access
interpretation. A gap starting or ending at the selected depot is a
depot-visit case, not an off-depot stationary wait. A constructor that
rejects a needed endpoint or a wait at another source-flagged depot must
either establish that it never occurs here or supply the appropriate
canonical representative; table triangles alone do not prove inclusion
or mode completeness. These requirements are premises of the following
statement, not consequences of a coefficient-name or mode-count match.

\begin{proposition}[Canonical value preservation under the exact law]
\label{prop:exactenergydominance}
Under these assumptions, every feasible schedule in $\mathcal W$ has a
schedule in $\mathcal C$ with the same fleet and service assignment/order
and componentwise no greater grid purchases in every market interval.
For any coordinatewise nondecreasing supply objective $F$, physical and
complete-plan convexified infimum values therefore satisfy
\[
 D_{\mathcal W}^{\rm rat}=D_{\mathcal C}^{\rm rat},\qquad
 \CH_{\mathcal W}^{\rm rat}=\CH_{\mathcal C}^{\rm rat}.
\]
These are value equalities, not equality of attainable load sets. For
nonnegative posted prices the private-score infimum also agrees, but all
response ties need not be canonical. No negative-price conclusion is made.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
Both $q$ and $w$ satisfy directed triangles, by their nonnegative linear
dependence on the assumed metric tables. Consider a gap of duration $T$
between fixed services that never visits the selected depot. Its total
consumption is $16T+\sum w_{ij}$, including its off-depot waits. Repeated
triangles make this at least $16T+w_{ab}$ for its endpoints $a,b$.
Direct travel and the remaining off-depot wait attain that value and fit
the gap by the time triangle. There is no charging, and consumption is
nonnegative: a no-smaller final SOC suffices for all intermediate lower
inventory bounds, while no charging can violate capacity.

For a gap visiting the selected depot, replace the prefix to its first
arrival by immediate direct travel, and its suffix from its last departure
by latest direct travel. Time triangles enlarge the depot window; $q$
triangles and nonnegative waiting weakly lower prefix and suffix energy.
Delete all intermediate excursions and remain parked. Keep old charging
at its original clocks, curtailing only when the larger SOC would exceed
capacity. Unit efficiency, zero depot draw and the absence of minimum
sessions make this feasible. The new SOC dominates the old at its charging
times because the removed excursions consumed nonnegative energy. The
no-more-costly suffix then preserves feasibility of the next service.
Each purchase and instantaneous rate only decreases, so shared connector
and market-period constraints are preserved.

Latest direct pullout from a full initial battery eliminates unnecessary
pre-service consumption. Earliest final return contains the former
terminal charging clocks; the common opening-zero policy excludes a
visit-label access loophole. Keeping those clocks with capacity curtailment
restores full terminal inventory because the original schedule ended
full. Zero-diagonal arcs and finite repeated cycles cause no exception;
the same argument deletes them without removing an extra task or access
right, which the assumptions explicitly exclude. Apply the replacements
successively along each duty, preserving its next service's SOC. Services
and fleet cost do not change.

Canonical inclusion gives one infimum inequality. The componentwise
dominance and monotonicity of $F$ give its reverse. Apply the replacement
to every component of a finite complete-plan mixture to obtain the hull
inequality: its mean load decreases componentwise and its mean operating
cost is unchanged. This proves the stated equalities. The nonnegative
posted-price assertion uses the same argument with a linear supply term.
\end{proof}

\paragraph{A fresh certificate for the public-derived target.}
The original binary supply objective in \eqref{eq:publicF} may be retained;
it need not be rounded again when consumption changes. A new target
certificate must nevertheless revalidate every lower-bound row and fleet
exclusion and admit each proposed upper component under the exact law.
We performed those checks on the retained 375-node partition, using its
fixed multiplier/tangent proposals and two predetermined route sets.
Each route set received one exact charging reconstruction and one independent
physical replay under the new law. Their fixed historical mixture weight
was retained without optimizing it again. Direct mixed-load evaluation and
an independent quadratic-variance identity give
\begin{equation}\label{eq:exacttargetbounds}
 \CH_{\mathcal C}^{\rm rat}\le441.142238<441.532912
 \le D_{\mathcal C}^{\rm rat}\le441.532913,
 \qquad D_{\mathcal C}^{\rm rat}-\CH_{\mathcal C}^{\rm rat}\ge0.390674.
\end{equation}
The six-decimal outward numbers coincide with \eqref{eq:publicbounds},
but their exact rational certificates differ. Neither exact optimum is known.
The complete canonical time/access qualification and unchanged nonenergy
fields establish the representative-coverage premise; the exact law and
qualified time/distance tables establish the triangle premises.
Proposition~\ref{prop:exactenergydominance} therefore transfers the new
separation to $\mathcal W$. Under the usual convex
differentiable supply and attainment assumptions,
Proposition~\ref{prop:support} then gives the own-marginal-price conclusion.
The finite canonical formulation supplies compactness when its charging
variables have closed bounded constraints; value preservation does not
require the unrestricted finite-walk set itself to be compact.
The first target computation retained valid bounds and physical points but
failed in a diagnostic-object identity guard before evaluating the mixture.
A separately frozen summary-only consumer completed that comparison; it
repeated no branch proof, charging reconstruction or physical replay. The
failed attempt and its costs remain recorded. This is internal independent
verification, not external replication. The existing binary-model conclusion
remains exactly as scoped in Section~\ref{sec:public}.
Neither result establishes empirical road completeness,
energy calibration or operational prevalence.
