\subsection{Classical exact formulations for charge-only setup}
\label{app:setupchargeonly}
Loss of the minimum-$r_m$ summary does not require enumerating all matchings
or using a learned method. The common-window charge-only model admits
ordinary resource allocation and a compact duty formulation.
These are classical baselines, not new optimization algorithms.

Assume common capacity $B$, zero other draw/reserve, full initial and
terminal inventories, common efficiency $\alpha\in(0,1]$ in both windows,
early accepted grid power $K_c>0$, terminal accepted power/budget $H\ge0$,
and common setup $\tau\ge0$ assessed separately for each bus's nonzero
early action. Each used bus pays $f>0$. Both windows
have unit duration and all used buses are available throughout them.
Both windows permit charging only, and allowed transitions have zero
time, energy and cost. Terminal actions have no setup. No minimum session, taper or intrawindow
access change is allowed. Early sessions on one bus can then be merged
without changing endpoint SOC or increasing transfer time or setup count.

\subsubsection{An exact interval for a fixed matching}
Fix the duties. A bus consuming $A_v$ before the early window and $B_v$
afterward has the true grid-energy box
\[
 r_v=\frac{(A_v+B_v-B)_+}{\alpha},\qquad
 u_v=\frac{A_v}{\alpha},\qquad r_v\le q_v\le u_v.
\]
Absent services have energy zero; require $A_v,B_v\in[0,B]$.
Let $R=\sum_vr_v$, let $h$ count the mandatory recipients $r_v>0$,
and put $U_h=\sum_{r_v>0}u_v$. Sort the positive optional capacities
as $v_1\ge\cdots\ge v_p>0$. Zero-capacity buses remain in the fleet
and fee count, but need not enter this scan. If
$R>K_c(1-\tau h)$, early charging is infeasible. Otherwise define
\[
 C_{\max}=\max_{h\le k\le h+p}
 \min\left\{U_h+\sum_{j=1}^{k-h}v_j,\ K_c(1-\tau k)\right\}.
\]
The attainable early totals are exactly $[R,C_{\max}]$. A $k$-recipient
schedule cannot exceed either the largest available capacity or its
remaining transfer time. Conversely those top optional capacities and
the mandatory intervals attain every total above $R$ up to their sum,
with arbitrarily small positive optional allocations when needed. If
the maximum equals $R$, use only mandatory recipients. Decreasing charges
toward their lower bounds never increases active count, proving there
are no holes. Negative later scan terms cannot improve the feasible
mandatory baseline. In particular $h=0$ admits zero actions for every
$\tau$, even when all optional capacities vanish.

Write $G=\sum_v(A_v+B_v)/\alpha$ for total grid replacement energy,
not the planner value $D$. Terminal refill gives the exact final interval
\[
 \max\{R,G-H,0\}\le C\le\min\{C_{\max},G\},
 \qquad E=C,\quad L=G-C,\quad c=fN,
\]
when its endpoints are ordered. Valid individual boxes, not merely
aggregate clipping, ensure physical inventories. For $K_c=0$ separately,
only $C=0$ is possible, requiring all $r_v=0$ and $G\le H$.
The scan uses mandatory count and ordered capacities as well as $R$;
it is not an exact joint-matching reduction.

\subsubsection{A compact duty MILP}
Use each allowed pair $(i,j)$ and each standalone $(i,*)$, $(*,j)$
as a candidate duty $d$, at most $n^2+2n$ in total. Let $A_d,B_d$ be
its energies before/after the early window, with zero for an absent
service, and $s_d=A_d+B_d$. Variables are duty activation $y_d\in\{0,1\}$,
setup permission $z_d\in\{0,1\}$, early purchase $q_d\ge0$ and terminal
purchase $t_d\ge0$. Impose exact service cover
\[
 y_{i,*}+\sum_{j:(i,j)\in\Gamma}y_{ij}=1,\qquad
 y_{*,j}+\sum_{i:(i,j)\in\Gamma}y_{ij}=1,
\]
and, for every duty,
\begin{align*}
 z_d&\le y_d,&\alpha q_d&\le A_dz_d,&
 \alpha q_d&\ge(s_d-B)y_d,\\
 \alpha(q_d+t_d)&=s_dy_d,&0\le\alpha t_d&\le By_d.
\end{align*}
The shared constraints are
\[
 \sum_d q_d/K_c+\tau\sum_dz_d\le1,\qquad
 \sum_dt_d\le H.
\]
An additional fleet limit may bound $\sum_dy_d$; cover alone gives
$n\le\sum_dy_d\le2n$. Define $E=\sum q_d$, $L=\sum t_d$ and
$c=f\sum y_d$, optionally adding common affine energy wear.

Every physical bus induces one duty. Merging its charge-only early
sessions yields these inequalities and sets $z_d=1$ for positive $q_d$.
Conversely, serialize each positive $q_d$ with one setup at power $K_c$.
The capacity and reserve inequalities keep SOC between zero and $B$
before and after B; nonnegative service consumption preserves prefixes.
Serialize terminal charges using the shared budget; the equality restores
every used battery exactly full. A permitted $z_d=1,q_d=0$ only
overcounts setup time: omit that action, preserving projected feasibility.
Thus the formulation is exact for aggregate energy/cost decisions, not
for rewarding the literal count of nonzero sessions.

Any affine aggregate query is an MILP; convex quadratic grid cost gives
a convex MIQP. Polynomial formulation size does not mean polynomial
solution time, nor is its continuous relaxation automatically the
complete-plan hull. Exact sets here exclude counted idle buses; with
positive fee they cannot improve $c+F(E,L)$ in this charge-only,
full-start/end model, because they exchange no energy and only add cost.
The result does not extend the session-merging argument to V2G or
state/session-dependent degradation. It supplies no hardness, numerical
advantage, public-data or ML claim.
