\section*{How to read this record}
This supplement preserves the computational chapter of draft 0.6 as a
historical development record. Its tables and figures have not been rerun or
reclassified. The main paper now reports joint physical, hull and regret
quantities and stronger analytical lower bounds. The observations below
do not establish systematic acceleration or generalization.

\begin{itemize}
\item On the three-service multivisit case, source runs exhausted four pricing
calls after about 3.5 seconds of a 60-second wall allowance. In the changed
market, cold methods reached the projected 4096-bit rational-polishing limit;
the reuse method certified after one pricing call. The reported difference
is conditional on these limits. Certification or runtime under a different
cold budget has not been measured.
\item Three failed cells in the ordered comparison exposed the handling of a
pricing call without an incumbent. That code path was subsequently repaired
and tested; the historical failures and spent time remain. A new comparison
would be a separate experiment, not a replacement of these observations.
\item The fixed-price start pilot did not establish native acceptance of
submitted starts and used one seed. Its public objective differences cannot
identify a start effect or a speedup.
\item The feasible-pool pilot and ordered comparison are different campaigns.
For example, depot 16's reused target lower bounds of 329.22 and 350.87 refer
to those distinct campaigns. The tables cannot be pooled as independent trials.
\item These computed hull lower bounds omit the stronger energy-floor baseline
now given in the main paper. Their historical plots are not comparisons
against the best currently available lower bound. Capped runtime panels
cannot establish time to equal quality.
\end{itemize}

The word ``established'' in the historical evidence map refers only to the
specified run outcomes, with the qualifications above. It does not designate
a validated computational method. No new optimization was performed to
assemble this supplement.

The historical tables and figures use published scalar summaries from the
feasible-pool pilot, ordered solver-baseline comparison and fixed-price
pricing-start pilot in the \href{https://github.com/ndandnd/egg}{EGG research

script. Native bounds remain numerical and tolerance-qualified. The v0.7

these development comparisons were removed from the main paper.
