# Focused literature update for manuscript scope

Checked 27 September 2026 against primary author/publisher sources. This is a targeted update, not a systematic or exhaustive search. Search-engine dates were not treated as publication dates: the Lacombe thesis found during discovery is from 2023, despite a recent index date. No new novelty priority claim follows from this search.

| Work and primary source | Verified scope | Consequence for EGG (our interpretation) |
|---|---|---|
| Lacombe, R., Murgovski, N., Gros, S., and Kulcsár, B. (2024). *Integrated charging scheduling and operational control for an electric bus network*. Transportation Research Part E 186, 103549. [University publication record](https://research.chalmers.se/en/publication/541552), [author manuscript](https://research.chalmers.se/publication/541552/file/541552_Fulltext.pdf), DOI 10.1016/j.tre.2024.103549. | Integrates operational control and charging through an MILP, using Lagrangian decomposition and a feasible-solution heuristic; evaluates a Chicago bus-network case. | Cite as a direct bus-specific decomposition antecedent. EGG should identify the complete-fleet convexification and price-support diagnostic as its particular question, rather than present decomposition or dual lower bounds as new. |
| Ricard, L., Desaulniers, G., Lodi, A., and Rousseau, L.-M. (2026). *Chance-constrained battery management for electric bus scheduling*. European Journal of Operational Research, online 1 June 2026, DOI 10.1016/j.ejor.2026.05.046. [Publisher record](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ejor.2026.05.046); [2025 author preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.19853). | Chance constraints address stochastic energy and battery operating policies; the method uses branch-and-price with stochastic pricing. | Deterministic reserve/loss sensitivity is a structural robustness result, not a substitute for probabilistic reliability or battery-policy validation. Add this boundary to limitations. |
| Manzolli, J. A., Eslami, A., Miranda-Moreno, L., and Yu, J. (2026). *When Agents Meet Electric Bus Fleet Operations: Pricing Behavior, Trade-offs, and Policy Implications in an Aggregator Framework*. [arXiv 2606.26400v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.26400v1), submitted 24 June 2026; [full text](https://arxiv.org/html/2606.26400v1). **Preprint.** | Supervisory agents guide tariffs, disturbance-triggered reoptimization and schedule acceptance; an optimization core enforces fleet feasibility. The study compares coordination objectives and value allocation. | Acknowledge contemporary fleet pricing/aggregation work. EGG analyzes marginal-price support of a common physical optimum and makes no claim to test an agentic tariff-setting system or prove general aggregator performance. |

Access: Chalmers metadata and manuscript were accessible. Ricard's publisher abstract/content appeared in indexed primary results, but direct DOI/ScienceDirect fetch failed; the author preprint abstract was opened. Do not claim full journal-text verification or quote experimental percentages from it. Manzolli's versioned abstract and HTML sections 3.2/5.5/Appendix B were inspected; mark preprint status. Author lists use explicit bibliographic headers, not generated 'and two others' snippets.

Recommended manuscript addition is two or three short related-work sentences and one limitations sentence, with bibliographic entries. Do not expand the experiment campaign or claim priority from the absence of search matches.
