# Independent review: solver-baseline comparison, attempt 1

**Verdict: supported within the stated development scope.** The derived tables, figures, README and results-note headlines match the sealed cell evidence and the independent replay recorded in `replay.json`. Root completed the transport, source-pin and sealed-manifest checks separately; this review did not repeat that archive audit. No solver was run.

The summary accounts for all 32 declared cells: 11 `certified`, 18 `budget_exhausted` and 3 `failed`. All 29 available raw results replayed through `base.assess` and matched their saved assessments and summary bounds. The 12 retained state-1 admissions replayed against their own arm’s state-0 evidence; all were eligible, matched the saved source identities and file pins, and matched the worker’s source-check receipt. All four cache-enabled target cells reproduced their full target-market cache candidates from the admitted state-0 physical pricing lowers. The replay also confirmed empty state-0 pools, and independently recomputed all 16 paid-pair totals as state-0 child time + state-1 child time + one parent admission check for retained arms. The saved state-1 child source check was not added a second time. No replay mismatch was found.

The three failures are public depot 15 state 1 `reserve_cold_hull` and public depot 15/16 state 1 `qp_feasible_hull`. Each final pricing call recorded native `NO_SOLUTION_FOUND` with no incumbent and no plan. The compact wrapper then raised `Compact oracle extraction policy mismatch` while checking extraction policy on that absent plan. Earlier bounded pricing evidence remains in each event stream, but the failing call emitted no `pricing_result` or new global bound, so the cells correctly have no final interval. Each receipt has return code 2, `hard_timeout=false`, and `on_time=false`; elapsed child time of 171.90–173.00 seconds remains in paid totals. These are child failures caused by the no-plan return path, not invalid completed physical witnesses or QP proposal failures. The 28 recorded QP proposals had zero non-success results.

The public QP + cache lower-bound decomposition is accurate and explicitly within-run. At depot 15, the selected inherited physical-pricing lower re-evaluated for state 1 is 405.8331, versus a best fresh target lower of 307.0089, a difference of 98.8243; the outward-rounded interval is [405.83, 471.52]. At depot 16 the corresponding values are 420.4551 and 350.6249, a difference of 69.8302; the interval is [420.45, 491.46]. Each target recorded two cached candidates and one fresh successful pricing result. Both intervals remain open: their unrounded gaps are about 65.68 and 71.00 objective units, and both outcomes are `budget_exhausted`. The paid totals, including each arm’s own state 0 and one parent admission, are 352.27 and 352.22 seconds. The QP-without-cache target failed at both public depots, so the inherited-bound contribution does not isolate a causal between-arm cache effect or establish equal-quality speedup.

The review preserves the negative outcomes and qualifications: reserve + pool hit projected rational bit-size limits at both public state-1 targets, while QP + cache recorded at most 275 bits against the configured 4096-bit cap; restricted-pool gaps are not global gaps; the global enclosures use solver-reported lower bounds with Gurobi tolerances; and a feasible convex mixture is not a single executable fleet schedule. The two public depot variants share one Hildenbrand timetable. This is one fixed-order development comparison, not a factorial interaction study, independent replication, ML evaluation, scalability result, public optimality result or general speedup claim. The synthetic multivisit cold arm remains budget-exhausted at both states; retained-pool arms certify the changed market. The cyclic synthetic case certifies under all four arms at both states.

The charts communicate the accounting and failures correctly. The paid-time figure distinguishes state 0, state 1 and parent admission and marks failed targets with a red × while retaining their spent time. The public state-1 bounds figure labels the three failures without inventing intervals. The README says the displayed endpoints are outward-rounded; both the README and results update distinguish inherited from fresh target lower bounds and carry the solver-tolerance caveat.
