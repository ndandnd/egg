# Scope decision: analytical baseline in reporting

This package turns the v0.7 analytical calculation into a reusable opt-in
reporting baseline for the two reviewed public physical cases. It is not a
solver improvement or a new experiment. Its acceptance criterion is correct
scope and evidence separation, not narrower bounds on newly selected cases.

The exact energy/cardinality certificate belongs to the ideal model with full
terminal replenishment. It must not silently become a global lower bound in the
native coordinator, cached physical-pricing evidence, a linear-pricing objective
cut or a successful native status. The first integration therefore sits in the
read-only computational screen reporter. Its default output and original stage
rows remain unchanged. The optional appendix can state the ideal floor and a
conditional combination with compatible native upper witnesses, separately
labelled. Native lower bounds stay in their original numerical scope: the
ideal-model enclosure uses the exact analytical floor and does not import a
stronger native lower without an additional proof of model equivalence.
A missing/failed native result stays missing/failed, even when an independent
ideal lower bound is available. An incompatible upper or certificate is an
explicit inconsistency/unavailable result, never a clamped interval.

The initial certificate catalog is restricted to the exact reviewed depot15
and depot16 physical payloads. Identity, proof lineage and assumptions are
checked before price-only recomputation. The one-bus obstruction uses the
reviewed chronological proof; service energy above one battery alone is not
sufficient because a vehicle might recharge between services. All cardinalities
are covered by the two-bus flow floor and three-plus-bus comparison. Unsupported
price/curvature regimes remain unsupported instead of being extrapolated.

Pure tests and a read-only replay of curated frozen/scalar evidence are enough
for this reporting change. No native solve, cluster query or allocation,
protected/private data, budget change, retrieval submission, manuscript/PDF
revision or learned-model training is part of this package. The pending
replacement-run decision remains separate. The next prospective experimental
step is a bounded sizing diagnostic, after this baseline integration is checked.
