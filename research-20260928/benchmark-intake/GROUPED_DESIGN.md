# Prospective public-base grouping

This is a metadata-only reservation made before comparative outcomes. It is a
grouping plan, not a claim that every base is qualified or will be solved. The
reproducible assignment and member hashes are in
[`GROUPED_RESERVATION.json`](GROUPED_RESERVATION.json), generated by
[`group_public_bases.py`](group_public_bases.py) from the fixed inventory and
the two selected member files per operator.

The 20 public timetable folders are grouped by exact byte identity of
`trip_set.xlsx` and `deadhead_trip_matrix.mat`. All depot, day, vehicle, tariff,
timing, and other scenario variants inherit their base timetable's split.
Hildenbrand and Stadtwerke Eberbach are fixed development groups; `cyclic2`,
`multivisit3`, and every prior screen case and its variants are development
only. The remaining public groups are ranked by ascending SHA-256 of
`egg-base-network-reservation-2026-09-28-v1|size_stratum|base_fingerprint`,
with the fingerprint as a tie-breaker. Strata use inventory service-row counts:
1–200, 201–999, and 1,000+.

The resulting reservation is 10 train, 4 development, and 6 test groups. The
candidate test groups are Bad Nauheim (137 services); Nahverkehrsgesellschaft
Jerichower Land (616) and Weimar (839); and ASEAG (5,147), NIAG (3,909), and
Kölner Verkehrsbetriebe (6,281). Hildenbrand (37), Stadtwerke Eberbach (105),
DVG Duisburg (2,141), and Stadtwerke Passau (984) are development. The
remaining ten groups are train. These counts provide small, medium, and large
reserved groups; they do not make every large network a required first-draft
run. Initial comparisons can use a few preselected development cells.

The two-file fingerprint detects exact byte duplicates only. It does not
establish semantic independence: if later inspection finds a candidate test
base aliases a studied train/development/prior-screen base, quarantine the
merged base from clean test membership. Never backfill an exposed alias with a
new test group after outcomes. Keep test groups unopened for adaptation,
training, and comparative outcomes until a separately frozen evaluation is
authorized. Public member bytes are hashed but not parsed by the grouping
script; source qualification remains a separate step.
