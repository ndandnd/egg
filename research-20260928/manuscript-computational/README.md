# Computational manuscript chapter

The six-page LaTeX chapter consolidates the three completed, reviewed development
campaigns. It includes four tables and two vector figures: public target hull
intervals beside paid source-plus-target work, and fixed-price starting-fleet
effects on incumbent quality and admitted lower bounds. It is a component of
the expanded manuscript, not the completed first draft or a submission.

The results support useful reuse of feasible fleets and inherited pricing
bounds in particular cells. They do not establish public global optimality,
equal-quality acceleration, learned-proposal benefit or independent-network
generalization. Both public depots remain one Hildenbrand timetable. Campaigns
reuse cases and sometimes source evidence; their cells are not pooled as
independent repetitions. Failed target intervals remain missing and their paid
time remains included. The unexecuted six-case retrieval study contributes no
algorithm result and is not a headline manuscript figure.
