# Literature source map for the readable manuscript

Checked 28 September 2026 for this bounded citation pass. The links below go
to publisher records, author-hosted manuscripts, or the official Figshare data
record. The map states what the source supports and what the manuscript does
not infer from it. It is not a systematic review or a novelty census.
