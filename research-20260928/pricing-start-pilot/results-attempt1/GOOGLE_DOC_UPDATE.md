## Feasible starts help selectively, without a measured speedup — 28 September 2026

The 16-call fixed-price pilot is complete. Job 577225 used one CPU for 22 minutes 14 seconds. All calls returned: the eight small synthetic calls were certified numerically, and the eight public calls retained open optimality intervals. Every public call used its 160-second native solve allowance. The public depot variants belong to one timetable, and the results use one solver seed.

The comparison gives mixed results. At depot 15's linear prices, a starting hint gave the same feasible cost but a weaker lower bound. At its marginal-price query, it improved the feasible cost by 1.10 while weakening the lower bound. Depot 16's linear query was unchanged; at its marginal-price query the same feasible cost was accompanied by a lower bound stronger by 11.78. A submitted hint is not evidence of solver acceptance. The results compare final quality at matched budgets and do not establish faster convergence or a complete iterative-method speedup.

Both methods share the same known feasible source-fleet baseline. Those source fleets cost 0.32–2.74% more than the best newly returned public incumbents; this is not a guarantee relative to the unknown optimum. Its original generation cost is retained, as are input preparation, complete child time and job overhead. The inexpensive act of proposing a fleet and the expensive act of establishing its global quality must remain separate in later retrieval or learning experiments. A learned route proposal would not itself certify optimality or the paper's price-support gap.

The next bounded package is independent timetable intake and a small synthetic scaling design, with base-network groups reserved for testing before training. Then compare cold solving, retained columns and nearest-neighbor proposals, measuring feasibility, repair, solution quality and verification separately. The current cases remain development data. No expanded solver-start sweep or learned model is launched on the strength of this mixed pilot.

Independent non-optimizing review reproduced the physical plans, numerical bounds and complete call accounting.
