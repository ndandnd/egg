# Targeted literature correction

Verified 29 September 2026 against primary sources, without asserting a priority claim.

- Zoltowska and Lin (2021), [publisher paper](https://doi.org/10.3390/en14164727), Sections1.3/2: bus-level timing and energy inputs; mixed-integer aggregation/disaggregation surrounding a linear auction. Cite as operational context, not as an author claiming joint service assignments are convex.
- Bichler, Knörr and Maldonado (2023), [author repository abstract and metadata](https://repository.essex.ac.uk/33062/), [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1287/isre.2022.1139): flexible demand and nonconvex electricity pricing. Journal volume34(2),652–675; online2022, issue2023.
- Löbel, Borndörfer and Weider (2024), [author preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.14446): nonlinear charging approximations, variable recharge rates and grid limits. Cited as a preprint; no invented journal status.

The defensible motivation is extending the support question from a specified charging opportunity set to alternative complete service assignments. Existing convex charging work is not thereby refuted. Full replenishment removes a terminal-inventory artifact; indivisibility remains the mechanism.
