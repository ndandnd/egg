## Registry and physical assumptions

The versioned `physical-fleet-learning-v2-full-factorial` registry assigns 128 independent training base IDs 10000–10127, 32 development IDs 20000–20031, and 32 reserved test IDs 30000–30031. All markets and solver variants of one physical timetable share one base group and split. The historical reserved IDs 2004, 2005, 2020 and 2021 are untouched. The first selected shard is training IDs 10000–10007; this job does not construct any development or test timetable. A shard index 0–15 selects a distinct eight-group immutable attempt path for future separately recorded prospective submissions. The wrapper defaults to index 0; no job array or concurrent dispatch is configured.

One physical battery profile is assigned per base timetable; crossing all three battery variants within eight bases would triple the first shard's cells, while still representing eight independent base timetables. Profiles distinguish nameplate/upper/reserve/operating span: 100/100/20/80 kWh control; 290.4/232.32/58.08/174.24 kWh modeled 20–80% SOC; and 290.4/261.36/29.04/232.32 kWh modeled 10–90% SOC. These SOC windows are *modeling assumptions*, not manufacturer recommendations. The generator's multiplicative index permutes a 54-combination factorial of three battery profiles × two sizes (20/28 services) × three drive-consumption assumptions (1.20/1.45/1.70 kWh/km) × three minimum depot-dwell thresholds (0/15/30 minutes). Every 54 successive groups covers the complete factorial, so battery profile is not locked to size, energy rate, or depot opportunity. The first eight happen to contain profile counts 3/3/2, sizes 5×20 and 3×28, consumption counts 2/3/3 and depot-threshold counts 3/3/2. Duty start spacing also varies deterministically (25/30 minutes).

Revenue-service energy is elapsed drive minutes × 36 km/h ÷ 60 × the assigned kWh/km. Deadhead trips use the same speed and rate with declared A/B/depot drive minutes. Direct waiting consumes 1.2 kW of auxiliaries; depot dwell is modeled engines-off with zero movement draw. These are synthetic assumptions, not inferred vehicle specifications. One shared 90-kW grid/90-kW per-bus resource, charging efficiency 0.9, terminal access from minute 1080 to 1800, and full terminal refill remain explicit. The generator declares feasible direct/depot arcs rather than fabricating a route after seeing solver outcomes. A constructive paired-duty fleet is checked through independent native replay for **all 128 training IDs once locally** before the first cluster launch; all passed. The first eight witness hashes and full generated cases are frozen in the shard manifest. This proves each input has at least one feasible physical fleet under its assumptions, not that a solver will find or certify the best fleet.

## First shard cells and labels

Each of eight groups has two independent native source-market solves, then six fixed-source target charging solves: the best replayed source0 and source1 fleets each recharged under three tariffs. Source0 is flat 0.20; source1 has 0.10 in periods 18–21 and 0.30 otherwise. Target `late` has 0.10 in 22–25, `day` has 0.10 in 10–13, and `flat` is 41/150 throughout. All markets have quadratic coefficient 1/900. Total: **16 native source + 48 fixed-source charging = 64 serial cells**. All source cells precede target charging labels in the shard. There is no cold target run in every training group and no learner fit in this job.

The native source cell retains solver status, elapsed time, all bounded raw result/event files, separately replayed global lower certificate if available, and the least-cost replayed physical source plan and its full movement/vehicle/charging data. A source incumbent from a failed or time-limited child remains feasible but provisional; it is not an optimality certificate. The fixed-source cell pins the source plan hash and entire movement set, solves only charging under the **linear** target tariff (45-second native phase, 55-second wall cap, one thread), and independently replays its returned plan. The saved label is the exact *curved* target cost of that returned plan. It is not the curved minimum for that topology or a globally optimal fleet cost; different linear optima may have different curved bills. Every failure, timeout, lack of replayed incumbent, native status, bound, plan hash, physical plan, and paid time is catalogued. Missing source fleets produce typed failed charging cells rather than a hidden retry or cold substitute.

`manifest.json` is immutable and stores split registry ranges, only selected train case/market identities and full serialized graphs, constructive witness hashes, assumptions, cell order, and budgets. `frozen.json` pins the manifest hash, execution commit, source hashes, and runtime/backend identity. The append-only JSONL catalog gives each row `base_group`, physical profile, case/market identity, source or target role, replayed plan, feasibility/status, bounds if verified, failure, and child receipt. One exclusive controller lock and per-cell launch/receipt allow resume only past fully receipted cells. An interrupted unreceipted cell is preserved without automatic retry. The attempt path for shard 0 is `result/physical_learning/20260930-shard00-attempt1`.
