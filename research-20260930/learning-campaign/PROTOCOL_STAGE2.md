# Stage-2 synthetic learning campaign

Stage 2 is a new, independently frozen development attempt at
`result/learning_campaign/20260930-stage2-attempt1`. The successful
`20260930-attempt1` pilot and its frozen commit/results remain unchanged.
The pilot yielded 17 replayed feasible cells, a successful prospective
training receipt, and no observed speed advantage for the learned arm on
the eight-service development case. Stage 2 tests larger synthetic cases
and provides more independent training groups. It makes no speedup claim
in advance.

The fixed timetable profile is:

| Seeds | Split | Services | Groups | Native cells |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 2010, 2011 | train | 12 | 2 | 10 |
| 2012, 2013 | train | 20 | 2 | 10 |
| 2014, 2015 | train | 28 | 2 | 10 |
| 2016 | development | 20 | 1 | 7 |
| 2017 | development | 28 | 1 | 7 |

Total: 44 native cells. Seeds 2004 and 2005 remain reserved test groups;
this attempt does not materialize their physical cases, tariffs, labels,
or features. All tariff variants of one physical timetable stay in the
same split. The generator is deterministic, seed-specific, and independent
of the earlier eight-service generator. The frozen design records case and
market identities and a pure replayed paired-duty feasibility witness hash
for each active group. No A6, B3, protected holdout, or private data enters
this campaign.

The controller runs every training group first: source0, source1, cold,
retained, nearest-price. It then runs **both** development groups' source0
and source1 cells. Only after all four development source receipts are in
the append-only catalog does it call the CPU trainer once, with a 45-second
hard cap. The trainer reads training labels and both development source
pools, derives both development target market inputs from the frozen design,
and writes immutable model, proposal, report, and training receipts before
either development target cold solve starts. The controller records its own
training receipt, including catalog input hash, source hashes, runtime,
elapsed time, return status, and output hashes. Training failure is kept
and does not suppress native target baseline cells.

Each development group then runs cold, retained, nearest-price,
cheapest-bill, and learned target arms. All use the same physical case,
target market, compact native pricing oracle, QP master, thread count,
and native budget. Cold has no imported fleet. Retained imports all unique
replayed source columns. Learned, nearest-price, and cheapest-bill use
the same one-best-fleet-per-source catalog pool. The learned topology
prediction is projected onto a replayed complete source fleet and that
specific fleet is imported as a feasible-pool proposal. Nearest-price
selects by exact squared source-market tariff distance; cheapest-bill
selects by exact full nonlinear target objective. Any native target
certificate is generated by the target solver; source bounds are never
carried across markets. The proposal is not described as an MIP start.

Every native child has a 70-second solver budget and 100-second hard child
cap. The controller checks a 5,400-second elapsed guard before launching
each child; the shell has a 5,550-second external cap. The Slurm request is
one CPU, 8 GB, one native solver thread, 100 minutes, no requeue, and
exclusion of `scaglione-compute-01`. Wrapper receipts record both the
requested CPU and actual allocation, since Slurm may allocate more logical
CPUs than requested. Source acquisition, model training, online inference,
proposal checking, adapter preparation, native target time, failures,
and timeouts remain visible separately. The worst-case 44 child caps plus
the trainer cap total 4,445 seconds, below the controller guard.

The attempt pins a clean execution commit, source hashes, portable runtime,
native backend probe, split/profile, physical and market identities,
all cell order and budgets, and the learner source files. Each completed
child has immutable launch/receipt files and one append-only catalog row.
Receipt-preserving resume skips completed cells; an interrupted unreceipted
cell is preserved for postmortem and stops automatic resume. A replayed
single-fleet incumbent may remain feasible after a failed or bounded solve,
but its `optimality` stays `unknown`; no incomplete result becomes a
certified label. Scientific admission remains pending independent review.
