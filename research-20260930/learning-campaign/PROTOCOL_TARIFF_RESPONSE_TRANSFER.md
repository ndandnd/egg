# Frozen-model tariff-response transfer, 30 September 2026

This single development attempt tests whether the **already fitted** 2022–2027 charge-response ridge chooser adapts source-fleet selection to target tariffs. The preceding four-group validation favored source1 in three of four cases, but the ridge chose source1 in all four; an always-source1 rule had the same choices. This batch therefore measures price-sensitive choice against that fixed rule, not merely average cost against cheapest-direct. No model is refit, no decoder is changed, and no held-out test case is accessed.

## Independent groups and tariffs

Four new timetable groups use stage-2 deterministic seeds 2032 and 2034 at 20 services, and 2033 and 2035 at 28 services. Each physical timetable has the same two source markets and three target tariff variants. The variants are prospectively fixed: `late` is 0.10 during periods 22–25 and 0.30 otherwise; `day` is 0.10 during periods 10–13 and 0.30 otherwise; `flat` is 41/150 at every period, the same nominal 30-period mean price. Every variant has quadratic coefficient 1/900. The day window intersects the generator's depot-visit period; terminal-only refill opens at minute 1080 (period 18). Flat removes the timing discount. These tariffs stress a ridge model trained under the original late target only, and its response outside that training tariff is exploratory. Do not call the 12 market variants 12 independent timetables: uncertainty and conclusions are grouped by the four base timetables. Seeds 2004, 2005, 2020, and 2021 remain unmaterialized reserves.

The exclusive attempt is `result/learning_campaign/20260930-tariff-response-attempt1`. The freeze pins the execution commit, all source hashes, old `EdgePrior`, charge-response model and training data/receipts, runtime/backend, four physical cases, twelve target markets, the archived six-group training-majority choice, cell order, and budgets. The model is loaded from `20260930-charge-response-attempt1/learned/model.json`; only independently replayed **training** charge labels from seeds 2022–2027 determine `train_majority`. Prior development outcomes never enter it.

## Ordering and methods

Eight native source acquisitions (source0/source1 for each timetable) run first. Before any target cell, the controller freezes **all twelve** tariff-specific pre-target choices and their input hashes. A separate ≤60-second inference child evaluates the unchanged response ridge. If it fails or is interrupted, the attempt records that failure once and continues independent LP and cold controls without retry after target outcomes. Prospectively frozen simple controls remain available. Every target charging LP must use exactly the selected archived source fleet's movements; it changes charging, not routes.

For each timetable/tariff, compare seven pre-target choices: always-source0, always-source1, majority among the six archived training group winners, cheapest exact curved **direct** bill, nearest source tariff, the old `EdgePrior` topology projection (minimum symmetric-difference, then score, tariff distance, row ID), and the frozen charge-response ridge predicted post-LP cost. A missing chosen source remains unavailable rather than silently falling back. Run both source-specific target charging LPs for paid paired evaluation (24 LP cells total), plus one cold target native hull solve per tariff (12 cells). Thus 8 source + 24 LP + 12 cold = **44 serial cells**. One-LP methods pay only for their chosen LP; best-of-two is a **two-paid-LP hindsight benchmark**, never an online free oracle.

Each source and cold native cell uses the prior stage-2 hull budget. Each fixed-route charging cell uses GRB, one thread, 45-second phase/55-second wall cap, and optimizes only linear target `market.a`. The independent replay gate verifies source topology and physical charging. Labels are exact *curved* target costs of the plans returned by that pinned linear-tariff procedure, not minimum curved costs for a route or globally optimal fleet costs. Degenerate linear optima can have different curved bills. Keep native lower-bound evidence distinct from provisional feasible costs; preserve failure, timeout, native status, partial replayed plans, source/LP paid time, and no-solve outcomes.

## Falsifiable endpoint and timing

For each of the four independent groups, report the three tariff-specific choices, whether a method **switches source across tariffs**, and each method's paired excess exact curved cost over the best of the two replayed paid LP plans when both exist. Compare those group-level patterns with always-source1, train-majority, cheapest-direct, nearest-price, and old-prior controls. A ridge that keeps selecting source1 or fails to reduce grouped excess cost relative to always-source1 has no evidence of adaptive price selection, even if some chosen plans beat cold incumbents. Report source acquisition once per group; frozen training time as historical offline cost; this run's baseline preparation and model inference time separately; one chosen LP versus both paid LP times; and cold cost/bounds/time. Do not count dev variants as independent replicates or claim global optimality/speedup from nonmatched methods.

The one serial Slurm job requests one CPU, 8 GB, one native/BLAS thread, 100 minutes, no requeue, and excludes `scaglione-compute-01`. There are no automatic retries. Forty-four cells each have a 100-second child cap, plus one 60-second inference cap: 4,460 seconds maximum. Controller cap is 5,400 seconds; outer shell cap is 5,700 seconds. Immutable receipts and an exclusive controller lock make the attempt resumable only at fully receipted cells. Local prelaunch tests are pure/mocked and do not run GRB or a full-fleet solve.
