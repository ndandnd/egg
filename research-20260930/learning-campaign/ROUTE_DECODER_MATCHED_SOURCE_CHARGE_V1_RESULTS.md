# Matched source-topology charging attribution on four TRAIN timetables

The pilot has **no demonstrated incremental route benefit from learned decoding** on these four predeclared TRAIN timetables. A source-only control that applies the same target-day fixed-route charging and independent physical replay matches or beats every replayed learned proposal. The prior apparent gains over direct source plans on 10037 and 10038 arose from giving learned covers a charging step while comparing them with source plans under their old charging schedules. These are four timetable comparisons with correlated model and source variants within each timetable, not 16 independent cases or a development/test estimate.

Costs are exact curved target-day whole-fleet bills, displayed to three decimals. The [read-only verifier](verify_route_decoder_matched_source_charge_v1.py) saves the exact rational values, plan hashes, per-source status/timing and paired differences in [verification JSON](ROUTE_DECODER_MATCHED_SOURCE_CHARGE_V1_REPLAY.json). It verified the admitted shard-04 source/target table hashes, exact group/source/cell identities, pool source hashes, all seven available direct and charged plans by independent physical replay and pricing-start checks, unchanged complete movement sets, and exact bills recomputed from replay. It preserved the eighth intended cell, 10037/source0, as a failed dependent target with no plan or cost. No optimizer or model was rerun.

The charged source control is a fixed-topology **linear** tariff optimization with the same 45-second phase, 55-second wall, one-thread budget as the decoder's fixed-charge stage. Its `OPTIMAL` status refers to that linear subproblem only; the exact curved bill has no curved-cost optimality claim. Indeed, on 10036 charging raises source0's curved bill from 538.987 to 539.232, so the reasonable source-only policy retains the cheaper direct plan. That direct-or-charged source policy is no worse than every learned plan on every timetable. Each source cell's paid time is retained in the JSON; the eight intended cells consumed 24.747 seconds in their original admitted label run, including 2.726 seconds for the failed 10037/source0 cell. These are pre-existing offline labels, not a new online decoder run.

Cold and retained hull numbers in the [pilot report](ROUTE_DECODER_TRAIN_PILOT_V1_RESULTS.md) remain separate computational controls. Their best single replayed whole-fleet columns are physical incumbents; their final convex-hull mixtures have multiple positive columns and are **not** physical single-fleet optima. A matching hull lower and upper bound certifies only the convex-hull relaxation. Neither those bounds nor the matched source comparison establish a global curved-cost physical fleet optimum or an end-to-end speed gain at matched quality.

It does not justify a larger model-size sweep or a route-benefit claim.
