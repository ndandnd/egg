# Four-group TRAIN route-decoder pilot: saved-evidence review

The pilot does **not** establish a learned complete-fleet benefit. Of 16 fixed learned arms (32/64 groups × logistic/tree × four timetables), 12 independently replayed full fleets and four had no integral path-cover incumbent at the 5-second cap. Five of the 12 replayed fleets had a topology absent from the observed source bank; seven reused a source topology, sometimes with different charging. The score-free cost-only decoder replayed on all four timetables. Each saved candidate and all seven available source plans passed fresh, optimizer-free physical replay, pricing-start, plan-hash, exact target-bill and topology checks. All 24 native hull assessments replayed identically. These are four predeclared TRAIN timetables with correlated model and source comparisons within each timetable, not a development or sealed-test estimate.

All costs are full-fleet operating plus curved target-supply costs, rounded to three decimals. The best *physical column* is a single independently replayed whole fleet from the saved hull. The CH number is a feasible **convex-hull mixture** cost, not a physical fleet cost: every final cold/retained mixture has two to four positive columns, and the `day` market has positive curvature (`b=1/900`). Even `status=certified` at 10036 and 10037 certifies the CH relaxation only; it does not prove an optimal single physical fleet. Bounded hulls retain lower/upper bounds but no matching CH certificate. The [corrected verification JSON](../../research-20260930/learning-campaign/ROUTE_DECODER_TRAIN_PILOT_V2_REPLAY.json) saves exact rational costs, whole-fleet plan hashes, mixture support/purity, CH bounds and per-arm provenance. A fixed-route charging `OPTIMAL` status likewise concerns only its linear fixed-route subproblem.

The learned route signal is clearest on 10037, the censored group: all four learned covers are novel and improve 7.047–7.996 over its one available source plan’s *direct* target bill. Yet the cost-only cover also improves 7.503, and the cold hull found a replayed 442.011 single fleet without a learned seed. Only the 64-group tree beats cost-only there, by about 0.493. On 10038, three learned arms reuse a source topology and improve its direct bill by about 1.738 after fixed-route charging; the one novel learned cover costs slightly more than those reuse arms. This comparison gives learned candidates a charging reoptimization that the direct source controls do not receive, so it cannot attribute that difference to route decoding. On 10036 all learned covers reuse a source topology but cost 0.245 more than the cheapest direct source plan. On 10039 all learned covers hit the same five-second HiGHS time limit without an integral incumbent; cost-only finds a replayed route but costs 25.375 more than the cheapest direct source plan.

The first, nearest-tariff and cheapest-direct-bill source controls coincide on 10036–10038. On 10039 first/nearest select source0 (590.608), while exact rescoring selects source1 (589.849). Base 10037 retains the failed source0 and three dependent target censors in the pool manifest; source0 was never imputed or retried. The source-acquisition ledger charges both intended source cells, including the failed cell: 30.91, 7.90, 14.37 and 127.05 seconds by group. This is sunk offline acquisition, separate from online decoding. The scoring child used 4.97, 5.10, 9.66 and 3.57 seconds respectively, including model loading; saved timing separates artifact load from movement inference. Successful learned cover, fixed charging and independent replay generally took under four seconds per arm; each separate hull took about 4–16 seconds on groups 10036–10038. On 10039 each learned cover consumed its approximately five-second cap, while each cold/retained/cost-only hull took roughly 61 seconds. The cold hull found a cheaper single physical fleet than every replayed learned proposal in these saved runs. An end-to-end speed benefit at matched physical quality or certificate level is **not established** by this four-case pilot.

The [read-only verifier](../../research-20260930/learning-campaign/verify_route_decoder_train_pilot_v2.py) checked all four completed task/result, model-score, source, wrapper and source-code hashes; fixed TRAIN registry and fold membership; pinned sklearn runtime; exact bills and eligibility; plan/replay/cover agreement; every saved whole-fleet hull column and CH mixture; hull reassessment; and failure paths with no fallback credit. All four Slurm wrappers returned zero without timeout. Task wall times were approximately 119, 42, 69 and 216 seconds; Slurm/accounting details are collected separately by root. No model or optimizer was rerun for this review.
