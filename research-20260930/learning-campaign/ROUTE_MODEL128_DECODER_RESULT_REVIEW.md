The 128-group replay report agrees with its saved JSON on the equal-timetable metrics, paired common-32 comparisons, and denominators: 128 groups, 255/256 observed source fleets, with base 10037 retained as a one-source group. All three edge classifiers improve kind-frequency log loss on 128/128 groups. HistGBDT has the best aggregate edge metrics; the common-32 gains are modest and mixed by metric. These are predictions of movement membership in observed source incumbents, not evidence of a legal or lower-cost route.

The decoder result report now distinguishes hull-relaxation mixtures from whole-fleet plans. Of 16 learned arms, 12 yielded independently replayed full fleets and four failed at the cover stage; cost-only yielded a fleet on all four groups. Five learned fleets use topologies absent from the available source bank, while seven reuse source topologies. The four cases are TRAIN diagnostics only. The direct source-bill comparison was initially unmatched for charging; the separately matched source-charge check summarized by root reports no incremental learned-route gain on these cases.

For example, on group 10036 the cold hull mixture cost is 538.100 cost units while the best saved replayed single column is 538.531; on 10037 the corresponding values are 441.075 and 442.011. The updated decoder report carries the separate best whole-fleet-column values for all four cases. Its `certified` label at 10036/10037 applies to the CH relaxation, not an optimal single physical fleet. The V2 read-only verifier replayed the saved hull columns; I performed no native replay or optimization.

Disposition: no metric or denominator mismatch found in the 128-group report, and no decoder evidence supports a learned complete-fleet benefit, speedup, or physical-optimality claim. Review used the saved replay/report artifacts and the corrected result text; no fit or solver run was performed.
