# v8 graph training-budget launch review

The v8 package is ready for root's freeze and launch. The fresh-fit loop preserves the v6 graph architecture, feature transform, batch order and weighting, deterministic CPU setup, Adam settings, strict inner-loss selection, and patience rule. It initializes a new model and optimizer per arm. For every completed epoch through 300, it checks fit/inner losses and improvement decisions against the pinned v6 curve; the epoch-300 gate also checks the selected epoch and tensors, and movement-keyed fit/inner labels, probabilities, rankings, and fixed-threshold classes. Both arms must pass and persist selected fit/inner evidence and the inner promotion before outer predictions begin.

Two review findings were corrected before approval: epoch-300 current and prefix-best states are now saved before scalar-anchor checks, with a mismatch fixture proving those states and the failure ledger survive; the wrapper now records early guard failures as nonzero attempts, with a fixture checking both receipt and shell status. The training stream is flushed and synced each epoch, best weights are checkpointed every 50 epochs and at anchor/stop/failure points, and each task path is exclusive with no retry or resume.

The declared limits are internally consistent: 12 tasks at concurrency four, one requested CPU, 8 GB, one hour and a 3,500-second wrapper cap; each candidate has a 1,600-second soft cap and each fold a 3,400-second cap. The runtime probe runs before the pool is loaded, and the wrapper records its result and allocation. The study remains a fixed exact128 TRAIN extension; a longer epoch cap alone does not establish generalization or physical route benefit.

Focused validation passed: 9 tiny fixtures, Python compilation of the two new Python modules, and `bash -n` for the Slurm wrapper. The fixtures cover both architecture anchors, early patience and unavailable anchors, partial-gradient discard, failure preservation, outer-data ordering, runtime rejection, and wrapper guard receipts. This review did not run a campaign fit or inspect outer outcomes; the pinned Linux runtime probe and actual resource accounting remain launch-time checks.
