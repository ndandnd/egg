Seeds are repeated measurements, not independent timetables or model-training replicates. The scale generator's deterministic draws use base_id/duty/field, not service count: the 60-trip case is a trip prefix of its same-ID 80-trip counterpart, with shared profile parameters. Resource power changes with size. These are four progenitor groups and eight correlated size cells, not eight independent draws.

The protocol's “large majority” robustness gate has no numerical cutoff. Do not invent a retrospective threshold or select only the better keep fraction. - Scheduler COMPLETED is not a physical-success status. A no-incumbent or replay/projection refusal remains an unsuccessful accepted-plan outcome. Do not silently exclude joint failures, count them as learned wins, or replace them with fallbacks; the executable explicitly promises no fallback. - The no-time wrapper preserves the planner's earlier best plan when an extra round cannot start. However, extraction/projection exceptions are not caught inside the planner: a later refusal can propagate to E3's outer exception handler before the earlier best plan is returned. Report such a run as its recorded failure, not as evidence that every internally encountered valid incumbent was retained. Paired seed numbers align configurations but do not imply correlated random solver trajectories across graphs.
- Learned4 versus cold4 is the matched four-round comparison; learned4 versus cold additionally changes round allocation. E11 tests this pruning pipeline, not ranking quality isolated from graph size, and has no greedy/random arm to establish that learning itself causes the gain.
