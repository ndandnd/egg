# EV pricing and complete-fleet hull certification: source review

Research date: 2 October 2026. Research only: no optimization or cluster runs, no sealed data. Local mapping checked against `doc/GPT_HANDOFF_20261002.md`, `src/egglab/native_pathflow.py`, and `src/egglab/native_hull.py`. Effort and compute estimates below are planning estimates, not facts reported in papers.

## Decision

The highest-leverage near-term change is a **certificate-aware pricing policy**: cheap learned/restricted solves generate replayed complete-fleet incumbents, while the full physical model supplies the global lower bound. Spend the last portion of the budget improving that bound at a promising price rather than generating more near-duplicate columns. Second, test price stabilization with the existing supply curvature and explicit inexact-oracle safeguards. A new route branch-and-price implementation is a conditional investment, not the default recommendation.

Novelty needs revision: supervised GNN pruning and greedy/learned hybrids for electric buses have direct prior art. Learning to prune these graphs cannot carry an Operations Research contribution. Price-responsive learning coupled to **independently valid complete-fleet certificates** is a stronger hypothesis, requiring theoretical separation and computational comparison. [Gerbaux et al., published primary PDF](https://research.dial.uclouvain.be/bitstreams/6bd29199-00d3-46c3-a27a-38bcc31ecf17/download).

## What transfers to the current oracle

### Charging-aware dominance is a modeling result, not a score threshold

EV2's battery resource extension and backward feasible-energy/cost bounds are valuable ingredients for eliminating **provably infeasible** movements or partial schedules. They avoid gratuitously expanding the entire charging grid. However, EGG pricing minimizes operations plus a time-dependent linear price on energy, under shared interval capacity and terminal full replenishment. A label that reaches a trip with more SOC can differ in charging cost and station-time footprint; dropping the other label requires a proof for this objective and all relevant shared-resource duals.

EV3 retains a cost–SOC tradeoff function, which is closer to that requirement than keeping a single cheapest/highest-SOC label. But importing its label algebra still requires matching EGG's service, availability, continuous-energy and replenishment conventions. Grid discretization that **removes** feasible charging choices gives an incumbent/restricted problem; a conservative relaxation or exhaustive exact breakpoints are needed for globally valid lower bounds. Sparse routing plus continuous charging is not equivalent to exact pricing solely because charging is continuous.

An initial useful application is a route-based relaxation **inside** the complete-fleet oracle, or a structural preprocessing bound, without changing the external column contract. Route columns must carry the exact shared charging-capacity coefficients, trip coverage and bus limits. At fixed outer price, an exactly priced route-LP relaxation yields a lower bound on the integral fleet optimum; an integral route selection with replayed charging yields a feasible fleet column. These are different outputs.

### The convex hull being priced must stay the same

Let \(X\) be the full physical fleet set, including every trip covered once and all shared charging constraints. Define fleet projection \(L(x)\), operations cost \(c(x)\), and

\[
H=\min_{(L,c)\in\operatorname{conv}\{(L(x),c(x)):x\in X\}} c+S(L),\qquad
\phi(p)=\min_{x\in X}\{c(x)+p^T L(x)\}.
\]

EGG's complete-fleet D-W master mixes **already jointly feasible fleet schedules**. A conventional EV route master instead convexifies individual bus choices and imposes coupling constraints on the fractional route mixture. In general, convexifying first and then intersecting with fleet coupling gives a relaxation of the complete-fleet convex hull. It need not give the same value or support prices. Equivalence requires a proof; route-LP success or absence of route reduced costs is insufficient.

A route LP is still a useful **valid relaxation** of \(\phi(p)\), provided every physical fleet embeds in it and all objective coefficients agree. Beware: an incomplete route RMP is a restriction of that relaxation, so its minimization value is generally an **upper** bound on the full route LP, and cannot by itself be used as a lower bound on \(\phi(p)\). Full route pricing, or a valid dual correction from globally bounded reduced costs, is required.

For example, if \(\sum_r y_r\le K\), master duals obey all required sign constraints, and a certified bound \(\rho\) is no larger than every route reduced cost, then a safely corrected dual objective is

\[
z_{\mathrm{dual}}+K\min(0,\rho).
\]

This follows from writing the route objective as its dual contribution plus reduced-cost terms. It is a globally valid route-relaxation bound, hence a fleet-pricing lower bound, when the embedding and dual identity have been proved. A pruned-network minimum reduced cost is not a valid \(\rho\). Per-depot cardinal bounds give analogous summed corrections.

### A pruned oracle can generate columns but cannot certify absence

For any restricted set \(X_R\subseteq X\), \(\min_{X_R}(c+pL)\ge\phi(p)\). Even a globally optimal restricted solve, or its solver lower bound, may exceed the true full-model optimum. Thus a restricted MIP lower bound must **never** enter `fenchel_bound` as a global pricing lower bound. The full-model MIP bound is valid under the existing admission contract; so is a proven relaxation bound. A restricted feasible plan can enter the pool after exact replay and provide a full-model MIP start.

No certificate is lost by using heuristic columns first, provided termination and the reported lower enclosure still depend on full-set bounds. ML2 already demonstrates the heuristic/full-network switching pattern. This is a useful baseline and an established technique, not novelty by itself.

## Use the certificate gap to allocate work

For EGG's supply function

\[
S(L)=\sum_t a_t L_t+\tfrac12 b_tL_t^2,\quad L_t\ge0,
\]

the conjugate is \(S^*(p)=\sum_t ((p_t-a_t)_+)^2/(2b_t)\) when \(b_t>0\). For \(b_t=0\), its finite domain requires \(p_t\le a_t\). `native_hull.py` implements these cases. A globally admitted bound \(\ell(p)\le\phi(p)\) gives

\[
D(p)=\ell(p)-S^*(p)\le H\le U,
\]

where \(U\) is a replayed feasible-mixture objective. A replayed pricing incumbent score \(u(p)\) supplies \(\phi(p)\le u(p)\), not a dual certificate. These statements are algebraic deductions from the checked formulation, not empirical claims from a source.

At a mixture load \(\bar L\), choose \(p=\nabla S(\bar L)\). With mixture operations cost \(\bar c\), the gap becomes

\[
U-D(p)=\bar c+p^T\bar L-\ell(p).
\]

Let \(m(p)\) be the minimum linear score in the existing pool. Then this separates into the observable **pool optimization gap** \(\bar c+p^T\bar L-m(p)\) and **missing-column/pricing gap** \(m(p)-\ell(p)\). The latter combines undiscovered columns and loose full-model bounds. Pricing's enclosure width \(u(p)-\ell(p)\), duplicate projections and repeated full-model root bounds help determine which dominates. At prices rounded from exact gradients, retain the exact Fenchel residual rather than assuming equality.

Practical policy: when \(u(p)<m(p)\), an improving column is already found; exact pricing optimality is unnecessary before adding it. When the same projection repeats and the pool gap is small, resume/tighten the full oracle at that price. Stop certification when \(\ell(p)\ge U+S^*(p)-\varepsilon\), with the project's numerical guards. Spend accuracy on prices that can improve the best global enclosure. Preserve the best valid \(D(p)\) across iterations.

### Curvature-aware stabilization and inexact oracles

The dual objective is \(d(p)=\phi(p)-S^*(p)\). In positive-load coordinates the conjugate already has curvature \(1/b_t\). Price scaling or a proximal metric proportional to \(\sum_t(\Delta p_t)^2/b_t\) is therefore a natural EGG-specific experiment. Zero-curvature coordinates require separate domain handling. Strong convexity in load does not imply unique mixture weights; degeneracy among fleet columns remains possible.

A cheap first comparison is adaptive damping between the restricted-master price and the best certified price. Each proposed price is globally priced; its bound is evaluated in the **original** dual. A proximal/bundle variant is a second step if oscillation explains the trace. Avoid reporting a penalized master objective as the original hull value.

Exact-oracle bundle assumptions deserve care. A feasible incumbent \(x\), even from a restricted oracle, gives the globally valid affine **upper** model \(\phi(q)\le c(x)+q^TL(x)\). It need not furnish an exact supergradient at the sampled price. A full-model lower bound \(\ell\) plus incumbent \(u\) establishes an inexact-oracle error at most \(u-\ell\); serious/null-step acceptance must account for that interval. A restricted bound does not establish this error. CH3's exact per-generator oracle experiments do not settle the inexact EGG case.

IO1 supplies relevant on-demand-accuracy precedent. A theorem-bearing EGG variant would need a precise descent test, admissible oracle interval/subgradient model, and accuracy policy matching a proved framework. A wall-clock allocation heuristic remains useful, but should be presented with finite-budget valid enclosures rather than unsupported asymptotic convergence claims.

## Do not pursue as the next major investment

- **Column-selection ML before profiling the QP master.** ML1 addresses a master-time bottleneck. E9's stated bottleneck is full-fleet pricing; pruning a small pool cannot improve the full-model lower bound.
- **Claiming route-LP prices are complete-fleet CH prices.** The convexification/coupling order can change the target. Keep the route LP as an explicitly named relaxation until equivalence is proved.
- **Using station-sequence dominance from EV2 without time-price/resource analysis.** Fixed arc cost and battery feasibility do not encode all EGG charging tradeoffs.
- **Importing UC Benders/hybrid/ADMM architecture on the strength of headline speedups.** CH2 and CH4 rely on participant hull structure and independence EGG has not established. Favor strengthened formulation, global pricing bounds and measured stabilization first.
- **Presenting GNN arc pruning, incumbent imitation, per-node arc safety or heuristic/full pricing switching as the core novelty.** Prior art is direct. The contribution must survive a structure-aware greedy/hybrid baseline, demonstrate price-response value, and improve globally admitted certificate behavior beyond faster restricted solves.

## Research risks to resolve in the final synthesis

1. Full-model best bound, restricted incumbent score, pool optimum and route-relaxation bound need distinct names and provenance in the report. A solver's `ObjBound` is only global over the model it actually solved.
2. A positive difference between a physical **incumbent** and a hull value does not prove that the physical optimum has no supporting price. Such an obstruction requires an independently valid physical lower bound strictly above a valid hull upper bound (or an equivalent theorem/certificate).
3. Classical exact CG finite-convergence results for polyhedral linear masters do not automatically give finite convergence for EGG's curved supply objective and numerically bounded oracle. Use the actual \(U-D\) enclosure as the stopping certificate.
4. An Operations Research submission would need a substantive mathematical insight or method plus broad, fair evidence. A pipeline assembled from known pruning, MIP, QP and D-W components with 40–105-trip demonstrations is not by itself evidence of SOTA or scale leadership. New experiments should isolate why complete-fleet convexification and supply curvature materially alter learning/pricing, not merely improve aggregate runtime.
