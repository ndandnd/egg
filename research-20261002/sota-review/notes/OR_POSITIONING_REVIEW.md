# Adversarial OR positioning review — 2 October 2026

Research only. Read the Gerbaux primary full PDF, `ECONOMIC_CERTIFICATES_AND_NOVELTY.md`, and E9 results; reuse the previously inspected E7/E8/E10 evidence. No experiments or sealed-data reads. This is a contribution assessment, not an endorsement or a novelty proof.

## Strongest defensible potential contribution

The strongest candidate is **budgeted computation of operationally validated complete-fleet schedules together with certified economic supportability intervals**, using learned search to supply feasible columns and full-domain pricing bounds to establish what prices can support. The result should explain when investing another unit of time in primal search versus certification changes the economic conclusion under coupled charging and convex supply cost.

To reach an OR-level algorithmic contribution, this needs a substantive fleet-specific mechanism: for example, tighter hardware/energy-derived bounds that enable useful safe price-bound reuse or screening, combined with an allocation policy whose advantage survives strong baselines. Show validity of the economic enclosure independently of prediction accuracy, and demonstrate materially earlier decisive conclusions. A collection of known components plus elementary weak-duality inequalities is insufficient. The current notes correctly label those inequalities as working derivations, not new theorems.

## Three novelty threats

1. **Direct electric-bus arc pruning, including per-trip top-m, already exists.** Gerbaux, Desaulniers and Cappart, *“A machine-learning-based column generation heuristic for electric bus scheduling,” Computers & Operations Research* 173 (2025), 106848, DOI [10.1016/j.cor.2024.106848](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cor.2024.106848), uses supervised GNN arc selection for capacitated charging with a nonlinear charging model. Section 5.2.4/Algorithm 2 selects the highest-scored incoming **and** outgoing arcs at each trip vertex and unions them. Section 5.3 combines GNN and greedy arc sets. Its large-instance/transfer experiments also undermine novelty based merely on generalization or scale. E10's selection principle is directly anticipated. Different costs, architecture, or compact MILP implementation establish a variant, not originality by themselves. [Primary full PDF](https://research.dial.uclouvain.be/bitstreams/6bd29199-00d3-46c3-a27a-38bcc31ecf17/download).

2. **Learning to accelerate certified column generation is established.** Sugishita, Grothey and McKinnon, *“Use of Machine Learning Models to Warmstart Column Generation for Unit Commitment,” INFORMS Journal on Computing* 36(4), 1129–1146 (2024), already predicts initial duals and compares with column prepopulation, LP initialization, and cold starts. The publisher abstract was independently checked in this pass; detailed performance should follow the companion full-text review. [Publisher source/DOI](https://pubsonline.informs.org/doi/abs/10.1287/ijoc.2022.0140). Ordinary stabilized/inexact CG and warm starts are also necessary baselines. Complete-fleet economic certification is a meaningful target, but its combination with ML is not automatically a new method.

3. **The proposed guarantees largely inherit standard optimization/statistics.** Fenchel bounds, retained feasible references, relaxation-based reduced-cost fixing, and instance-level conformal coverage have established foundations. Apollo-MILP already alternates prediction, trust-region correction, and uncertainty-guided fixing; see the [ICLR 2025 primary paper](https://proceedings.iclr.cc/paper_files/paper/2025/file/ebcdb4d0372ebbdd0e6a7dce9662b6b4-Paper-Conference.pdf). A new allocation/screening theorem must add something beyond valid bookkeeping. Synthetic conformal calibration cannot certify public optimality; reference-fleet coverage does not imply optimum retention.

## Computational controls required

- **E9 timing:** seeds come from three learned 300-second arms, with fixed-route charging reoptimization/replay, before the 600-second hull run. The ten tighter enclosures show a warm-start benefit at equal *hull-stage* time. Report cold-start end-to-end results including all instance-specific seed generation, scoring, repair, and replay. Also report a separately labeled amortized case with a declared reuse count and break-even point. Give cold/full-price alternatives the same total budget and compare matched ordinary incumbent/column pools. Do not assume all seed runs consumed their maximum; measure actual cost.
- **Evaluation independence:** E7/E8/E10 public outcomes have informed scorer/keep choices. Those timetables are development evidence even though training never saw them. Freeze methods, targets, and stopping rules prospectively; obtain additional authorized public groups for evaluation. Seeds and tariffs on one timetable are dependent repetitions, not independent transfer samples. Respect the sealed-data boundary.
- **Reliability:** count E10's four rejected Eberbach per-trip runs as failures. Any repair must pass the original replay rules, with its time and objective change charged to the method. Preserve earlier accepted incumbents and compare identical round policies. Report failures and timeouts alongside bill, buses, time-to-quality, hull width, and certified regret.
- **Certification:** use unrestricted or safely screened pricing bounds, original complete-fleet hull semantics, and explicit arithmetic margins. Compare retained-column, stabilized-CG, full-pricing warmstart, and simple widening controls. Verify that any alternative route decomposition certifies the same economic object.

## Claims unavailable today

No first GNN EV pruning or per-trip top-m claim; no established end-to-end E9 speedup; no certified public generalization; no demonstrated robust deployment while replay failures persist. E9 closes only one hull and every physical-minus-hull gap contains zero, so it does not establish large-instance non-support. Positive regret of a suboptimal seed alone does not prove institutional impossibility. Tiny development gaps require defensible numerical margins. The strongest contribution remains a falsifiable research direction until the missing theory, controls, and decisive certificates are produced.
