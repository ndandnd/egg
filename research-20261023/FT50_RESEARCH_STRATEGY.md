# EGG: a falsifiable route toward an FT50 contribution

**23 October 2026. Bounded positioning review; no experiment authorization.** This assessment uses the 17 October reviewer handoff and the compact 2 October SOTA review. It does not independently reproduce their numerical results. No sealed DEV/TEST/A6/B3/confirmation/GIRO data were opened, and no solver, training or cluster work was performed.

## Assessment and journal fit

**The current study supports a next mechanism test, not an FT50 submission claim.** Its strongest result is that learned ranking improves feasible decision quality in one larger synthetic regime under a fixed short deadline. Its strongest weakness is that the apparent gain could substantially reflect a weak comparator interacting with solver congestion. Neither attention nor connection pruning is the contribution to sell.

Operations Research and Management Science are both listed as FT50 journals by [Stevens Institute of Technology Library](https://library.stevens.edu/ft50), which identifies an April 2026 FT update; [HSE's university guide](https://gsb.hse.ru/en/financialtimesfifty) independently lists both. The [FT source](https://www.ft.com/content/3405a512-5cbb-11e1-8f1f-00144feabdc0) returned a subscription page, so membership is corroborated through university sources rather than claimed as a direct reading of the current FT list. Membership says nothing about acceptance prospects.

**Operations Research is the more coherent primary aspiration if EGG develops a general optimization contribution:** a principled allocation of restricted candidate search and unrestricted certification under coupled charging, with a precise bound interface, nontrivial structural result and convincing computational advantage. This is positioning judgment, not a journal requirement or a novelty finding. The [journal's editorial statement](https://pubsonline.informs.org/page/opre/editorial-statement) is the primary scope reference.

**Management Science becomes plausible if the central result changes an important operational or economic decision:** when a fleet can respond to electricity prices, when price signals cannot support the desired physical schedule, and how much reliable computation changes the operator's choice. Its Optimization & Decision Analytics statement emphasizes practical impact and clear applicable insights, and its data-science statement emphasizes a relevant application context. A synthetic ranking comparison alone does not deliver that message. See the [current editorial statement](https://pubsonline.informs.org/page/mnsc/editorial-statement). Neither journal should drive premature theoretical ornamentation or a larger neural architecture.

## Novelty boundary

Gerbaux, Desaulniers and Cappart (2025), *A machine-learning-based column generation heuristic for electric bus scheduling*, already reduce electric-bus scheduling networks using supervised GNN arc selection and greedy techniques; their combination performs best in the reported study. The author institution's record reports experiments with hundreds to thousands of trips. EGG therefore cannot present learned electric-bus arc pruning, greedy/learned combinations, or simply scaling from 40 to 80 trips as new. Different tariffs, charging coupling and solver formulations matter only if they produce an identified methodological or operational distinction. [Primary institutional publication record and open-access paper link](https://research.dial.uclouvain.be/entities/publication/ce60f346-f699-41e0-b2d3-d0d7744d91aa); [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cor.2024.106848).

Decision-focused evaluation is also established: Elmachtoub and Grigas's *Smart “Predict, then Optimize”*, Management Science 68(1), studies prediction through induced decision loss. EGG predicts useful connections rather than necessarily unknown objective coefficients; their consistency results cannot simply be transferred. The relevant lesson is to evaluate physical decisions and economic conclusions, rather than edge accuracy. [Publisher record](https://pubsonline.informs.org/doi/10.1287/mnsc.2020.3922).

Recovery, incumbent retention, decomposition and inexact oracles have prior art identified in the October review. Combining them is a research design, not proof of novelty. A potentially distinctive claim would connect the specific charging/price structure to a validated decision-and-certificate algorithm. That claim remains open.

## What the present result establishes

The handoff reports graph-versus-heuristic median group bill improvements of **0.47% at 40 trips and 68.36% at 80 trips**, using ten different timetable groups at each size and three paired seeds. Graph versus CatBoost is essentially tied at 40 trips and favors graph by 44.37% at 80. These are finite-budget feasible-bill comparisons; the pooled 33.03% obscures the regime split. Three seeds do not turn twenty timetables into sixty independent applications.

**A fresh post-hoc compact-receipt check sharply narrows the mechanism.** At 80 trips, 68400 of 74415.49487672 summed paired bill savings (**91.916%**) comes from vehicle-count cost. Median vehicle counts are 4 versus 27.5; graph uses fewer vehicles in all 30 pairs. The median paired reduction is 23, which differs from subtracting the two marginal medians. This sum-based component share does **not** decompose the 68.36% median group relative gain. At 40 trips, all 30 pairs have identical vehicle counts (both arm medians are 3), so all paired bill differences are electricity cost. Even there, changed routing and energy requirements can affect the bill without better price responsiveness. This strengthens the route-construction/search hypothesis and makes tariff-aware value unproven. See [independent compact-receipt check](BILL_MECHANISM_INDEPENDENT_CHECK.md).

The repaired common pipeline and reported successful independent replay of all 342 supervisor-valid plans make the next controlled experiment more credible. They do not prove optimality, deployment reliability, or the correctness of every economic certificate. Restricted MIP bounds, fixed-route charging objectives and unrestricted pricing bounds must retain different names and domains. At the October17 checkpoint recovery was a contract/prototype. The October23 follow-up implemented the interface and passed six handcrafted native primitive checks; its benefit on timetables remains untested.

Only one day tariff and a shared synthetic population were tested. Different base timetables occupy the two sizes, so size is confounded with timetable composition. Retention uses the same rule but slightly different resulting cardinalities. The simple frozen heuristic is legitimate as a prespecified baseline, but cannot represent the strongest nonlearned method. No-plan endpoints are not infeasibility proofs; bill comparisons must preserve valid-pair denominators and separately report failure/censoring.

## Three falsifiable hypotheses

1. **Useful routing information survives strong controls.** At equal total decision time and matched retained cardinality/coverage, graph ranking improves group-level feasible-bill trajectories over charging-aware greedy, LP-informed ranking and CatBoost, including newly generated intermediate sizes. Falsifier: the advantage disappears against those controls or after equalizing construction/charging overhead. This tests information value, not generalization by itself.

2. **Recoverable restriction improves the full decision process.** A restricted prefix followed by restoration of all connections, with a replay-valid retained witness, improves a prespecified bill-versus-time/feasibility endpoint against both static restriction and a full-graph solve given the whole allowance. Falsifier: restoration overhead consumes the gain, or an identical classical prefix performs as well. Preserving one acquired witness is a narrow safety property; it does not imply dominance over a different allocation of the entire budget.

3. **Price-aware computation changes a certifiable economic conclusion.** Across prespecified tariff and charging-bottleneck regimes, the proposed candidate/certificate allocation reaches a tolerance-qualified support or non-support conclusion sooner than classical candidates with the same unrestricted oracle and total budget. Falsifier: only incumbent bills improve while conclusions remain unresolved, or stronger conventional oracle effort matches the benefit. An unresolved interval remains unresolved; smaller width alone is not a policy result.

## Ranked next experiments and stopping decisions

**1. Challenge the explanation before expanding training.** Freeze a small development comparison of graph, CatBoost, charging-aware greedy and LP-informed scores. Add a tuned full-graph solver control and a classical neighborhood/restriction control where implementable without changing the physical model. Charge preprocessing, relaxation and scoring to the same allowance. Match cardinality/coverage, and retain a common-policy comparison separately if exact matching alters the method. Track fleet-count trajectories and compare both unrestricted total bills and bills conditional on the same feasible fleet count; report any conditioning selection. Include intermediate sizes and nested/subsampled timetable constructions to separate size from generator composition. Report group-level paired curves, first feasibility, failures and costs; do not reuse pooled percentage as the primary endpoint. If classical methods erase the graph gain, retain the simpler method and redirect the paper.

**2. Test recovery as allocation, not as a safeguard slogan.** Compare static restriction, learned-prefix restoration, classical-prefix restoration and unrestricted-from-start using identical total budgets and explicit switch-time policies frozen before results. Validate split charging intervals, physical witness mapping and fallback retention before launching. Diagnose model reconstruction versus search cost. Promote recovery only if it improves a useful budget range rather than a selected endpoint; preserving the prefix alone is insufficient.

**3. Separate price responsiveness from timetable memorization.** Use development-only tariff perturbations and charging-capacity/energy-profile shifts, holding schedules paired where possible. Include tariff-blind ranking, price-feature ablation and a cheap charging-opportunity score. Predefine decision-relevant bill decompositions so fleet/deadhead changes cannot masquerade as electricity-price sensitivity. Freeze the final evaluation protocol before opening any sealed source. A failed shift test should narrow the claimed operating regime, not trigger adaptive tuning on the sealed cohort.

**4. Pursue certification only after a bottleneck receipt.** On admitted small cases with strong reference bounds, compare identical candidate seeds and identical unrestricted oracles under different effort allocations. Check sign conclusions, tolerances and oracle provenance independently. Prove the narrow witness/bound interface actually used; distinguish numerical qualification from exact rational proof. If unrestricted lower bounds remain weak regardless of candidates, strengthen that oracle before spending on more ranking data. If only primal benefits survive, write a focused scheduling contribution and reassess journal ambition.

## Source and access limits

This is a targeted review, not an exhaustive novelty clearance. Current web checks covered primary journal statements, the Gerbaux institutional abstract/metadata and paper availability, and the SPO publisher abstract/metadata; no new full theorem audit or implementation reproduction was performed. Detailed Gerbaux per-trip overlap and broader recovery/certification prior art are inherited from the October review and should be checked against full text before manuscript claims. The FT page was paywalled; university lists provide corroboration. No prediction of acceptance is justified. The decisive next evidence is whether a general, price-relevant mechanism survives strong classical controls—not whether another architecture improves the existing development score.
