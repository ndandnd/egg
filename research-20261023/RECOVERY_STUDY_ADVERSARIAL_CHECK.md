# Independent adversarial design check

Design-only review of the three companion notes; no execution, inference, assets or data inspected. The pending qualification extension remains unapproved. Five issues need resolution before any prospective study freeze.

1. **Common retention rules do not hold graph size constant.** Global30% plus incoming/outgoing top-three coverage produces a score-dependent union: overlaps differ, so sources can retain different edge counts. Equal rules therefore identify the practical effect of the whole ranking-and-retention policy, not ranking quality at equal graph density. State that estimand explicitly and report flexible/mandatory counts, density and coverage additions for every arm. If equal-cardinality attribution is essential, prospectively replace the common rule with an exact-count, coverage-constrained selector whose count is determined from timetable structure alone and is large enough for the required coverage. Freeze its tie/fill procedure before outcomes; do not obtain the count from another model's scores, score other models outside the clock, or retrospectively match successful graphs. This is a design choice, not a request to add arms.

2. **The learned-value contrast remains a pipeline comparison.** CA1 has direct access to both `a,b`, whereas the frozen learned scorers receive price summaries from `a` only; their computation, retained density and search trajectories also differ. A graph-versus-CA1 win would establish a matched-time algorithm advantage under these contracts, not an isolated benefit of learning or graph architecture. Removing static arms additionally prevents a restoration-effect estimate. Keep those claim limits in the primary estimand. The mechanism subset isolates supplied ranking-price information within each learner, not retraining value or general tariff transfer. With fixed global summaries, nonwindow CatBoost features remain unchanged, while graph message passing could transmit neighboring charging-window changes; neither sensitivity nor economic benefit follows automatically. Missing `b` is a nonlinear-target limitation, not a universal flaw in price-taking prediction.

3. **Specified classical algorithms are not yet credible executable comparators.** CF1's optimistic SOC merges ignore shared charging contention; two reverse repairs and a short fixed-topology allowance may systematically fail even when good constructive routes exist. CA1's proportional-fill, pair-local proxy is not an optimized charging cost. Neither fact invalidates a fixed baseline, but naming them “charging-aware” or “constructive” cannot substitute for qualifying their exact implementation, useful witness production and80-trip timing before study outcomes. Freeze the single variants first; if qualification fails, revise the prospective comparison rather than silently interpret a weak/no-plan baseline as learned superiority.

4. **The budget closes arithmetically, not operationally.** The28,200s plan leaves only600s below the ceiling and600s below dispatch stop after nonshutdown reservations. A1:1 overhead cap is not validated for two workers,60s endpoints and new capture volume. Admission must debit remaining preparation, verification and shutdown commitments plus the entire next outer/monitor allocation, with no double charging of monitor children already included in reaped-child CPU. Qualify aggregate RSS, monitor latency and evidence-write latency under the proposed concurrency before accepting feasibility; otherwise retain an explicitly incomplete pilot rather than assume all192 cells fit.

5. **Failure-aware wins need an auditable classification rule.** “Algorithmically late” is a loss, whereas infrastructure unknown is missing; a slow replay/capture/write or monitor-induced cancellation can straddle those categories. Freeze cause precedence and evidence requirements before outcomes, retaining ambiguous cases as unknown. Report worst/best-case bounds at the fixed seed/base denominator, including an incomplete dispatch suffix. Both-valid bill gains and equal-fleet subsets remain selected descriptive summaries, not substitutes for this primary missingness analysis.
