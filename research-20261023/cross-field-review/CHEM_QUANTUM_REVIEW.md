# Chemistry and quantum-algorithm transfer to EGG

Research date: 2026-10-23. Scope: literature and proposed CPU experiments only; no experiment, solver, cluster job, or code change. Access details and exact searches are in `CHEM_QUANTUM_SEARCH_LEDGER.md`.

The strongest analogy is **adaptive selection in an exponentially large interacting space**, especially selected configuration interaction (SCI). The transferable mechanism is to solve a restricted problem, estimate which excluded choices matter *given the current solution*, restore a batch, and solve again. It is substantially closer to EGG than a proposal to run bus scheduling on quantum hardware. None of the reviewed papers establishes a bus-routing theorem or justifies treating pruning accuracy as global certification.

EGG's present result should remain described as finding inexpensive feasible plans under a time budget. The reported 80-trip advantage is principally fewer buses within 60 seconds; chemistry does not turn that into evidence that tariff interactions were learned. A good next contribution would explain **when adaptive restoration finds jointly valuable choices that a static learned restriction misses**, and separately measure the cost of certifying their value.

## Explicit mathematical mapping

For a finite-basis molecular Hamiltonian, SCI approximates the lowest eigenvalue by minimizing the Rayleigh quotient over a selected span: `E(S)=min{c* H_SS c: ||c||=1}`. A normalized coefficient vector is a feasible quantum state. Its energy is an upper bound on the full-basis ground energy, and nested spaces cannot worsen the exact restricted minimum. In EGG, let `z(S)` minimize the original objective over feasible plans using an allowed edge/route set S. A feasible incumbent also gives an upper bound on the full minimization problem, and exact restricted optima are monotone under expansion. This elementary set-inclusion fact transfers directly; it does not bound the remaining improvement.

| Chemistry object | EGG counterpart | What survives and what breaks |
|---|---|---|
| Selected determinants / active operator pool | Allowed trips-to-trips edges, complete bus duties, or charging patterns | A restricted representation controls computational cost. A determinant is a basis vector in a superposition; an EGG edge is only a component of a discrete plan. Route columns are a closer analogy than isolated edges. |
| Restricted diagonalization and updated coefficients | Restricted optimization and current fleet/charging allocation | Importance depends on the current restricted solution. Quantum coefficients have no direct bus analogue; edge frequencies or reduced costs are different quantities. |
| External residual `r_a=sum_i H_ai c_i` | Omitted-column reduced cost, constraint sensitivity, or measured repair gain | Both identify missing directions relative to a current solution. Exact reduced-cost pricing requires an appropriate continuous master formulation and duals; MILP incumbents do not supply equivalent prices. |
| Singles/doubles excitation neighborhood | One-route/two-route repairs, edge exchanges, or charging-time shifts | Structured generation avoids scoring the entire universe. A route's feasibility and nonlinear charging coupling must still be checked. |
| Second-order correction and stochastic standard error | Estimated improvement from sampled restorations, with uncertainty | An audit can quantify sampled missed opportunities. Quantum perturbation formulas and their error bars are not bounds on unsearched bus plans. |
| Entangled orbitals / jointly useful operators | Edges or duties useful only in coordinated fleet repairs | Selection needs interaction and coverage. Shannon entropy across candidate plans is an empirical statistic, not quantum entanglement. |

## Ten primary papers and what to borrow

**1. Holmes, Tubman, and Umrigar (2016), [Heat-bath Configuration Interaction](https://arxiv.org/html/1606.07453), JCTC 12, 3674–3680, DOI [10.1021/acs.jctc.6b00407](https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.jctc.6b00407).** HCI replaces a more expensive first-order coefficient calculation by `max_i |H_ai c_i|`; sorted excitation lists avoid generating small terms. Crucially, it separates a threshold for building the variational space from one for estimating the correction. EGG adaptation: separate a construction budget from a stricter omitted-option audit budget; benchmark inexpensive ranking against complete neighborhood evaluation. The sorted-generation trick requires a computable bound that permits skipping a whole candidate block. Learned scores alone do not provide this. Access: primary full-text HTML, selection equations and generation procedure inspected.

**2. Sharma, Holmes, Jeanmairet, Alavi, and Umrigar (2017; preprint 2016), [Semistochastic Heat-bath Configuration Interaction](https://arxiv.org/html/1610.06660), JCTC 13, 1595–1604, DOI [10.1021/acs.jctc.6b01028](https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.jctc.6b01028).** SHCI evaluates large correction contributions deterministically and the remainder stochastically, relieving memory pressure while controlling sampling error. Borrow the two-part audit: exhaustively inspect an important stratum, sample the tail with known inclusion probabilities, and report sampling uncertainty. Do not label that uncertainty an optimization gap. The underlying correction is an additive, specified perturbation sum; improvements from overlapping bus repairs are generally nonadditive. Access: primary full-text HTML, variational and perturbative stages inspected.

**3. Tubman, Lee, Takeshita, Head-Gordon, and Whaley (2016), [A deterministic alternative to the full configuration interaction quantum Monte Carlo method](https://arxiv.org/html/1603.02686), JCP 145, 044112, DOI [10.1063/1.4955109](https://doi.org/10.1063/1.4955109).** ASCI separates a small core used to generate candidates from the larger target space used in diagonalization, ranking by approximate amplitudes. EGG adaptation: generate repairs around several high-quality, structurally distinct plans, then optimize over their union. Distinguish generator size from retained-solver size in all ablations. Importance concentrated near the core is an empirical sparsity hypothesis, not a completeness guarantee. Access: primary full-text HTML; amplitude formula, core and target definitions inspected.

**4. Schriber and Evangelista (2016), [An adaptive configuration interaction approach for strongly correlated electrons with tunable accuracy](https://arxiv.org/html/1603.08063), JCP 144, 161106, DOI [10.1063/1.4948308](https://doi.org/10.1063/1.4948308).** ACI estimates each external determinant's energy contribution through a two-by-two interacting model and discards a tail whose *estimated* cumulative contribution stays below a chosen threshold. Expansion and coarse graining repeat to self-consistency. EGG adaptation: manage an aggregate missed-opportunity budget rather than retaining a fixed percentage everywhere. Its energy estimate is model-specific: summing marginal bus gains can double-count overlapping repairs or miss synergy. Treat the bus budget as calibrated diagnostic unless independent valid bounds justify it. Access: primary full-text HTML, equations (4)–(7) inspected.

**5. J. P. Coe (2018), [Machine Learning Configuration Interaction](https://arxiv.org/html/1808.05787), JCTC 14, 5739–5749, DOI [10.1021/acs.jctc.8b00849](https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.jctc.8b00849).** MLCI trains during selection using coefficients from the latest solves, retains rejected configurations for negative examples, and allows rejected choices to become important later. This is the closest learning architecture: retrain or update an EGG ranker after restoration, retain failed candidates with context, and periodically reconsider them. Labels should encode measured improvement or useful repair participation rather than permanently assigning every absent edge zero value. The paper's randomly split configuration validation is not evidence of transfer across unseen schedules. Access: primary full-text HTML, methods 2.2–2.5 inspected.

**6. Grimsley, Economou, Barnes, and Mayhall (2019; preprint 2018), [An adaptive variational algorithm for exact molecular simulations on a quantum computer](https://arxiv.org/html/1812.11173v2), Nature Communications 10, 3007, DOI [10.1038/s41467-019-10988-2](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-019-10988-2).** ADAPT-VQE scores an operator pool by current energy gradients, adds the largest-gradient operator, and reoptimizes all selected parameters. The paper also documents false gradient troughs and discusses cooperative operator selection. Borrow sequential state-dependent scoring, full reoptimization after expansion, and paired restoration as an explicit comparator. A small gradient in the tested pool certifies neither global optimality nor absence of valuable joint changes. Access: primary full-text HTML, algorithm and discussion inspected. CPU-side selection ideas are the point; quantum devices are unnecessary.

**7. Tang, Shkolnikov, Barron, Grimsley, Mayhall, Barnes, and Economou (2021; preprint 2019), [Qubit-ADAPT-VQE](https://arxiv.org/html/1911.10205), PRX Quantum 2, 020310, DOI [10.1103/PRXQuantum.2.020310](https://doi.org/10.1103/PRXQuantum.2.020310).** This paper constructs small complete operator pools and contrasts them with incomplete pools that can converge in gradient while missing the ground state. Borrow the question: which structural properties must the candidate generator preserve for it to remain capable of reaching good solutions? EGG might preserve trip coverage, depot connectivity, and alternative charging windows, then test harder counterexamples. Its Lie-algebra completeness results apply to specified real quantum-state constructions, not graph reachability or integer plan optimization. Access: publisher metadata and primary full-text HTML; pool-reduction section inspected.

**8. Stein and Reiher (2016), [Automated Selection of Active Orbital Spaces](https://arxiv.org/html/1602.03835), JCTC 12, 1760–1771, DOI [10.1021/acs.jctc.6b00156](https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.jctc.6b00156).** Cheap exploratory DMRG calculations expose orbital entanglement, with threshold plateaus guiding selection; the selected calculation is checked against exploratory patterns. Borrow exploratory breadth and stability plots: trace retained alternatives versus score threshold, compare selection after reoptimization, and expand when every candidate appears important. The paper finds that a mutual-information threshold can select an insufficient space, so a generic appeal to pairwise correlation is not enough. Molecular thresholds and entanglement measures cannot be copied numerically to EGG. Access: primary full-text HTML; threshold diagrams and selection protocol inspected.

**9. Bergeler, Simm, Proppe, and Reiher (2015), [Heuristics-Guided Exploration of Reaction Mechanisms](https://arxiv.org/html/1509.03120), JCTC 11, 5712–5722, DOI [10.1021/acs.jctc.5b00866](https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.jctc.5b00866).** Heuristics generate candidate reactive complexes; quantum chemistry optimizes intermediates and searches connecting transition states. Its reaction-network cutoff explicitly depends on temperature and timescale. EGG adaptation: enumerate promising repairs cheaply, validate physical charging behavior, and state the operational regime under which pruning is calibrated. A locally plausible connection is not a complete network; a physically replayed incumbent does not audit absent routes. This is a workflow analogy rather than an optimality theorem. Access: primary full-text HTML, network construction and cutoff discussion inspected.

**10. Nie, Liu, Yu, Wang, and Yang (2026), [Learning to Rank for Selected Configuration Interaction](https://arxiv.org/html/2605.10348), JCTC 22, 8797–8812, DOI [10.1021/acs.jctc.6c00937](https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.jctc.6c00937).** Published online August 20, 2026, before this review's cutoff. RCI argues that isolated coefficient regression/classification mismatches a selection task, and instead learns pairwise determinant rankings with a Transformer. This is especially useful prior art: EGG should compare a ranking loss against edge classification at matched candidate count and total CPU time, using decision-quality labels and schedule-level held-out splits. A better ordering can improve search without producing calibrated probability or a certificate. Paper-reported chemistry speedups must not be projected onto routing. Access: primary publisher metadata/abstract and arXiv full-text HTML; the latter was opened, with abstract-level claims used here.

## Ranked tools, assumptions, and certification boundary

1. **Adaptive restoration and decision-aligned ranking** (MLCI + ASCI + RCI): highest immediate value. Recompute importance after every expansion; keep a small exploration quota and several generator plans. These are adaptations, not inherited accuracy guarantees.
2. **Separate construction from audit** (HCI + SHCI): keep cheap feasible-plan discovery and missed-opportunity assessment as distinct outputs. Sampling formulas transfer literally only for a defined finite population, known nonzero inclusion probabilities, and the stated independence or sampling design. Estimated marginal repair gains do not sum to a global gap.
3. **Coordinated expansion and coverage** (ADAPT + active spaces): reserve budget for pairs or bundles of complementary choices and underrepresented trip/charger regimes. Diversity quotas are a proposed EGG design, not a theorem reported by these papers.
4. **Threshold stability and uncertainty propagation** (ACI + reaction networks): inspect performance over thresholds and perturb operational parameters; distinguish score calibration, physical-model error, sampling error, and omitted-space error.

The directly reusable bound is `z* <= z(S)` for a feasible restricted incumbent under the *same original objective and constraints*. Physical replay establishes feasibility only to the fidelity and tolerances of that replay. A solver bound for the restricted MILP need not lower-bound the unrestricted optimum. Valid full-space LP/convex-relaxation bounds require the full option universe or an exact separation/pricing argument, correct treatment of coupling, and valid relaxations. Even exact absence of negative reduced-cost columns certifies the continuous master under its assumptions, not integer global optimality. SCI perturbation estimates, extrapolated energies, and pool-gradient stopping rules supply none of the missing bus assumptions.

## Two small CPU experiment designs; no runs performed

**A. Recoverability under a fixed budget.** On a small public/synthetic instance whose full candidate universe can be solved or exhaustively audited, deliberately remove known useful choices. Compare static top-k, random restoration, current-state ranking, and ranking plus paired/diverse restoration at equal *total* CPU time including generation, training, solves, and replay. Keep bus count and within-fixed-fleet energy/tariff performance separate. Measure time to first feasible plan, bus-count recovery, objective conditional on bus count, restoration count, replay failures, and the unrestricted reference gap. Use paired instances with an isolated valuable edge and a jointly useful two-choice repair; the latter tests the proposed scientific mechanism. Multiple seeds and held-out schedules are necessary. A win only in bus count supports fleet search, not tariff learning.

**B. Does the audit predict missed improvement?** For the same small instances, partition omitted repairs into an exhaustively evaluated high-score stratum and a uniformly sampled tail. Compare reported tail estimates/intervals with exhaustive finite-population quantities, then measure actual improvement after jointly adding the audited repairs and reoptimizing. Track cases where estimated marginal gains disagree with joint improvement. Repeat under a modest parameter perturbation to consumption or charging availability. Success would justify an uncertainty-aware *audit diagnostic*; a persistent mismatch would reject the additive-correction analogy. A genuine certificate remains a separate full-space relaxation/pricing project.

For a long-term FT50-oriented contribution, the promising claim is a general decision-focused method for allocating scarce search and audit effort under shared nonlinear resources, supported by falsifiable mechanisms and operational robustness. Chemistry establishes that this architectural problem is mature elsewhere; it increases the burden to show what is new in EGG's interaction-aware decisions and guarantees.
