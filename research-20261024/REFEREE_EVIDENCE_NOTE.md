## Dates and interpretation limits

- `doc/MEASUREMENT_RESULTS.md:3` dates the synthetic measurement closeout to **2026-08-16**; its pilot addendum is explicitly **2026-08-17** at line 385. The full-population B2 closeout is identified by the **20260818** artifact stamp in `doc/RESEARCH_STATUS.md:68–70,83–85`; the preserved rolling handoff is dated **2026-08-18** at line 36.
- `ref/RESEARCH_DIRECTIONS.md:3,198–211` is an **August 14** hypotheses/priority record. It cannot by itself establish the present research agenda. The top of `doc/RESEARCH_STATUS.md:3–6` is a **September 27** snapshot of `codex/journal-research-20260927`, draft PR56, and explicitly says the historical material is preserved, not newly audited. The checked checkout currently reports branch `codex/ml-prediction-20261017`; that branch name does not date or validate the August experiments.
- The measurement document explicitly limits its findings to a synthetic laboratory (`doc/MEASUREMENT_RESULTS.md:20–23,185–199`): one generator, two terminals/one depot, simplified physics, no charger contention/network/V2G/reserves. The B2 size statement should not be generalized to later ML/replay work at 40/80/120 trips. This check did not inspect the current checkpoint or evaluate that newer direction.
- Detection definitions at `doc/MEASUREMENT_RESULTS.md:27–33` require price-state reproduction within `1e-4` for a fixed point, exact price-state recurrence at lag ≥2 for a cycle, and classify `max_iters` as unresolved. The baseline is a constant-damping family with nine specified alphas, with A0 its `alpha=1` member (lines 216–224). These passages do not specify sufficiently whether the reported fixed-point residual is the strict undamped price-response residual or the damped state-update residual; no equivalence is asserted here. The observed 0/176 is not a finite-enumeration proof that fixed points do not exist. “Cycles everywhere” also overstates a population containing unresolved transients and, at moderate feedback, observed fixed points.
- These sources substantiate a historical negative result for the specified stabilization variants and a historical welfare comparison under the laboratory assumptions. They do not establish present project quality, current novelty, general scalability, or external validity. No current literature review, model rederivation, or independent precision/replay verification was performed here.

Recommended corrected phrasing: “On the August 2026, 64-instance matched synthetic B2 comparison (`n=8/12`, `T=28`), A3–A5 reduced clean pricing calls but used more total oracle calls and higher median corrected solver wall than A2. Separately, the earlier measurement campaign found no fixed points in 176 strong-feedback (`b=.05`) algorithm-parameter cells, including unresolved transients; moderate feedback retained some fixed points.”
