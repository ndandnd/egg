Static preparation only. No actual receipt, table, row, plan, charge, load, cost or label was opened or evaluated by the implementing agent. No code, project import, hash, test, replay or solver ran. The new `run.py` copies the relevant bounded admission/streaming pattern into this namespace; it neither edits nor calls the old audit main.

The only data inputs are the literal completed metadata/support receipts and the two literal TRAIN tables/pins. Driver/PROTOCOL/LAUNCH and exactly `egglab/__init__.py`, `native_recharge.py`, `native_hull.py`, `physical_learning_cases.py` are bound once inside the metered batch. Only that small pure project closure is imported after its bytes are bound, with module paths checked. No dataset/learner/feature adapter, generator, decoder, compiler, optimizer, QP, weights, shard or referenced file is opened.

The same80 FIT groups,79 intended pairs,159 expected witnesses, source1-only10037 group and fixed48 differing/31 identical membership strata are reconciled from the completed receipts. FIT stored case dataclasses/defaults, market identity, full original-plan hash and membership are checked. No positive economic result is published before both original table pins/stability checks pass. Missing, duplicate, malformed and invalid records remain visible without replacement.

The unchanged `nr.replay_native(case, ORIGINALplan)` is called once per identity-admitted witness. Its required stored load/ops assertions stay unmodified, but valuations use only returned recomputed load and ops. Separate source_replay and cached raw/nested diagnostics are not interpreted. Charges and path fields are consumed only by the unchanged replay, except the already admitted vehicle/movement paths needed for fleet and movement-leg-minute components. Physical rejections retain their exception category/message; no clip, repair, fallback witness, completion solve or tolerance change exists.

For each valid pair the one recomputed operations scalar is converted with `nh.rational` and reused in both validated source markets. Four totals are EXACT Fractions `nh.rational(c_j)+nh.supply(market_t,L_j)`, without a float-total downcast. Linear/quadratic electricity uses the same represented-input conversions. Fleet cost is rational vehicle coefficient times fleet count; movement-leg minutes follow the replay's vehicle/movement/leg traversal and numeric duration definition, with rational deadhead coefficient times that sum. Any residual against sequential replay ops or the component total is reported, never forced to close or used as a new guard. The singleton contributes replay/intrinsic bookkeeping only, no paired valuation.

All nine signs use exact D=F(P1)−F(P0), positive favoring P0, and separate cost-unit tau=1e−6 with inclusive tie endpoints. Own-context flags permit overlap; raw R0=max(0,−D0), R1=max(0,D1) are not tau-excesses. Exact individual Ebar/normalized gaps are optional and have no epsilon floor. Normalized aggregate means are intentionally omitted to avoid enormous mixed denominators; raw Fraction distributions, group means/medians and all nine counts are present in the initial receipt, both full79 and fixed48/31 strata. No effect, physical-relaxation or model-training gate is introduced.

Output includes all80 statuses and compact replay/identity/failure counts, component scalars and pair metrics, never raw charge/load/ID vectors, plans or SOC trajectories. Body270CPU/540wall and total300CPU/600wall, one thread and observed5GiB apply to hashing/imports/replays/reporting/exit. Exclusive fresh attempt01 preserves failed/incomplete receipts and charges; no automatic retry. Parent freezes protocol/field/input/source/command after the one static review. Catalog preference is not a full-space optimum, causal teacher-noise diagnosis, routing-versus-charging isolation, information value, generalization or training readiness.
