## The positive result is a restricted-catalog statement

With `D_t=F_t(P1)−F_t(P0)`, positive means P0 is cheaper. Every pair has D0 above
the fixed positive reporting threshold and D1 below its negative counterpart:
P0 wins at source0 and P1 wins at source1. Thus each own-context witness is the
strict preferred member of its **two observed whole-schedule catalog** at its
own source tariff, under the selected replay and represented-value objective.
The simple failure mode “the other already observed witness is cheaper than its
own-context label” is absent from this catalog. This is stronger evidence of
economic coherence than membership variation alone.

that each selected movement is uniquely useful. Zero R means no improvement
