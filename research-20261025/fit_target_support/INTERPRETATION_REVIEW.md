## What the target summaries say

Of the79 admitted pairs,48 have different binary movement-membership targets
and31 are identical. The same48/31 classification holds for the direct/depot
flexible subset. This establishes observed within-physical-case target variation
under the bound source contexts in this stored FIT bank. It does not establish
optimal tariff response, signal predictability or useful learned performance.

All four aggregate distributions have planned79, defined79 and missing0. Their
admitted-group means therefore coincide with their reported complete-planned
means. These are means of **group-level fractions**, not one ratio pooled over
all edges or79 independent trained/evaluated models. The31 identical pairs stay
in these summaries; they are not omitted to magnify variation.

| Scope | Hamming denominator | Reported median Hamming | Jaccard-disagreement denominator | Reported median disagreement |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| All movements | Full case movement universe | `1/173` | Union of positive IDs | `2/17` |
| Flexible direct/depot | Flexible case universe | `1/160` | Union of flexible positive IDs | `2/13` |

Hamming counts a symmetric difference against all available IDs, including many
negative edges. Jaccard disagreement counts the difference against the selected
positive union. They answer different questions. The flexible median is not the
mean: use the receipt's separate exact mean fields when discussing average
variation rather than relabeling `1/160` or `2/13` as averages. Removing boundaries
changes both the relevant universe and positive union; the matching48/31 pair
classification does not establish that boundary membership never changes in a
changed pair. Nor does it make full/flexible fractional differences interchangeable.

No aggregate all/flexible ratio is undefined here. Individual movement-kind
records can legitimately have an empty positive union and null Jaccard
disagreement; that is absence of a positive-membership denominator, not a zero
fraction, invalid teacher or new missing physical pair. Preserve those meanings
when presenting by-kind examples.

## Same physical case and equal fleet counts are narrower than causality

The paired records bind a common physical case/grid to different source a and
equal b through stored market identity checks. All admitted pairs report equal
source vehicle counts. Thus the observed membership differences do not require
a difference in the number of vehicle records. This is informative compared with
an unqualified variable-fleet contrast, but it does not fix vehicle-to-trip
assignment, route topology, deadheading, energy demand, charging schedule or
integrated cost. None of those cost/physical mechanisms was measured here.

Single feasible-incumbent teachers may differ because of price, search path,
initialization, finite budgets or incumbent quality. This receipt neither
attributes variation to teacher noise nor rules noise out; equal fleet counts
cannot make that attribution. Identity binding does not independently replay
physical feasibility, verify all historical source execution or certify teacher
optimality. Conversely,31 identical binary targets do not show that charging,
cost or optimal actions are identical under the two markets.
