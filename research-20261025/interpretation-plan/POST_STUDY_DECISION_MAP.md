# Post-study decision map

Prospective interpretation note, 25 October 2026. Prepared from the current
`warm_replication/PROTOCOL.md` only, without reading campaign outcomes. This note
changes no criterion, source, model, resource provision or current assignment.
Every proposed experiment below requires a separate prospective decision; none
is an automatic job, retry, quota change or training authorization.

## Fixed decision rules and claim boundaries

Keep all 16 priors, 384 assignments, eight arms and eight base groups visible.
For each learned family separately, continuation requires the complete protocol
prerequisites and all 48 timely, physically admitted, native-OPTIMAL QP candidates.
Against **each** of CoverCA1 and fixed_route_QP, require at least six of eight
strictly positive complete group medians and the complete 48-pair mean
`d=(control integrated cost − learned integrated cost)/E0 > .01`. E0 is the common
prior's late electricity cost. Missing or zero E0 is undefined. CatBoost remains
secondary; it cannot replace a failed primary GNN result. Do not rescue a gate
with available-only means, provisional QP points or retained-prior QP endpoints.

This is eight new development units, with sizes and seeds repeated within them.
It is neither 48 independent replications nor a 12-group pooled confirmatory test
with the four earlier development groups. A gate pass is a direction diagnostic,
not significance or promotion. Report integrated system costs, their operating,
deadhead and electricity components, grid energy and loss tails. E0-normalized
total-cost gain is neither a percentage reduction in comparator total system cost nor
necessarily energy savings. Any bill-relative or energy-relative percentage must
name its own denominator; fixed fleet does not fix deadheading or demand.

The objective is operating plus integrated supply cost, not the atomic
uniform-price electricity payment. Historical field names containing “bill”
do not authorize a claim about actual private payments, profits or market welfare.

Use the following two levels. First assign exactly one policy verdict A–C, or
“qualified gate pass without the C caveat.” Then describe the aggregate
conditioning pattern through D/E, retaining mixed or unclear results as such.
F is a **pair-level overlay** on either pattern, not a mutually exclusive
aggregate verdict. A conditioning observation never overrides A/B, and a
favorable E observation never erases C. Where several concerns occur, the
earliest unresolved issue determines the **one** next step; do not launch the
menu in parallel.

## Policy verdict

**A. Incomplete study, invalid admission/accounting, or any unqualified QP control.**
Claim only the recorded operational outcomes and failure rates, with planned and
defined denominators. Concede that the strong-control comparison and learned
continuation are inconclusive, even if available GNN costs look favorable. A
valid provisional candidate remains a witness, not a qualified optimum. Minimal
diagnostic: identify the first blocking acquisition, physical, numerical,
timeliness or accounting condition from compact receipts and distinguish it from
later undispatched cells. **Next one experiment:** only if the failure is
understood and repair is scientifically worthwhile, a separately specified
reproduction of that failed component on its fixed input, with unchanged physical
admission standards. It does not complete or retroactively pass this study. The
publication-critical gap is a complete competent-control comparison; expanding
the cohort or model family cannot supply it.

**B. Complete qualified study, but GNN fails either strong-comparator gate.**
State which comparator/criterion fails, include losses and fallbacks, and concede
that the prespecified evidence does not support continuing the primary GNN policy
under this fixed-fleet clock. A CatBoost pass is a separate secondary finding.
Minimal diagnostic: decompose the paired deficits into deadhead, electricity,
grid demand and failure/fallback contributions; do not select a favorable size,
seed or group to reverse the verdict. **Next one experiment:** if a substantive
route-versus-charging ambiguity remains, a bounded common-QP evaluation of the
already admitted learned and classical route topologies, prospectively covering
the complete paired set. This can diagnose where policy losses arise; it cannot
repair the original operational result. Without such an unresolved mechanism,
stop this learned-policy expansion. Missing evidence is an advantage over strong
classical policies, not another pruning benchmark.

**C. Formal GNN gate passes, but CA1, random or full ties or outperforms it.**
Report the formal pass exactly while naming the stronger or equally effective
reported alternative. Do not declare global learned superiority: the gate has
two comparators, not every possible policy. Exact ties and the observed pattern
of differences are descriptive; lack of a separation is not a newly invented
equivalence test. Minimal diagnostic: retain all comparator pairs and assess
whether common Bw leaves ranking room, whether selected pools differ, and whether
fallback/search completion explains costs. **Next step:** absent an identified
ambiguity, concede the lack of incremental value and stop ML expansion. Only a
specific scoring/search-budget ambiguity justifies one crossover that changes
identification, for example a fixed native-work budget with separately reported
paid scoring, common target and start. Repeating the unchanged clock comparison
does not answer that question. Any work-limit/API implementation would need
future verification; none is asserted available here. The publication-critical
gap is learning's incremental value over the simpler policy, not generic learned
pruning novelty.

## Conditioning verdict, conditional on the policy evidence above

**D. GNN beats routing controls, but GNN_day ties or wins.**
The fixed GNN policy may be useful; this study does not show that changing its
input from day to target a improves replanning. The existing `|d|<=1e-6` convention
is descriptive only, not an equivalence or promotion threshold. Minimal
diagnostic: inspect all 48 contrast pairs, eight group summaries, protected Bw,
ranking room and missing-pool counts. **Next step:** first establish data readiness
for the comparably trained aware/blind comparison in a matched-blind design note:
available admissible groups/labels, leakage-safe grouped splits, matched model
capacity, fit/selection budgets and evaluation policy. No training is automatic.
If that design is feasible and worthwhile, its one subsequent experiment compares
matched trained aware/blind models, rather than another collection of inference
perturbations. Constant/permuted inputs would test sensitivity, not the missing
trained-information claim. If room is exhausted, this study does not identify
price sensitivity; do not enlarge K retrospectively. Publication lacks evidence
that target conditioning helps, and day-at-inference is not trained blindness.

**E. Target-conditioned GNN wins and its retained pools differ from GNN_day.**
Describe favorable fixed-model inference-time conditioning under this policy,
with its full distribution and loss tails. Different pools establish exposure to
the intervention; they do not by themselves establish that the pool change caused
the gain. Minimal diagnostic: report the full 48-pair overlap and room summaries
alongside cost, demand, completion and fallback contrasts. Full-pool Jaccard
includes common protected Bw. **Next step:** first establish the same matched
trained-aware/blind data/design readiness described in D; a favorable inference
contrast does not supply it. A frozen-pool crossover is justified only if a
specific unresolved scoring/search-timing ambiguity must be isolated, not as a
required repeat of the current comparison. Select that diagnostic instead of,
not in addition to, another automatic run. Publication still lacks independent
cohort/shock transfer and a comparably trained blind model before a claim about
information learned during training. The present models observe a, not b.

**F. Pools are identical but costs differ.**
Apply this overlay to each such pair within D/E rather than interpreting its
difference as a candidate-pool benefit. Report mixed identical/different-pool patterns without
using an available-only subset to recalculate a gate. Minimal diagnostic: verify
the recorded exact flexible IDs and common contract, then contrast paid scoring
time, search/completion, admission and fallback. Current construction preserves
original movement order, so identical pools supply no set-adaptation mechanism;
remaining differences concern runtime/search variation, not a demonstrated pool
benefit. **Next one experiment, only if that ambiguity matters to the claim:** a
predeclared same-pool, same-construction solver control with scoring cost reported
separately and a future-verified common native-work provision. Otherwise narrow
the interpretation without another run. Publication-critical missing evidence is
mechanism identification and reproducibility; favorable cost alone is not a
learned-information result.

## What even the strongest path leaves open

No branch establishes a novel pruning theorem, optimality of full routing, market
equilibrium, a realistic distribution of tariff shocks, or performance after an
economically optimal day schedule. The common day prior is fleet-first; the QP
control optimizes charging only on its fixed topology. Complete-policy backends
and call structures differ. The two sizes have scaled resources, and benefits
may reflect demand changes as well as scheduling. Declared TRAIN base-ID
nonoverlap is narrower than global alias/generator independence. Broader claims
require independently designed evidence, not relabeling these eight development
groups as a final test. A decision to stop, narrow the claim or prioritize a
classical method is a valid scientific outcome.
