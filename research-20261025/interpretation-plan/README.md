# Conditional interpretation package prepared during the frozen study

25 October2026. These notes were prepared while warm_replication/attempt01 was
running, without reading its partial costs, labels or outcomes. They alter no
current source, asset, case assignment, budget, threshold or analysis command.
The numerical criteria were already frozen before launch in commitbc54492f;
these notes are neither external preregistration nor new promotion rules.

- POST_STUDY_DECISION_MAP.md states what to concede under incomplete controls,
  negative comparisons, dominant simple alternatives or absent price-conditioning
  evidence. It selects at most one justified next study rather than an automatic
  architecture/ablation expansion.
- RESTRICTION_SEARCH_INFORMATION.md separates restriction regret, finite-budget
  search error and information attribution. Its algebra is classical and its
  optima are unobserved; it supplies no new theorem or exact certificate.
- MATCHED_TARIFF_BLIND_DESIGN.md describes a possible later matched training
  experiment, conditional on policy evidence and price-varying data readiness.
  It authorizes no training, labels, confirmation access or resource commitment.

First collect the completed owner/receipts/meter and run the existing frozen
finalizer once under its allowance. Read all group, failure and control
denominators before using this map. A formal continuation flag is not a finding
of superiority over every control, trained information value or publication
readiness. Concede a useful classical solution if the evidence favors it.

Economic interpretation remains integrated system cost c+a'L+.5L'BL, not atomic
payment c+a'L+L'BL. Current groups are development, with nested sizes and repeated
seeds. No sealed/private data, new numerical work, literature survey, model fit,
inference, source tests, hashes or solver calls were used for this package.
