## Scientific stopping boundary

A power restriction shrinks an individual ideal fixed-route feasible set, but
adaptive advantage need not grow. A weak/absent resource interaction, a change
explained by energy/operations rather than opportunity, or competent conventional
recourse resolving the question are legitimate reasons to narrow or stop the
learning motivation. Algorithmic construction/qualification failure instead
makes the associated comparison unresolved; it cannot identify physical cause.
Both useful operational gains and failure modes remain conditional on the fixed
policy, clock and exposed cases. No positive ML narrative, novel theorem,
independent generalization, new fit, cap search, full79 expansion or automatic
job follows. Parent can proceed to the narrowly scoped implementation and one
focused review/freeze without repeating the design exercise.
