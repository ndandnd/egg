# Interpretation of the completed fixed-topology charging pilot

25 October 2026. Read only the compact `readout-stdout.txt`, subsequently completed
`economic-export-stdout.txt`/`ECONOMIC_EXPORT.json`, and frozen protocol/numerical
qualification contract. No original tables, metadata, plans, loads, raw worker
results or labels were opened; no new calculations, conversions, hashes, tests,
inference, replay or solves ran. Values below are reported point/display results,
not independently recalculated optima or error bounds.

## What qualified

The compact view reports owner passed, complete accounting, no configuration/
resource failure and no failures. All32 planned cells were attempted, parent-
accepted and QP-qualified; none was undispatched. Each of eight groups has four
qualified cells, covering both original topologies under both source tariffs.
The cohort deliberately contains six changed-membership groups and two
same-membership controls, spanning20/28 services. These are selected exposed FIT
diagnostics, not eight random new test units or a prevalence estimate over79
pairs. Do not pool them with the separate warm-study development groups.

Qualification is meaningful: the prescribed numerical/native/physical/source/
path/timing checks passed according to the compact report. It does not mean a
justified objective-gap bound was obtained. Every target comparison still says
`mathematical_route_ordering=UNRESOLVED` and
`optimization_gap_bound_available=false`. Native OPTIMAL and source-point
agreement cannot silently override those frozen limitations.

The later economic export reports native status2 and passing source guards in
all32, zero candidate/fallback discrepancy flags,30 direct candidate canonical
comparisons and two source-guarded route inferences with fallback retained.
Importantly, `native_bound_null=0`: attempted native bound attributes were finite
in all32, not unsupported/null at runtime. Their presence does not establish a
rigorous bound on physical objectives or an optimization-gap interval. The two
bound names are attempted reads of the same attribute, not independent evidence.

value; it shows why fixed charging witnesses were an insufficient route-target
justification. Group10018 is a specific remaining numerical routing-contrast
lead, not population-wide confirmation of useful price-sensitive routing.

## What the two same-route controls contribute

Groups10006 and10005 have verified identical canonical ordered routes and return
zero point differences at each tariff, whereas their original fixed schedules
had strict own-context reversals. Identical canonical routes define the same
ideal fixed-route feasible set/objective at a given tariff; this structural fact
does not depend on an empirical tie threshold. The zero returned points are
consistent with that control, without certifying the common optimal value.

These controls substantiate the scheduling confound in the original catalog:
tariff-dependent preferences between two full charging witnesses can be present
without different route topology. Once charging is reoptimized, those old
witness preferences need not persist. The changed-route results suggest this
mechanism is relevant beyond the identical-route controls, but the pilot does
not isolate every cause of differences among the historical imperfect teachers.
It neither diagnoses teacher noise nor rules it out.

## Next scientific direction and stopping point

The substantive question is **when route flexibility changes useful charging
recourse**, not whether another student can reproduce more membership labels.
For a fixed topology, its available charging opportunities and physical resources
determine a feasible aggregate-load set and its energy requirement; the priced
charging problem then values that set together with route operating cost.
Different movement memberships need not produce appreciably different valued
opportunities. Conversely, equal returned costs at these two tariffs do not prove
that two feasible-load sets are identical or that one dominates the other. Any
identity/dominance finding would be a classical structural diagnostic, not a
new theorem or learned contribution.

The next bounded step should be **static source-defined opportunity/resource
structure**, with no new rows or solves, to formulate one falsifiable mechanism.
For example: routing has little additional value when common charging access
and shared resources wash out the routes' opportunity differences, but may matter
when route-dependent access intersects a binding resource. This is a hypothesis,
not a fact established by the current compact output. The source analysis must
identify both its prediction and a condition that would falsify it; it must not
infer load-set equality from small point gaps.

The earliest controlled numerical experiment, only after that design is frozen,
would vary **one** justified resource axis on a small prespecified physical/
route/tariff comparison: retain a baseline and a structurally predicted binding
condition, keep other physics/objectives fixed, and retain infeasibility/failures
without repair. Selection cannot simply target the pilot's most favorable
observed route contrast. Include a competent classical routing policy followed
by direct charging optimization and the charging-only fixed-route control, so
any route-flexibility claim survives a strong simple alternative. Separate
changes in grid-energy requirement from timing/opportunity value; use qualified
physical costs and label numerical uncertainty rather than borrowing
OptimalityTol as an objective-gap bound.

This would study a classical operational mechanism first. It is not permission
to search resource regimes until ML wins, to refit, relabel or run all79 pairs.
Any later execution requires its own reviewed source, deterministic selection,
admission and practical budget; no manifest, threshold or job is supplied here.
Nothing here supports trained-price-information causality, generalization or a
model promotion. The pilot makes the membership-learning story harder: membership
targets can differ without appreciable separation of the reoptimized route points. That
is a reason to narrow or stop this ML-information direction unless the structural
and economic question supplies independent scientific value. It is not proof
that every routing distinction is useless. The warm practical-margin failure
remains unchanged.
