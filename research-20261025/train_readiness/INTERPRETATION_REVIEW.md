## Concessions that remain after a clean audit

`unknowns=[]` records no unexpected projected value shape. It is not the same as
“no scientific unknowns.” The explicit `readiness_limits` still leave actual
within-case prices, conditional binary target variation, teacher quality,
candidate-bank/search truth, alias closure and matched-blind readiness unknown.
Do not hide those named limits behind the checks-passed flag.

The static source contract supplies intended source0/source1 tariff definitions
and labels movement membership in a single replayed feasible incumbent. It does
not establish that all other feasible movements are bad, that the incumbent is
globally optimal, or that differences between two teachers are caused only by
tariff. Availability of two sources establishes an opportunity to examine paired
labels; it does not establish that their price inputs or their targets differ.
Likewise, varying prices alone would not establish conditional label signal.
No interpretation here restores the completed warm study's failed practical
margin or establishes a learned-information claim.

## Minimum FIT-only facts needed before a fitting decision

A separately specified, bounded inquiry would need to establish the following
facts on the fixed declared FIT groups, preserving the79 paired/one incomplete
availability pattern and reporting all undefined cases. This is a statement of
missing evidence, not authorization for a read or a new quantitative gate.

1. **Actual within-case intervention:** explicit physical/case identity and a/b
   identity for each available source pair. Confirm which physical inputs and
   movement universe remain common and what tariff actually changes; do not
   substitute source names or generator defaults for observed provenance.
2. **Conditional target variation:** align admitted teacher membership labels on
   that common movement universe and report which paired FIT groups have equal
   versus different binary target vectors, with missing/unalignable cases kept
   separate. Summarize at physical-group level; movement rows are not new
   independent units. A changed label vector is descriptive support, not proof
   of an optimal tariff response or future student benefit.
3. **Teacher comparability:** establish the admitted incumbent's objective,
   status/failure/provisional flags, feasible-label lineage and available quality
   evidence, together with candidate-bank/search policy, budgets and native
   seed provenance. Distinguish genuine target changes from teacher-search
   variability or unequal quality. Do not infer missing gaps or optimality from
   the mere existence of a selected plan.
4. **A usable matched design:** close the selected GNN pool/task lineage and
   specify a blind intervention that removes current-case tariff channels and
   derived leakage while preserving shared labels, grouped splits, weighting,
   fit-only preprocessing rules, capacity, seeds and selection/compute policy.
   Both students may share historically price-influenced supervision and the
   same target-price optimizer; that is current-input blindness, not historical
   price-free training. The day-scored existing GNN is not this trained control.

These facts should precede a decision whether one matched trained-aware/blind
experiment is scientifically justified. They do not call for automatic label
generation, a collection of perturbation runs, refitting or cohort expansion.
The defensible present conclusion is **declared paired FIT support largely
exists, but conditional target signal and full trained-model provenance remain
unestablished by this metadata-only audit**.
