# Post-result scientific critique

**Decision: replicate the conditional warm-replanning result on new groups and add a frozen day-scored GNN pool before training a tariff-blind model.** Retain every existing result; this is a research-direction decision, not model promotion or a result-driven refit.

The frozen exploratory gate passed for GNN: mean paired integrated-cost advantage was **1.2318882% of prior late-tariff electricity cost E0 versus CoverCA1**, and **12.0116748% of E0 versus fixed_route**, with positive medians in all four groups for both. CatBoost failed against CoverCA1. All 168 endpoints were admitted; 167 met the stricter method-success definition. The remaining CatBoost cell retained a valid improved round despite a later timeout. These are total integrated-cost differences normalized by E0, not percentage reductions in total cost or electricity savings alone. Evidence: `COMPACT_RESULTS.json`, continuation, denominator and non-successful-cell fields; interpretation: `PROTOCOL.md`.

What is identified is a **conditional warm fixed-fleet operational comparison**: from a common valid day prior, frozen GNN ranking plus the shared original-physics optimizer obtained lower target integrated costs under the measured 60-second policy on these development groups. Prior protection, common quota, warm tangents, replay and complete accounting address trivial prior loss and failure exclusion. The fixed_route contrast motivates route adaptation beyond charging an existing topology; it does not isolate learning or tariff conditioning. Beating full under a short search budget concerns search effectiveness, not full-space optimality.

The strongest paper threat is the **small gain over a serious ordinary control and the narrow benchmark**. Four base-group units are not 24 independent timetables; sizes are nested and resource power scales. GNN–CoverCA1 group-median d values are 0.003558, 0.004047, 0.008149 and 0.034623: positive but heterogeneous. Passing 1% is weaker evidence than a stable improvement distribution. Report paired wins/ties/losses, zero or negligible improvements, loss tails and electricity/deadhead decomposition. Endpoint acceptance does not establish useful improvement in every cell. Adjacent synthetic IDs, one intervention, solver variation and quota-induced search effects could defeat broader utility claims. No market, equilibrium, cold-start, unrestricted-optimum or generalization claim follows.

Next freeze **eight new base groups**, both sizes, two native seeds and four arms: target-scored frozen GNN, **day-scored frozen GNN with its pool fixed during target optimization**, CoverCA1 and fixed_route. This means 16 prior acquisitions and 128 decisions; propose five CPU/six wall hours, subject to parent approval. Preserve physics, fleet, prior protection, K, all failures and 60-second admission. Charge both scoring paths inside their windows; both optimizers see the late objective. Freeze IDs/comparisons before generation, without replacement or extensions. This tests replication and inference-time tariff conditioning using the same weights. It does **not** replace comparably selected tariff-blind training for a later training-information claim. If replication or the day-pool contrast is negligible, accept that before new training; generic GNN novelty is not the objective.
