## Material design conclusions

1. **Use one uniform strong control, not a retrospective best-of-methods bank.**
   New fixed_route_QP assignments should all use the replication-local Method0
   helper, same prior topology, original physics and60/52 clock. Only the new
   seed guard broadens to frozen17/41/73 with actual set/readback; each assignment
   is fresh. Preserve older17-only helper sources. Do not splice seven old v2
   witnesses and the later Method0 witness into the new evaluation or reuse a
   single QP solution across seeds. Raw OPTIMAL/ObjVal without physical admission
   is never a feasible comparator; provisional and fallback statuses stay visible.
2. **The primary contrast is end-to-end operational policy value.** Restricted
   arms retain common Bw/original K and an already viable prior; direct QP fixes
   that topology and optimizes charging with a specialized backend. It is a strong
   conventional control under the same paid clock, but backend/procedure differs
   from the routing arms. Keep all components/energy and QP success/admission
   counts. Parent has prospectively required all48 QP decisions to be timely,
   physically admitted QP candidates with native OPTIMAL. If any fails, retain
   every operational contrast but mark the control gate false and learned
   continuation inconclusive. Neither a retained-prior endpoint nor raw optimum
   satisfies this requirement. Do not erase failures by switching to an
   available-only estimand, retrying a control or claiming route adaptation
   beyond optimal charging. This rule is fixed before new-cohort outcomes and
   does not revise the original four-group gate.
3. **GNN_day narrows the mechanism, but does not establish learned information
   value.** Fresh day-a versus target-a scoring with identical frozen weights,
   preprocessing, prior protection and target objective tests inference-time
   conditioning. A null contrast may arise because Bw consumes exposure or both
   pools are identical; record fill/overlap before interpreting it. Both learned
   scorers lack b. Fixed fleet still permits changed deadhead and energy demand;
   neither normalized total-cost gains nor lower grid energy alone isolate
   price-sensitive charging timing. Trained tariff-blind and further resource/
   curvature interventions remain separate later questions, not permission to fit.
4. **Freeze the entire grouped readout, including negative conclusions.** Eight
   bases,16 variants,48 repeated case/seed blocks and384 assignments remain fixed.
   The GNN gate against both CoverCA1 and strong QP is >=6/8 positive complete
   group medians plus full48-pair mean d>.01 for each, with all prior/attempt/
   admission/accounting/fatal and all48 strong-QP qualifications. CatBoost stays secondary and
   separately reported; all other controls remain. A dominant ordinary CA1 or
   full/random control cannot be hidden because it is outside the formal gate.
   Eight deterministic development groups support a diagnostic next decision,
   not significance, practical generalization or an acceptance forecast.
5. **Reconcile prospective metadata before any command.** The files read still
   name capped `fixed_route`, and the old cohort prose says all arms share tangent
   rounds; that is incompatible with the new specialized QP arm. The earlier
   post-result critique suggested four arms/two seeds, whereas the parent's
   current choice explicitly retains eight arms/three seeds. Treat those as
   preserved historical proposals, not simultaneous executable rules. The new
   draft specifies the current choice; parent/source owner must update or
   supersede every operative manifest/control label and finalizer gate together.
