# Neural restart: a distinct conditional hypothesis, with a concrete next gate

The human asked to revive useful neural prediction and diagnose the price–route
chicken-and-egg problem. The old pruning policies keep their frozen negative
results. The reopened hypothesis is that a price-and-dual-conditioned proposer
can supply valuable feasible route columns sooner than competent conventional
pricing, while an optimization master and classical correction retain control
of feasibility and stopping. This is a new target, not a relabeling of the old
gate or an authorized architecture sweep.

Before a new fit, complete these in order:

1. **Close the nonlinear economic interface.** The actual adjacent oracle now
   works at linear posted prices. Specify a minimal retained-column master or
   outer approximation for the original institutional objective, with the
   correct marginal values, shared station constraints and physical realization.
   Use system gradient a+bU+bL or strategic marginal outlay a+bU+2bL as appropriate.
   A naive price-taking best response at the current clearing price is not the
   strategic optimizer. Keep grid/continuous objectives and convex relaxation/
   physical integer incumbent scopes separate. Resolve the grid-versus-realized
   selection objective and include conventional physical-cost tie-breaking or
   charging completion before learning from ambiguous grid-optimal labels.
   Do not change source tolerances
   or hide a fractional mixture as a schedule.
2. **Measure the candidate bottleneck and target support on a predeclared grouped
   development workload.** Select synthetic model-consistent timetables and price/
   resource conditions before outcomes, with fixed station semantics and all
   failures retained. Record existing pricing/master/build times, repeated or
   redundant columns and delivered feasible costs. This one six-trip fixture is
   far too cheap to support acceleration. Do not select scales or engineer
   scarcity to manufacture a learning advantage. No protected data or new operator contract is required
   merely to study an explicitly documented synthetic benchmark.
3. **Only then collect and train on decision-valued proposals.** Supervision
   should recognize multiple valuable alternatives at an actual master state,
   including trip/plug/fleet/terminal duals and institution-correct price inputs.
   It should not punish an equally useful route for disagreeing with one chosen
   incumbent. Preserve label provenance, unresolved teachers and grouped split
   identities; fit/preprocess/select only in training/inner groups.

The first learned comparison must include the existing classical pricer and its
stabilization, cached/reused columns, cheap deterministic/random proposals and
the capacity-aware fixed-duty control. Give methods a common admitted incumbent
when the API permits it; otherwise explicitly use matched cold acquisition.
Pay model load/features/inference, graph construction, search, validation and
publication. Report feasible objective versus time, missing/failure rates and
oracle work saved; classification accuracy is not the endpoint. A comparably
trained price-blind control is required for a claim about price-information
value, with price-derived graph/normalizer inputs removed before encoding.

Proposed columns require unchanged physical/coverage/capacity admission. Exact
pricing at stopping, or a justified oracle-error bound, remains necessary for
the corresponding pricing certificate; a learned confidence score is neither.
Retaining all useful columns and a true-cost incumbent prevents discarding known
good alternatives, but does not by itself prove global integer convergence.

Stop or redirect this specific acceleration hypothesis if representative
conventional pricing remains cheap, useful new proposals are absent, or inclusive
benefit disappears against these controls. That is not a universal neural no-go.
The immediate next work is the narrow nonlinear-interface design in item1,
reusing existing EGG objective/tangent code and this callable adjacent oracle.
No new dataset, solve, fit or benchmark launch is frozen by this note: each next
bounded package needs its own exact inputs/source/command/resource decision.
