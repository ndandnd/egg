## Exact objective and station-to-market map

For column j, retain its fixed **realized** station-hour grid-energy vector \(e_j\), nonenergy coefficient \(c_j\), coverage, fleet contribution, terminal energy and occupancy. Declare a dimensionless linear energy map \(A\) from registered station-hour coordinates to market-period energy: \(L=A\sum_j e_j\lambda_j\). A may sum stations within the same market hour, preserve separate station commodities, or coarsen aligned energy periods; none is implicit. Finer temporal projection needs the actual witness blocks upstream, not an arbitrary split of a stored hourly energy total. Do not average kWh into kW or pool physical station capacities when choosing A. Declare matching \(a,b,U\) in that market basis, \(b\ge0\), and optional fixed station-hour adder vector \(\sigma\). Source-defined hourly posted tariffs must be decomposed prospectively into the market part and adders, or the first nonlinear design must explicitly choose its own synthetic coefficients. They must not be counted again inside c.

Let \(\alpha=a+bU\). For the original institution, use

\[
\begin{aligned}
F_{sys}(\lambda)&=\sum_j(c_j+\sigma^Te_j)\lambda_j+\alpha^TL+\tfrac12\sum_t b_tL_t^2,\\
F_{strat}(\lambda)&=\sum_j(c_j+\sigma^Te_j)\lambda_j+\alpha^TL+\sum_t b_tL_t^2.
\end{aligned}
\]

System objective is incremental supply cost, excluding the constant background-only cost. Strategic objective is the atomic fleet's uniform-price payment. The corresponding market marginal values are \(g_{sys}=a+bU+bL\) and \(g_{strat}=a+bU+2bL\); clearing price is \(a+b(U+L)\). Clearing price is therefore **not** the strategic marginal outlay. Posted-price comparison holds the quoted vector fixed.
