# Economic route recourse: smallest competent API composition

Static design only. Read the scientific decision and narrow `resource_recourse.classical.route` / `warm_replication.qp_control.solve` source bodies. No case/spec/row/plan values, outcomes or models were opened; no imports, hashes, tests, numerical work or native calls were run. No old files changed. This note authorizes no implementation or experiment.

**Recommendation for the intended strong conventional test:** one new research-local **direct convex MIQP composition**, retaining the already target-QP-valid common incumbent externally. It directly optimizes economic routing and charging over the full declared graph at exact N, avoids pre-polish screening and needs only one native search window. Reuse the existing matrix-copy/objective/extraction machinery, with a narrowly reviewed integer-type/exact-N/admission delta; do not modify the old QP helper or repeat its full identity audit. Per-candidate polishing is the smaller change to qualified primitives, but its two tangent-generated routes and additional solves make it a less decisive negative test. It remains a valid fallback design if the MIQP compatibility delta cannot be coherently qualified.

## Concrete defect and reuse boundary

Current `route(case,a,b,fleet_N,*,prior_plan,first_native_end,routing_end,check,ledger,record)` initializes a global keeper with the prior, extracts/replays each tangent-MIP candidate, values its **current charging schedule**, and retains it only when that bill improves `best_bill`. It returns only that keeper; packets expose metadata rather than every candidate plan [source](/Users/nadan/Documents/ChatGPT/egg/ml-prediction-20261017/research-20261025/resource_recourse/classical.py:250).

For a candidate route `r`, its unpolished feasible bill is an **upper**, not lower, bound on `V_r=min_charging F`. Thus `F(raw candidate)>F(common incumbent)` cannot justify discarding `r`: its polished value could be lower. This is a source-level possibility, not evidence it occurred in the closed study. Improving only the final `route()` return cannot fix it.

The common incumbent must already be independently replay-valid for the **same current case, target a/b and N**, with its target charging-QP qualification recorded. Such qualification is numerical/physical admission, not proof of an exact charging optimum. Any needed acquisition plus target QP is separately paid and shared explicitly; an uncharged zero-price acquisition is not an equivalent starting control. If that preparation fails, keep the assigned comparison missing rather than inventing a target baseline or reopening a failed resource cohort.

## Thin per-candidate polish composition

Keep old sources immutable. A new campaign-local routing loop can reuse the frozen builder/call/extract/tangent pieces underlying `classical.route`; it needs a callback at the point **after independent candidate replay and before `if bill<best_bill`**. Required policy deltas are:

1. Maintain an external durable global keeper initialized with the target-charged common plan. Admit only same-case/exact-N physical witnesses; rank by exact represented integrated cost. Keep any valid raw improvement operationally, but never replace the keeper with a worse candidate merely to polish it.
2. For every distinct admitted candidate topology, call unchanged `qp_control.solve(case,a,b,candidate_plan,17,absolute_native_end,absolute_admission_end,check,record)`. **The QP entry fallback must be that candidate's own replay-valid schedule.** Passing the common plan would fix and polish the common topology instead. Its QP fallback bill and the global incumbent bill are different quantities.
3. Compare the callback's replayed retained value with the global keeper, including when the candidate was worse before polishing. Preserve raw-candidate, fresh-QP-candidate, QP-retained and global-retained values/flags separately. No raw-cost pruning is valid unless an independently justified fixed-route lower bound rules out improvement; the present helper supplies no such generally supported bound.
4. Canonical duplicate-route handling is a prospective within-decision policy only: compare complete ordered trip/movement path multisets at the same case/target/N, not movement counts. A common/previously polished topology may be skipped under a frozen no-duplicate rule, with the skip recorded; this is not proof that a repeated numerical solve could never improve it. Do not create a free cross-decision cache.
5. Freeze whether the next tangent uses the callback's retained physical load; using that load is the natural target-recourse update. Preserve each admitted route/polish result if a later stage fails. Never rank a raw point as a qualified charging optimum, and keep ordinary search/QP failure separate from physical fallback availability and fatal contract failures.

The QP API already keeps the better of its entry schedule and an admitted new physical solution, exposing `candidate_bill_exact`, `fallback_bill_exact`, `true_bill_exact`, `used_fallback`, status/quality/copy flags and failures [entry](/Users/nadan/Documents/ChatGPT/egg/ml-prediction-20261017/research-20261025/warm_replication/qp_control.py:85), [retention](/Users/nadan/Documents/ChatGPT/egg/ml-prediction-20261017/research-20261025/warm_replication/qp_control.py:399). Its retained plan alone is not always the fresh candidate. Preserve existing direct comparison/source-guarded unique-path inference semantics; no decoder, physical tolerance or helper telemetry change is required.

**Clock consequence:** at most two routing MIPs plus at most two candidate QPs, rather than two MIPs and one final QP. The old17.5/35 routing cutoffs followed by one52-second QP cannot simply absorb callbacks for free. A future protocol must reserve absolute MIP/extraction/polish slots inside the selected parent-before-Popen endpoint (or justify a different total), account for cold imports/build/copy/publication, and refuse a stage without its remaining provision. Do not reset clocks inside callbacks or run a redundant final QP on the winning already polished route. Retaining the global incumbent survives a late/failed polish; late improvements are not admitted. No exact slot allocation or new budget is selected here.

## Direct convex MIQP alternative

The natural stronger control optimizes the original full declared movement graph with exact `sum(pullout)=N` and objective `ops+a·L+½ΣbL²` in one convex MIQP (`b≥0`). It removes tangent candidate selection and pre-polish ranking entirely. The existing source is a useful matrix/extraction template, **not an unchanged callable MIQP**:

- Current QP `solve` fixes every routing binary to prior IDs [source](/Users/nadan/Documents/ChatGPT/egg/ml-prediction-20261017/research-20261025/warm_replication/qp_control.py:143). The copy then requires those binary bounds to be singleton and maps every copied variable to CONTINUOUS [source](/Users/nadan/Documents/ChatGPT/egg/ml-prediction-20261017/research-20261025/warm_replication/qp_control.py:190).
- A new MIQP composition would retain free routing binaries as binaries, retain continuous physical variables, add/check the exact-N row, and adapt type/bounds readback. Linear rows, energy/SOC/resources and quadratic cost remain original. It would reuse the original-index value view and unchanged extraction/replay, but replace fixed-prior-ID admission with original-case full-route/exact-N admission [current guards](/Users/nadan/Documents/ChatGPT/egg/ml-prediction-20261017/research-20261025/warm_replication/qp_control.py:355).
- External incumbent retention is straightforward; feeding a useful Gurobi MIP start is a separate missing API path. These two functions do not translate a whole physical plan into all copied start variables. A binary-only hint must not be represented as a processed feasible start. If a native start is adopted, its actual translation/processing status needs narrow source/primitive qualification; no such work is performed here.
- Native MIQP status/bound interpretation and appropriate numerical settings need their own prospective delta qualification. Current Method0 continuous-QP qualification and nullable bound fields do not automatically certify MIQP behavior. One full-graph numerical bound would still concern this declared graph/current physics/fixed N, not unrestricted EVSP. A time-limit incumbent remains a feasible point only after unchanged physical admission.

The smallest concrete new callable can mirror `solve(case,a,b,common_plan,seed,native_deadline,admission_deadline,check,record)` with explicit required `fleet_N`, checked against the common plan. Keep the fallback admitted/durable before building, then perform exactly one original full-graph build/copy and one MIQP optimize. Reuse the current absolute native52/final60 endpoint pattern; imports, clone construction, start submission, search and extraction all remain paid. Do not add an LP/MIP feasibility phase or a final extra polish solve.

For a competent warm control, minimally submit a **partial binary route start** from the common incumbent through the already indexed routing map, leaving continuous variables unspecified. This is a new public-Gurobi start-field use needing narrow mapping/readback qualification, not a new physical decoder. It must be reported as partial/submitted, with actual solver processing unknown unless observed; it is not a full translated feasible start. A complete continuous start would require further interval/SOC translation absent from these APIs and is not part of the smallest recommendation. External fallback protection is mandatory regardless of start acceptance.

The essential future delta review is limited to: free binary preservation/readback; one exact-N row; unchanged convex objective and original constraints; partial-start mapping/claims; MIQP-specific integrality/gap settings and actual status/bound semantics; and arbitrary returned-route/exact-N replay admission instead of prior-ID equality. Do not transfer continuous OptimalityTol into an objective-gap guarantee or assume the old Method0 setting alone specifies the MIQP search. Freeze the actual integer/termination parameters prospectively, with no tuning/retry menu. Runtime, start usefulness and a meaningful gap are unknown. This is the cleaner economic optimization test, not a promise of speed or successful certification within an untested clock.

## Scientific decision and stopping limits

The direct MIQP is the smallest recommended **strong full-graph economic** test; the callback option removes the identified discard defect with the least new native machinery. Either can establish a positive retained economic improvement over a common target-charged plan while separating energy quantity, operations and charging timing. A callback null remains conditional on its two-candidate generator/reduced routing time; an MIQP null also remains unresolved without sufficiently informative, correctly scoped numerical bound/gap evidence. Neither statusOPTIMAL nor a source-point agreement screen alone establishes global interchangeability or absence of useful route value. Do not fill an unresolved economic conclusion with more training.

Keep all planned groups, acquisition failures, missing baselines and failed/late candidates. Do not select10006 or another favorable outcome as the evaluation cohort, retry the ideal-excluded45kW cases, alter fleet/capacity to rescue them, or pool away the learned-margin failure. Neither implementation option establishes learning necessity, a novel algorithm or publication readiness. Parent chooses a new prospective cohort/input authority, exact search/clock/qualification schedule and resource commitment before any implementation or numerical work.
