`all_mode_capacity/NEXT_SCIENTIFIC_DECISION.md`. No registry, original input,
plan, row, model, label or outcome was read, and no computation was performed.
This note grants no data authority, source modification, protocol, launch or

## Minimal falsifiable question

At the original declared resources and a fixed reference fleet, does a bounded
competent conventional route search reduce integrated target cost **beyond an
already target-QP-charged common feasible incumbent**, after paying for all
acquisition, charging and search? This is economic recourse, not whether routing
can restore feasibility after making a known schedule or topology impossible.
No new capacity restriction is needed for this first economic test.

Recommend a pilot of **four base groups, two target tariffs each**: eight paired
economic contexts. Two 20-service and two 28-service groups give a small check
across the existing generic size profiles without making tariff/resource copies
independent observations. Use one frozen native seed and the existing routing/QP
APIs and clocks. No learned arm, new controller, resource scan, retraining or
optimization certificate is required. This size is a diagnostic stopping point,
not sufficient evidence for population generalization or training investment.

The hypothesis is that fresh routing adds a practically useful target-cost
improvement after competent charging of the common topology. An admitted route
change without such an improvement fails to support that hypothesis. A zero
retained gain created by fallback only shows this bounded method did not deliver
an improvement; it is not proof that the intrinsic best-route gain is zero.

## Common incumbent and three endpoint types

Freeze the same reference fleet N across all methods in each group, defined from
the original source0 reference by the same source contract. N is not a proven
minimum or day-economic optimum. All physical constraints and target tariffs
remain unchanged. Any paid fresh reference acquisition must use the same
predeclared conventional constructor across groups; ordinary missing outcomes
cannot trigger an alternative constructor or extra call.

For each target context, keep these three endpoints distinct:

1. **Unchanged schedule:** reprice and independently admit the unchanged reference
   schedule at that target. This is an operational reference, not an optimized
   routing or charging control. Invalid/missing is reported, never repaired free.
2. **Charging-only/fixed route:** target-QP-optimize the admitted common topology,
   with physical replay and inclusive endpoint timing. Its retained valid result
   becomes the common target-charged incumbent **before** route search begins.
3. **Conventional route recourse:** start with that exact paid incumbent as a
   protected fallback. Use the existing full declared movement graph, same N,
   frozen classical search schedule and bounded final charging optimization.
   Compare every admitted final candidate against the protected incumbent by
   rederived integrated target cost; retain the incumbent on ties or failure.

The routing arm must preserve the target-charged incumbent throughout selection
and final retention. A larger search graph alone gives no dominance guarantee.
In particular, do not reproduce selection against a zero-price feasibility plan
followed by a single QP and call its fallback equivalent to fixed-route QP.
This design can retain one final candidate QP if that is the established API;
then it tests that bounded composition, not every candidate's best charging or
the ideal best route. Freeze the exact candidate-ranking/search schedule before
outcomes and report that limitation.

Pay reference construction, the common target QP, cold imports/builds, search,
extraction, independent physical admission, cleanup and final readout inside the
future total budget. Count a physically shared acquisition once in campaign
accounting, and disclose its latency/cost in each deployment interpretation.
Do not add overlapping child meters twice, subtract preprocessing as free, reset
the method clock between stages, or monetize compute into system cost without
an independently specified valuation. Parent must freeze stage clocks and the
complete package prospectively; this note does not allocate extra time.

## Denominators, failure and economic readout

Retain all four planned groups and all eight target contexts, including missing
priors, late packets, configuration failures and unresolved native calls.
Report acquisition availability, target-incumbent availability, attempted
searches, newly admitted candidates, timely endpoints, operational availability
and strict full-method success separately. A search failure can have a valid
operational fallback and zero retained gain; it remains a failed search. Failure
to obtain the target-charged common incumbent leaves the economic comparison
undefined. A cold feasibility witness may be reported operationally but cannot
be substituted into that economic denominator.

Primary cost is integrated system cost `ops + a'L + .5 L'BL`, not atomic payment.
The route increment is fixed-route target-QP cost minus retained route-recourse
cost. The charging increment is unchanged-schedule cost minus fixed-route
target-QP cost. Keep absolute cost primary. Any electricity normalization must
use one positive target-common-incumbent electricity denominator, fixed before
route outcomes and identical for that paired comparison; null/zero stays
undefined. Report its meaning beside total bill and energy, rather than calling
an electricity-normalized effect a bill percentage.

For each paired context report fleet/operations, deadhead cost and minutes,
movement and service energy, grid energy, linear and quadratic electricity,
period loads, and topology-changed versus unchanged. Same fleet does not fix
movement energy; more energy can buy better access or load spreading. A lower
quadratic component is not by itself proof of a cheap-window or causal timing
channel. These decompositions distinguish plausible explanations; they do not
identify an independent energy-versus-timing effect without a further controlled
design. Do not add such an ablation automatically.

Group summaries require both planned targets. An overall paired cost summary
requires all eight common-incumbent contexts and their operational endpoints;
otherwise it is undefined, with the planned denominator retained. Available
cell contrasts may be displayed descriptively with explicit missing counts, but
cannot pass a continuation rule. Endpoint cost and strict success denominators
must remain separate. Four group-level descriptions do not justify significance,
equivalence or noninferiority claims.

## Honest stopping before training

Freeze a scientifically justified practical cost margin and numerical reporting
policy **before** numerical input access. Do not inherit or lower the warm ML
gate, choose a threshold after these effects, or use tiny represented positives
as a go decision. If no practical margin can be justified, the pilot remains
descriptive and cannot authorize training. Exact fractions of represented values
and solver tolerances must not be relabeled exact physical gap certificates.

Stop the economic interpretation if common-incumbent/admission/accounting
prerequisites fail; preserve operational outcomes and do not rescue the gate by
available-only averages or replacement groups. With complete prerequisites,
no delivered improvement, only numerical-scale/tiny effects, or effects below
the frozen practical margin are a no-go for learning investment. Negative
retained gains beyond the declared numerical policy indicate a competence or
retention defect to report, not a favorable route-value result; no automatic
rerun repairs it. Strong heterogeneity is a limitation, not permission to select
winning groups or tune resources.

A small or negative delivered effect is not proof of equivalence or of no
possible routing benefit. It is sufficient reason to stop this proposed
learning direction under the tested operational conditions. Even a material
pilot improvement would only justify deciding whether a conventional mechanism
deserves independent confirmation; it would not establish an ML opportunity,
novel algorithm, trained-information effect or model promotion. The existing
learned-family no-go decisions remain intact. No new experiment follows
automatically from this design note.
