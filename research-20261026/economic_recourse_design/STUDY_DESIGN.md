# Prospective conventional economic-recourse pilot

Parent scientific decision, 26 October 2026. STATIC design only. No cohort IDs,
original data, plans or new outcomes have been opened or selected. This is not
an executable protocol, launch manifest, permission to read rows, or a refit.

## Question and scope

Test whether a competent bounded classical routing method delivers useful
integrated system-cost improvement beyond an already target-charged common
feasible incumbent. Preserve original physical resources and the source0 route's
exact fleet N. Do not introduce the failed 45-kW restriction, add buses, scan
tariffs/resources, or retry any prior case. This isolates an economic-recourse
question from the separately established feasibility-restoration mechanism.

Use four development base groups, two per declared 20/28-service profile, and
both unchanged source tariffs: eight target contexts. One native seed17. No
learned arm or fitted model is included. This is a competence/opportunity pilot,
not a scale claim, population test or recovery of the failed learning gate.

The cohort rule is the first two eligible numeric canonical FIT base IDs in each
profile after excluding the complete earlier selected topology/resource pilot
union {10001,10002,10018,10006,10009,10010,10015,10005}. All earlier pilot members
are excluded uniformly, not according to effects or solver statuses. Do not
exclude a selected group later because its reference is missing or difficult.
All FIT groups were already exposed in metadata/target/catalog audits; this rule
does not make them unseen or independent of training. The prior warm/density
synthetic development IDs are a separate declared family; no alias closure is
claimed. Exact metadata source/fields, prior-use union and selected identities
must be frozen before original plan access or outcomes. No identities are
materialized by this note; a missing profile/insufficient cohort stops selection
without replacement or changing the rule.

## Common incumbent and conventional method

Independently replay the unchanged source0 stored plan on its unchanged physical
case; use no cached admission or old cost as the endpoint. Reprice it under both
targets as the unchanged operational reference. For each target, run the existing
direct fixed-route Method0 QP once on that topology. A timely NEW physically
admitted candidate, native OPTIMAL and the existing copy/extraction/source guards
are prerequisites for the target-charged common incumbent. Retaining only the old
plan does not qualify that prerequisite. Preserve any failure or late result;
that context's economic-recourse comparison remains undefined, with no cold
substitution or additional acquisition.

After fresh-candidate qualification, the common incumbent is the QP helper's
retained endpoint U, with its returned physical plan independently admitted and
valued. U can be the better entry fallback B even when a fresh C qualified; expose
that fact, rather than equating U with C or the native point N. The helper need
not expose a discarded fresh candidate plan. Do not claim caller replay of an
unreturned C or use its raw native value as the common plan's feasible cost.

Give the routing method exactly that admitted target-QP endpoint as a protected
fallback before search. Select one direct convex MIQP on the full original
physical model with exact fleet N. API_NOTE.md supports a narrow generalization
of the existing quadratic copy and unchanged decoder/replay APIs; actual mixed-
integer compatibility is still unqualified. Preserve
integer routing types; do not fix them to the prior or relax them to continuous.
The operational fallback is distinct from any partial/full native MIP-start
processing. Unsupported starts do not justify an extra call. No final charging
QP is needed for a directly admitted quadratic candidate; there is only one
native optimize in the routing worker. This method selection requires focused
design review before static implementation; exact mixed-integer settings,
start-field mapping/readback, input identity and executable command still need
source-bound review and freeze before any data access or launch.

Submit only a partial binary routing start mapped from the common incumbent,
with continuous variables unspecified. It is a hint, not a translated full
physical start or proof of processing. Attribute/mapping failure is a preserved
contract failure, not permission for a second optimize or different method.
External fallback protection does not depend on native start acceptance.

The earlier two-tangent method discarded raw-charged candidates before their
topology was polished. Merely adding a stronger external fallback would leave
that selection problem. A per-candidate-polishing composition is an alternative
only if direct MIQP cannot be introduced narrowly; it must be a separately fixed
method decision, not a fallback selected after MIQP outcomes. No such alternative
is authorized here. No new monitoring, controller, source-identity
or full matrix audit framework is requested.

The routing method must retain the lowest independently admitted target cost
among its common fallback and new candidate, including on later failure. Record
native candidate, physical candidate, retained endpoint, canonical route change,
native-start processing, strict method success and operational availability
separately. A same-topology improvement beyond the existing source-agreement
envelope is a discrepancy to report, not evidence of economic routing value.
No optimization-gap or unrestricted certificate follows from finite native
bounds, OPTIMAL, parameter tolerances or source objective agreement.

## Readout and stopping

Keep all four groups, eight target-QP assignments and eight routing assignments,
including missing/unattempted states after a prerequisite or fatal failure.
Report every acquisition/strict method/physical/operational denominator. A group
mean needs both targets; the complete pilot mean needs all eight economic
contexts. Operational fallback can make delivered gain zero while the method
still fails. Do not replace missing comparisons with zero or promote an
available-only mean.

Report absolute integrated cost, fleet/operations, deadhead minutes/cost, service
and movement energy, grid energy, linear/quadratic electricity and period loads.
Show unchanged-to-charging and charging-to-routing increments separately. An
optional ratio uses the positive target common-incumbent electricity cost,
fixed before the route outcome. It is neither a bill percentage nor the old
warm E0 or catalog Ebar. Energy/timing accounting is not causal attribution;
same N does not imply same energy, and no extra energy-matching ablation follows.

Generator costs are not yet calibrated as real monetary stakes. Accordingly this
pilot is descriptive: no invented practical margin and no automatic training,
model promotion, broader cohort or publication claim. A negligible delivered
gain does not prove equivalence or absence of intrinsic route value. It does
remove the basis for further learning investment in this tested setting. Even
substantial route gains would support further conventional scientific design,
not establish a learning increment. Keep all earlier learned-family stopping
decisions and failures unchanged. No retry, method switch or outcome-selected
source adjustment is authorized.
