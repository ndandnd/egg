## Scientific disposition

This pilot repairs an important conventional-comparator weakness: it tests
direct joint economic recourse beyond an already target-charged, protected
common incumbent. It finds a nonzero conventional gain in one development
group, with a small complete-pilot mean and six zero/point-screen-sized delivered
increments. That supports neither a claim of universally zero routing value
nor a claim that routing, learning or another training target is necessary.
The time-limited pair and numerical certificate limits prevent equivalence or
intrinsic no-opportunity conclusions.

The evidence supports keeping ML training and scale expansion paused pending
a justified account of problem stakes, physical/economic realism and a valuable
decision target. More labels or model families do not turn a small conventional
increment into a learning opportunity. No lowering/pooling/rescue of the earlier
learned gate, favorable subgroup selection, new experiment, retry or automatic
job follows. All cost points, limits and separate prior stopping decisions remain
unchanged.
