## Concrete prospective adapter boundary

Recommend a **policy-explicit physical descriptor first**, separate from any solver or learned selector. Do not present the current NativeCase as a drop-in EVSP-DR instance loader.

Input contract: `source_binding` (project/revision/instance/base/service-day, source trip/station namespaces and immutable source reference); `time_basis` (origin, timezone/calendar convention, minutes, horizon, exact resolution); complete service records and directed transfer alternatives; explicit `coverage_mode`; `commitment` (`free_routes`, `fixed_trip_sequences`, or `fixed_movement_paths`, with ordered IDs); `fleet_policy` (`cap`, `exact_N`, or committed duties); per-vehicle initial/battery/reserve and terminal policy; efficiency; station-labelled powers/plugs/windows and any distinct shared-grid constraint. Keep tariff/objective institution in a separate field, not inferred from a physical witness or copied between projects. These are proposed fields, not an implemented or authorized data-read schema.

Output contract: `mapping_disposition` (`exact_declared_subset`, `deliberate_restriction`, `unsupported`), axis-by-axis reasons, unchanged source/day/base references and identifier mapping, retained and omitted constraint policies. Emit a typed `NativeCase` only for the representable subset, preserving values/order/defaults; give exact-N/fixed-sequence/fixed-path restrictions as separate explicit solver policies, because the base builder does not take those arguments. Return no claimed feasible witness from a source-only conversion. A later independently replayed output would distinguish service/commitment/fleet/SOC/resource checks and scope failures, rather than a single transferred “feasible” flag.

Current callable boundaries remain `nr.validate_case(case)`, `nr.replay_native(case,plan)` and `pf.build_feasible_model(case,backend)`; the builder returns model/compiled/x/SOC/charge/load/ops metadata, not a solved dispatch or generic station adapter ([return:244](/Users/nadan/Documents/ChatGPT/egg/ml-prediction-20261017/src/egglab/native_pathflow.py:244)). The plan uses schema/case identity, ordered vehicle paths, selected movement IDs and connector0 charge sessions; there is no station field that could rescue a missing station-resource mapping ([ownership:275](/Users/nadan/Documents/ChatGPT/egg/ml-prediction-20261017/src/egglab/native_recharge.py:275)). Reject unsupported policies rather than modify old inputs or weaken replay. A general multi-station/aggregate-terminal bridge would require a separately designed versioned schema/model; no such source change or execution is proposed here.

Physical correspondence also does not equate economics: the inspected EVSP-DR capacity path integrates exogenous station-time electricity prices with session costs and fleet stages; EGG's current pilot uses integrated quadratic supply cost. [EVSP-DR energy cost:197](/Users/nadan/Documents/projects/demandresponse/.codex-work/capacity-packed-20261001/src/capacity_event_pricer.py:197). This mapping corrects an overbroad problem-level stop while leaving prior EGG results, learning gates and runtime-equivalence claims unchanged.
