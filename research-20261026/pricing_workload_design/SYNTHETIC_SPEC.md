## Fixed cohort and prefix construction

Use base seeds **61001, 61002, 61003, 61004**, in that order. Each group has sizes **12 and 24 trips**: four duties, with the first three or all six waves respectively. Keep both size variants, all quote contexts and any later pricing snapshots from one base group together for development/selection. These are four synthetic group units, not eight independent instances. Deterministic seed streams describe construction; they do not themselves establish population independence or real-world representativeness.

Number wave `w=0..5`, duty `d=0..3`, trip `t=4*w+d`; duty d is `(d,4+d,8+d,...)`. For each wave, define `u(seed,w)` as the unsigned big-endian integer represented by the first eight bytes of SHA256 of UTF-8 `capacity-workload-v1|<seed>|<w>|start` (decimal integers, no spaces). Propose common-wave jitter `j=5*(u mod 3)` minutes. Set start `60+180*w+10*d+j`, duration **45 minutes**, service energy **40 kWh**. This hash recipe is a future generator specification; no hashes were evaluated here. Freeze the four seed-specific descriptors once, before solver outputs; a coincident descriptor is disclosed, not replaced.

All generation for a size is a projection of that group's full six-wave literal rule. Existing trip records and induced transfer tuples are unchanged when size grows; the larger universe adds only later trips/arcs. Boundary return/charging opportunities remain available at both sizes. Thus timetables and resources are nested/fixed, while each duty is lengthened; optimal routes, columns, bounds and discovery effort need not be nested. There is no fleet/resource scaling with size.

All four trips of every wave overlap: latest start is 30 minutes after earliest start and duration is45. This supplies a **four-bus service lower bound**, independent of charging; it is not a proof of a physically feasible four-bus assignment. Using 0/15/30/45 offsets would not provide that four-way witness. Keep service energy fixed40 in this first specification: unreviewed energy perturbations would combine a new feasibility perturbation with the size/quote question. Small timing jitter, wave position and station access already generate declared variation, though four groups may remain similar or cheap.

## Selected acquisition policy and admission limits

The parent selects a paid flat-quote actual-oracle A0 acquisition per group/size/restriction, as specified in STUDY_DESIGN.md. No hand-constructed charging witness or guaranteed-feasible eligibility filter is required. The four prescribed chains are topologically compatible; that is not proof of grid/SOC/shared-plug charging feasibility. Transfer energies2/4/7/9 and SOC step10 are not lattice-aligned, rounding can remove opportunities, and one-plug queues couple duties. A continuous energy balance or long gaps alone do not admit a schedule.

Before outcomes, materialize and bind the fixed descriptors/adjacency and verify domain, prefix, timing, duty partition, four-way overlap and horizon contracts. Detect canonical physical aliases after removing seed/administrative labels. Retain and group collisions without replacement; fewer than four distinct group clusters makes the continuation screen inconclusive. These checks occur inside a later frozen construction phase, not during this source-only design.

A0 is an assigned, fully paid search that may fail; no case is replaced or silently reconfigured. A valid selected fleet must pass unchanged grid/physical admission including joint plugs, station power, SOC/reserve, return and aggregate terminal energy before it can become the external target fallback. Missing A0 leaves the cached branch missing, while the fresh target search remains cold-eligible. If the common fleet is valid but the whole exported bank cannot be admitted, retain the fleet and mark cache selection unavailable. All48 planned A0/F1/C1 states remain in the final denominators. No repair, alternate witness search, seed replacement, resource/fleet relaxation or automatic retry follows. Ordinary search failure is not mathematical infeasibility.

This resolves the earlier optional constructive-witness dependency by a concrete acquisition policy; it does not claim any generated case is already physically qualified. The separate fixed/full acquisitions also prevent a shared union bank from masquerading as independent fixed-duty discovery.
