# Decision target: conventional escalation for service continuity

Static conceptual gate, 26 October 2026. Read the current continuation and the
closed economic pilot's RESULTS, NEXT_RESEARCH_DECISION and interpretation only.
No new data, metadata, plans, outcome JSON, source execution, calculation, model
or literature review was used. **No experiment is launched or authorized.**

**Decision: NO-GO for the current learned economic-pruning claim.** Meaningful
operational stakes and a valuable residual decision opportunity beyond competent
charging/conventional routing have not been established. Keep training and scale
expansion stopped. The conditional direction below is a candidate redirect, not
an established contribution or a request to keep designing until ML wins.

## One direction worth testing only if its operating stakes are credible

Redirect the proposed contribution from predicting teacher route membership to
**deciding when a committed service plan needs routing recourse after an
externally specified change in charging availability**. The decision is whether
to keep the routes and reschedule charging, or escalate to conventional joint
routing/charging while preserving the genuinely committed fleet and service.
The proposed result is an operational account of that escalation decision, not
a new optimization theorem, learned-pruning novelty or an asserted ML advantage.

This direction follows the surviving distinction in the closed evidence:
charging access can matter to feasibility, whereas incremental economic routing
value beyond competent target charging was small and concentrated in the new
pilot. It must not be built by selecting10006, retrying unresolved cases or
manufacturing a45-kW/large-curvature regime. Resource changes must come from a
credible operating decision or engineering requirement fixed independently of
method outcomes. Their frequency and material consequences are currently
unknown; the existing development witnesses do not establish them.

The falsifiable proposition is: **under those independently justified conditions,
routing recourse delivers timely, physically valid full-service plans that
competent charging-only control cannot deliver at the same committed fleet**.
This concerns feasibility restoration. A separate conditional accounting can
ask whether routing saves cost when both policies are feasible, but neither
endpoint may be substituted for the other.

## Stakes gate before another numerical study

First identify who makes the decision, when charging availability becomes known,
what service and fleet commitments can actually change, and whether the existing
physics represent that decision. Fixing N merely because a teacher used N buses
does not establish an operating commitment. A synthetic capacity reduction alone
does not establish an outage, congestion rate or credible service risk. If the
real decision permits spare vehicles, changed service or another charging site,
silently excluding those alternatives would overstate the value of routing.

Use integrated system cost consistently if the decision maker bears or optimizes
`ops + a'L + .5L'BL`. If the institution instead pays an atomic uniform-price
bill, it is a different economic question; do not import system-cost conclusions
into it. Time, power, interval energy, efficiency and battery quantities must have
consistent units. Any claim about economic stakes also requires defensible
units for a, b, fleet and deadhead coefficients. Current generator cost units
are not money. Do not assign an arbitrary missed-service penalty or invent an
effect threshold to combine feasibility and cost into one favorable score.

In the current source convention, L is grid-side **period kWh**, a has cost/kWh
units and b has cost/kWh² units. A physical average-power price slope beta would
require `b=beta/Delta_h` for interval duration Delta_h; changing time resolution
without that mapping changes the economics. The current expression is integrated
system procurement cost, not atomic endogenous uniform-price payment, and no
actual market institution has been established. The30-hour generator, vehicle
coefficient100 and duration coefficient0 are synthetic; exact N makes operations
cost constant in the tested routing comparison. Those conventions cannot support
a dollar threshold or an unqualified claim about a real price-making fleet.

Until these commitments, resource assumptions and objective institution are
credible, the gate is **stop: no further training or numerical scale expansion**.
This is an unresolved requirement now, not a claim that practical stakes exist.

## Value-aligned target and competent controls

The primary target is a decision outcome: timely, independently admitted delivery
of all committed service at the specified resources and fleet. It is not a binary
label for belonging to one incumbent route, a count of changed movements, or a
necessary-cut pass. A cut can reject an ideal fixed topology under its assumptions;
passing cannot certify a schedule. Full-universe ideal exclusion and numerical
native statuses retain their separate scopes.

The two essential controls are:

- **Competent charging-only:** independently optimize charging on the committed
  topology under the actual target resources, with all preparation and physical
  admission paid. Failure of an old timed charging schedule is insufficient.
- **Strong conventional joint recourse:** the declared full routing/charging
  problem at the same actual commitments and paid deadline, protecting a common
  target-valid incumbent whenever one exists. A native start hint is not a
  processed physical start; delivered physical feasibility and native status
  stay distinct. Missing valid charging-only incumbent is permitted in the
  restoration question, but it does not create a finite cost baseline.

Transparent opportunity/capacity checks may explain escalation candidates or
serve a cheap conventional screen. They must compete with, rather than replace,
the physical controls. No new predictor is needed to establish this basic value.
The known cuts and rerouting mechanisms are classical. An inexpensive exact
diagnostic must be a baseline, not automatically a training target for a
surrogate whose necessity or computational advantage has not been demonstrated.
Any later economic comparison where both policies are feasible must start from
the already target-charged paid common incumbent used in the stronger pilot.
All loading, construction, optimization, extraction, replay, failure, cleanup
and decision latency count; no free teacher acquisition or equal-budget speedup
claim from an incremental pipeline.

For future prediction, the relevant conditional target would be **delivered
decision value relative to those controls**, with an explicit uncertainty/missing
state. It cannot be manufactured from label disagreement or native objective
bounds. Economic value does not establish ML value: a simple conventional
screen or optimizer may already capture it. Trained-information questions only
become relevant after meaningful operational value and a credible information
set have been established; they are not authorization to refit now.

## Denominators and competing explanations

Any future evaluation must retain every prospectively assigned timetable and
resource condition, selected without inspecting feasibility, energy, cost or
method success. Group all tariff/resource/seed variants within their original
base timetable. Exposed FIT development evidence remains development; no final
generalization or population rate follows from the current four groups or
repeated conditions.

Report separately charging-only success, joint success, restoration witnesses,
neither-policy delivery, late/failed/unattempted states and unresolved statuses.
Do not count a passing cut as feasible or a missing plan as proven infeasible.
If both plans are valid, report the incremental integrated cost with fleet,
operations, movement/service/grid energy and linear/quadratic electricity.
Undefined cost comparisons remain undefined. Complete economic summaries require
their planned paired denominators; restoration counts cannot rescue them.

Competing explanations include insufficient total charging energy, individually
misplaced SOC opportunities, shared-window conflicts, an artificial fleet
restriction, incomplete conventional search, and ordinary convex load spreading.
Higher energy may coexist with better access; lower quadratic cost does not
identify a causal timing mechanism. Native time limits leave intrinsic
opportunity unresolved. These explanations need to remain distinguishable rather
than be relabeled one scarcity or tariff-learning mechanism.

## Stop or redirect

Stop this direction if credible commitments/resource conditions cannot be stated
without choosing favorable cases or tuning scarcity/curvature after outcomes.
If charging-only suffices under the justified conditions, or conventional
recourse/screening captures the useful benefit without prediction, concede that
result and redirect toward the relevant conventional operations problem rather
than add labels or model families. If complete, competent evaluation shows only
tiny delivered cost increments, do not treat represented positive points as an
ML investment case. Tiny/negative delivered effects are not equivalence or proof
of no intrinsic benefit; they can still justify stopping further investment.

Even a genuine restoration advantage would establish only a bounded operational
contribution worth assessing for realism and replication. It would not establish
necessity of a learned method, information causality, novelty or publication
readiness. Earlier learned-family no-go decisions remain unchanged. No favorable
subgroup, new promotion rule, automatic experiment, model fit or scale expansion
follows from this memo.
