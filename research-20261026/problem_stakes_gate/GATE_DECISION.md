## What the evidence permits us to concede

The eight-group warm study did not meet its frozen practical continuation margin
for either learned family. A positive median in seven groups cannot replace the
failed mean-margin rule. The later four-group classical pilot found a complete
mean delivered routing increment of .18127943 generator units. The mean of the
eight paired `(F_QP-F_retained)/E_QP` ratios was .10624451%, using each target's
common-incumbent electricity cost, after the much larger mean unchanged-to-QP
improvement of 18.62186109 units. Only one group exceeded point-agreement screens;
the time-limited fourth group leaves intrinsic opportunity unresolved. These
separate cohorts must not be pooled to rescue a claim. The normalization is not
money, total-bill percentage or proof of equivalence.

Neither different incumbent labels nor coherent repricing of two full schedules
shows that their routes have valuable different recourse after charging. The
stored-schedule reversals did not reliably survive competent charging in the
small topology pilot. Software qualification, replay, sensible classical cuts
and a conventional convex-cost energy tradeoff remain useful research assets;
they do not supply the missing novelty or learning increment. Do not market
generic pruning/recovery/convex-hull pricing as a new contribution.

## Fix the meaning before enlarging the model

The implemented objective is

\[
 c(r)+\sum_t\{a_t L_t+\tfrac12 b_t L_t^2\},
\]

with grid-side period **energy** \(L_t\), not instantaneous power. It is
consistent with a synthetic coordinated system/procurement cost. It is not the
atomic fleet's payment \(\sum_t(a_t+b_tL_t)L_t\) under endogenous uniform prices.
No actual bids, settlement, other participants or payment transfers are
established by the source payload. Use the system-cost interpretation for the
closed experiments. A price-maker market paper would need a separately specified
institution and welfare/payment model; changing a coefficient or renaming `bill`
would not settle that scientific choice. Do not retrospectively reinterpret
historical fields or recompute their results.

The narrow source review also establishes an important scaling constraint. For
an average-power marginal-cost curve \(m(P)=\alpha+\beta P\) over a period of
duration \(\Delta_h\), its period-energy objective has
\(a=\alpha\) and \(b=\beta/\Delta_h\). Thus increasing the number of time slots
at unchanged \(b\) need not preserve the represented physical economics. A
finer time grid also does not, by itself, make this period-energy objective the
integral of instantaneous squared charging power. More slots could make an
algorithmic test larger while changing its economic question. No scale study
is justified by the referee's predicted stabilization speedup.

Source prices, curvature, vehicle coefficient and zero movement-time coefficient
have interpretable dimensions but no validated monetary horizon or market
calibration. At exact fleet N with zero time coefficient, operations are constant;
the economic pilot principally measures energy/timing tradeoffs. Model battery
upper bounds differ from some nameplate values, and the field named deadhead
duration includes stationary movement-leg waiting. These conventions must be
declared before real operating stakes are assigned. Do not invent labor,
degradation, demand-charge or service-loss values to make effects look important.
The source-supported details and limits are in `UNITS_AND_INSTITUTION.md`.

## One falsifiable operational direction

Ask: **for externally specified charging restrictions, can a structure-aware
conventional policy identify and recover service-preserving duty reassignments
that charging-only adaptation cannot deliver at the same fleet?** Keep the
operator's decision and the constraint change separate from an endogenous market
or learned price-response story. Resource changes must come from an operationally
justified specification chosen before outcomes, not a search over caps or tariffs
until a gap appears. The old 45-kW variants stay closed.

The existing 10006 witness motivates this question: additional daytime access
coexisted with greater energy and restored a replay-valid endpoint, while the
reference violated a necessary ideal capacity condition. That is one exposed
development example, not proof of population frequency, native-precision
infeasibility, a new theorem, or learning necessity. Conversely, the all-mode
exclusions show why some fixed-N problems cannot be rescued by another route.
Their ideal mathematical scope and the old native labels remain unchanged.

"Cannot deliver" in a bounded operational comparison means no admitted endpoint
within the paid budget; it does not establish physical impossibility. A separate
ideal exclusion under explicit assumptions supports only its stated stronger
mathematical scope, not a new native-precision certificate.

The competing explanations are operationally distinct: a reference may only
need a new charging schedule; a route change may mainly reduce energy; it may
change which vehicle can charge before a reserve deficit; or no declared route
may support service at N. Time-limit/no-incumbent results can leave any of those
unresolved. An algorithm must distinguish what it knows from what it failed to
find, not label every solver failure infeasible.

For a future design, cheap necessary-capacity screens and charging-only QP are
mandatory conventional controls. A failed necessary cut is an ideal-model
exclusion under its explicit assumptions; passing is UNKNOWN, not feasible.
Replay supplies feasible witnesses separately. Use a competent full physical
route-recourse method and existing viable operational references, with every
acquisition and search paid. Where a target-QP common incumbent exists, protect
that incumbent and report added cost value. Where it does not exist, cost regret
against a fictional baseline is undefined: report restoration, exclusion,
unresolved status and service consequences separately. Do not average unlike
states into a favorable cost number.

The value-aligned object is **retention of a feasible useful recourse option
under a stated online budget**, not membership in one teacher route. Any future
restricted pool must be judged by downstream physical delivery and opportunity
lost versus competent conventional controls. Cheap exact diagnostic quantities
are baselines; learning an approximation to them has no demonstrated purpose.
An expensive decision-value target would require bounded teacher uncertainty,
independently grouped support, fixed effort across regimes, and all failure and
missing denominators. It is not permission to generate new labels now.

## Admission and stop conditions

Before another numerical study, require a concrete decision maker and economic
interpretation, consistent units/horizon, an external justification for the
operational restrictions and consequences, and a reason these consequences
matter despite competent charging. A later design must fix the cohort and
resource conditions without favorable-case selection; all variants of a base
timetable stay together. Keep original physical admission and all failed, late,
missing and unresolved outcomes. Report independent-group counts and complete
operational denominators before conditional cost summaries. Declare a meaningful
decision-value/precision rule from the stated stakes, not from the observed
.106% or the favorable 10017/10006 examples.

If realistic stakes cannot be supported, stop this claimed application rather
than optimize a synthetic headline. If competent conventional recourse handles
the meaningful cases within the operational budget, stop the learning claim.
If bounds and failures leave opportunity unresolved, report that uncertainty;
it does not justify automatically fitting models or extending hard cases. Only
after material residual opportunity exists would a matched trained blind control
and a new, fully specified learning target become relevant. Existing a-only
models and price-derived encoders cannot establish curvature information value.

**Disposition:** present economic-learning/price-maker claims are NO-GO for
expansion; conditional classical operational recourse is the sole candidate
redirect. Institution and externally justified stakes remain prerequisites, not
completed findings. No new data, native call, cohort, parameter axis, teacher
solve, fit, full-79 expansion, sealed source, cluster task or manuscript follows
from this gate. A future package needs its own prospective scope and review.
