# V2G and grid-pricing novelty: bounded primary-source check

27 October 2026. Five close papers; targeted source reading, not exhaustive novelty clearance. Local scope: only `PRIOR_ART.md`, `ECONOMIC_INTERFACE.md`, and narrow source excerpts defining charging/load, objectives, and fixed-duty semantics. No historical results, data, models, numerical runs, imports, or hash inspection.

**Finding:** “Nobody covers V2G or connects electric-bus scheduling to electrical engineering/grid pricing” is not defensible. Both connections have direct prior art. This does not establish that EGG's exact discrete scheduling problem and computational method have already been solved. Those narrower claims require an explicit comparison.

## Five closest checks

| Primary source | What is already covered | Distinction that survives this check |
|---|---|---|
| [Fei, Sun, Iacobucci & Schmöcker, TRC 2023, 104060](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0968090X23000499) | Charging, actual discharging to the grid, and bus service allocation; market-sale and frequency-reserve contracts. Methodology uses time-dependent service frequency and connected-bus counts at an aggregate strategic level, with observed time-varying prices. | Aggregate frequency/allocation is different from assigning every fixed timetabled trip to individual feasible vehicle duties. Observed prices do not establish endogenous market clearing. Bus V2G itself is occupied. |
| [Son, Im & Kim, C&IE 2025, 111169](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0360835225003158) | Two-stage stochastic bus dispatch and depot charging/discharging across routes and depots, with uncertain discharge prices and energy consumption. | Publisher text describes a depot-level LP and ToU/scenario prices. It does not establish EGG-style binary trip chaining or an endogenous network-clearing price. Full equations were not accessible; do not assert those features absent solely from the abstract. |
| [Blatiak, Bellizio, Badesa & Strbac, SEGAN 2022, 100738](https://arxiv.org/html/2204.11565v1) | MILP jointly chooses commercial-EV trip start times and charging/V2G balancing-service availability. Section 2 explicitly includes charge and discharge capability and prices known before optimization. | These are delivery/maintenance fleets with given trip length/distance and movable starts, not fixed bus-trip assignment. Crucially, the experiments set energy-sale price to zero: V2G reserve capability is not demonstrated energy-arbitrage export. Prices are exogenous to the fleet optimization, even where derived from a future-system model. |
| [Najafi et al., TRD 2025, 104664, institutional full text](https://backend.orbit.dtu.dk/ws/portalfiles/portal/391952206/1-s2.0-S1361920925000744-main.pdf) | Fixed timetabled service trips with connection decisions, bus/charger planning, charging, renewables/storage, and physical distribution-network constraints. Sections 3.2–3.5 cover trip connections and network coupling. | This reaches beyond charging a predetermined fleet schedule. Electricity price is an input; a physical PDN model is not automatically endogenous price formation. Charging/discharging of stationary BES must not be counted as bus V2G merely because the introduction discusses bus export. |
| [Wu, Guo, Polak & Strbac, Applied Energy 2019, 113798](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0306261919314850) | Publisher abstract explicitly couples bus service planning and electricity-market clearing in bilevel optimization, using distribution locational marginal prices for congestion management. | This directly defeats a broad “first bus–grid endogenous-pricing connection” claim. Abstract-level evidence does not settle equivalence to individual timetabled-trip assignment or establish bidirectional bus export. Inspect full formulation before claiming an exact residual gap. |

Access depth: full HTML for Blatiak; institutional PDF/formulation excerpts for Najafi; publisher abstracts/methodology snippets for Fei, Son, and Wu. Direct publisher opens sometimes returned 403, while indexed primary publisher text remained available. No secondary review is used as proof.

## What the inspected EGG paths implement

`evsp.py:235–251,305–312` defines nonnegative charging energy and `L` as its sum; its opening docstring explicitly calls V2G a later extension. `native_pathflow.py:182–193,239–241` likewise uses nonnegative charge variables and nonnegative aggregate consumption. These inspected paths provide flexible consumption, **not bidirectional export**. “Discharge” through traction energy use is not export to the grid.

`market.py:1–16,41–55` makes the fleet price depend on aggregate consumption through `p=a+b(U+L)`. It distinguishes posted-price payment, strategic uniform-price payment, and integrated system cost. `regimes.py:116–153` implements the latter two objectives separately. `native_hull.py:105–109` uses integrated affine-supply cost; the economic-interface note gives the coefficient transformations for alternative institutions. This is endogenous reduced-form aggregate pricing, not a physical OPF, nodal congestion model, generator auction, or general grid-clearing implementation.

`evsp.solve_fixed_sequences` retains ordered trip duties while optimizing direct/depot movement choices and charging. That differs from fixing every movement path. Neither flexible consumption nor this distinction supplies a V2G contribution.

## Defensible positioning

An accurate present description is **discrete electric-bus duty/movement optimization with shared charging resources and explicit aggregate price feedback**. A potential contribution is a computational method for recovering jointly useful integral schedules under that coupling, with institution-correct evaluation and a paid search budget. Its novelty must survive the learning/repair prior art already recorded in `PRIOR_ART.md` and competent conventional recovery comparisons. The application connection alone cannot supply an ICML contribution. Adding genuine V2G would require export variables, battery/export physics and settlement rules, and would still need differentiation from the papers above. The next conventional-recovery experiment remains useful without assuming either blanket novelty or blanket duplication.
