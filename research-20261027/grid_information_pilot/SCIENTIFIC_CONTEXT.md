# Why a new test, and what old ML failures do not establish

The old GNN/CatBoost operational advantages over CoverCA1 were small under their frozen policies. They did not establish sufficient model capacity, successful optimization, informative supervision, or absence of learnable grid information. Source audit identifies a width32/two-layer GNN with ceiling18000parameters, capped300epochs, and no small-data memorization control. CatBoost depth4/6 and up to800trees was a substantive nonlinear control, but still trained on incumbent-membership labels. Such labels do not distinguish alternate equally good schedules or measure marginal decision value. Learned route feature paths omit curvatureb/current congestion; capacity cannot infer inputs never supplied. Old GNN_day inference intervention is not a trained price-blind control. Old results/gates/failures are immutable; this source-only audit does not refit them or explain their cause conclusively.

Sources inspected by bounded agent: src/egglab/physical_route_graph_v6.py, physical_route_model_v3.py, physical_route_model_families_v5.py, learned_proposals.py; src/experiments/codex_train_classical.py; compact warm_replication conditioning/training-admission/results notes. No old weights, raw datasets or plans opened.

The new target supplies full observed background-load trajectories and known objective coefficients; main learning labels are grid outcomes, not arbitrary single-optimum route membership. It deliberately includes a correctly specified quadratic-regression control, nonlinear trees, two neural sizes/two initializations, a separate larger interpolation diagnostic, and separately trained blind controls. This can discriminate insufficient interpolation from poor generalization, weak decision surrogate or absent useful information in the new mechanism. It cannot retroactively prove the old GNN would fail at arbitrary capacity.

Decision-focused charging/energy forecasting is established prior art. Gu, Pan, Liu and Chen, [Learning and Optimization for Price-based Demand Response of Electric Vehicle Charging](https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.10311), ACC2024, combine demand-prediction and downstream charging costs. Sang, Xu, Long, Hu and Sun, [Electricity Price Prediction for Energy Storage System Arbitrage: A Decision-focused Approach](https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.00362),2023, train price forecasts through a regret surrogate. These primary abstracts were checked27Oct2026. Our finite-bank softmin loss and predict-then-optimize pipeline are not claimed novel. A positive mechanism test would justify developing a specific contribution around shared-resource discrete duty commitments with endogenous marginal costs; application conjunction alone is insufficient.

Earlier cheap pricing and represented fixed-bank optimum results concern known economic information. They do not settle value of information before a schedule commitment. Conversely a forecasting gain that only changes charging does not demonstrate route-learning value; the new fixed-topology conditional-reference contrast explicitly checks that distinction. No empirical uncertainty calibration, V2G/export settlement, omitted-route certificate or full ICML contribution follows from this synthetic pilot.
