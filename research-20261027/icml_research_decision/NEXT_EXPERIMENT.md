- **Ordinary complete retained pool:** permit the common fleet and all independently admitted full-discovery columns; jointly solve using the original partition/resources. This paid union is a prospective control, never a retroactive rescue of the previous campaign.
- **Direct joint completion:** same candidate universe and new-column count limit as the proposed selector, with the ordinary binary master from SCIENTIFIC_TARGET. Start from the same common fleet. A complete master is a mandatory baseline, not merely an offline teacher.
- **Classical recovery:** source-supported partition-aware diving, then a correctly qualified partition-compatible destroy/repair method if needed. Keep a no-dive control for the causal effect. A documented baseline that cannot represent the problem is unavailable, not a zero-score method.
- **Deterministic/resource-diverse and random augmentation:** same acquisition, cardinality, completion allowance and common incumbent. Preserve ordered physical witnesses; do not collapse distinct charge patterns by trip set.
