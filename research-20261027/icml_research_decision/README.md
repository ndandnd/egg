## Selected research direction

Investigate **budgeted integer completion after competent classical recovery**, not accelerated pricing on the failed effort-screen workload. The tentative learning hypothesis is conditional joint value of route/charge alternatives given the current pool and residual service/resource constraints. Generic integer-aware or set-based selection is already known; novelty and usefulness remain unestablished.

`SCIENTIFIC_TARGET.md` defines the target and a decisive conventional comparator. `PRIOR_ART.md` gives the closest collisions, including electric-bus work. `NEXT_EXPERIMENT.md` specifies the next discriminating research study and source seam. `literal_check.py` checks a small abstract integrality example; it does not generate a transport case, diagnose an old failure, or establish a learning opportunity.
