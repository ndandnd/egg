## Evidence-based diagnosis

Strongest plausible paper: a narrow analytical investigation of when indivisible,
fully replenished bus service assignments fail to be supported by an ex-ante
marginal tariff, with complete-fleet, individual-owner and strategic objectives
kept separate. Why it matters: solving a convexified energy problem or finding
a cheap feasible fleet does not by itself establish that the same tariff makes
that fleet a best response. The current distinctive evidence is a physically
explicit construction and participant-scale distinction, not a new general
pricing theorem or established learned algorithm.

Main obstacle: **significance and distinctiveness**, followed by external evidence.
General support/duality logic and whole-plan column generation are prior art.
Public optimal-fleet support remains unresolved; synthetic examples do not
establish tariff prevalence or monetary importance. No current ML result beats
strong classical baselines with meaningful residual routing value. A stronger
network did help prediction, so model inadequacy is not universally ruled out.
Do not promote the current state as a publishable ML method, broad V2G novelty,
market design, or operationally validated savings paper.
