## Paired route realizability is not established

The constructor/parser schema reads aligned matrix.distances and matrix.times arrays, checks shape/finite/nonnegative values, and exports their corresponding directed cells. build_depot_variant consumes each aligned cell's distance and time together in a modeled leg. These source operations state a modeled paired OD attribute; they do not supply a physical route ID, route geometry, or a guarantee that the distance and time were achieved jointly rather than minimized separately. Therefore project_od reports paired_leg_realizability=UNKNOWN. Passing componentwise triangle inequalities would not resolve that semantic question automatically. No new empirical assumption is introduced.

This is only a first public metadata-property gate. The existing exact sign certificate remains scoped to declared modes. Full mode completeness, canonical-window containment, exact energy dominance, charging access and any removal of the finite-mode scope remain downstream UNATTEMPTED until their distinct prerequisites and mathematical theorem are reviewed and qualified.
