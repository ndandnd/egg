## Target definition and remaining admission obligations

Use the same finite locations, fixed service timetable, selected depot d=15, vehicle/battery/fleet limits, connector/calendar, full initial and terminal inventory, and original binary supply objective. Each ordered OD cell is a primitive available arc carrying its jointly specified fixed time and distance. Nonnegative exact OD values, zero diagonals and directed triangles in both attributes are premises; the reported closed OD PASS can supply that premise only after exact identity transfer. Empirical realization of each pair by a common road path remains UNKNOWN and is unnecessary for this abstract arc model.

Define battery consumption EXACTLY by rational arithmetic:

    service = (6/5)*distance_km + 16*duration_hours;
    primitive travel q_ij = (24/25)*distance_km_ij + 16*tau_ij_hours;
    off-depot waiting = 16*duration_hours;
    parking at d = 0.

Services remain mandatory and consume nonnegatively; their coefficients change from the old rounded model to these exact formulas. Outside services, only d permits charging or free parking. A different location with a source depot flag is ordinary off-depot space for THIS declared target, unless excluded entirely. No other free parking, charging, mandatory excursion/task, service exchange, regeneration or time-dependent traffic is admitted. Transitions may use any finite walk over the primitive arcs, with arbitrary nonnegative waits. They do not acquire alternative primitive attributes by choosing a road path. Charging is charge-only with unit efficiency and the unchanged admissible rates/calendars; there is no session minimum, taper or setup penalty. Post-last-service charging access opens at absolute time zero, with the same terminal deadline regardless of visit label. Every counted bus serves at least one service; fees and all other non-grid operating costs are unchanged by canonical replacement (in particular there is no omitted movement-dependent monetary cost).

The canonical TARGET set must include the direct and one-via-d representatives whenever their timing predicates are feasible, with exact target energies, maximal depot windows, latest feasible full pullout and earliest final return. A reported canonical metadata PASS can establish time/incidence/old-source mapping, but does not by itself create target coefficients or settle a changed access policy. Admission must explicitly bind the target definition and check endpoint cases. In particular, an old constructor rejection of waits at ANY flagged depot cannot silently become completeness for the new selected-depot-only rule. A mathematical canonical target may include the needed alternatives; an implementation omitting one cannot claim the theorem.

## Canonical value-preservation theorem

Under the preceding conditions, every feasible broader target schedule has a canonical target schedule with the same service assignments/order and fleet, and componentwise no greater charging energy in every market period. Therefore physical-planner and complete-plan convexified infimum values agree between the two target opportunity sets for coordinatewise nondecreasing supply cost. The result preserves values and provides dominating representatives; it does NOT assert equality of full attainable load sets or every price-response tie.

**Proof.** Put rho=16 in hourly units. Exact travel q has directed triangle inequalities because it is a nonnegative linear combination of distance and time. Reduced weights w_ij=q_ij−rho*tau_ij=(24/25)*distance_km_ij also have directed triangles. Thus the exact conditions of EXACT_ENERGY_DOMINANCE_LEMMA.md hold with equality between canonical and primitive/linear-wait laws; there is no stored-total rounding seam in this new target.

For a service gap with no visit to d, all its waits consume rho per hour. If its fixed duration is T, consumption is rho*T+sum w_arc, at least rho*T+w_ij. Direct travel followed by the requisite off-depot wait attains that lower value and fits by the time triangle. Both old and new trajectories only consume energy during the gap; adequate ending SOC therefore guarantees every intermediate lower bound. Capacity is preserved because neither charges. A gap with an endpoint d counts as visiting d, even when the endpoint arc has zero duration; it is not put through the off-depot waiting argument.

For a gap visiting d, replace the prefix to its first arrival by immediate direct inbound travel and the suffix from its last departure by latest direct outbound travel. Time triangles enlarge the depot window, while q triangles and nonnegative off-depot waits weakly lower both consumptions. Remain parked throughout instead of taking intermediate excursions. Keep old charging at its original clocks and only curtail when the larger SOC would exceed capacity. Unit efficiency, zero depot draw and no minimum session make this feasible. Deleting nonnegative excursions cannot create an energy shortage; cheaper outbound consumption preserves SOC at the next service. Curtailment never increases any individual rate or shared resource use. Zero diagonal/zero-energy arcs and repeated finite cycles are harmless: the same replacement removes them without an added charging opportunity or mandatory task.

Before the first service, latest full direct pullout weakly lowers consumption from the fixed initial full battery; pre-service excursions provide no useful energy source. After the last service, earliest direct return weakly lowers consumption and contains all former charging clocks. The common opening-zero policy prevents any visit-label access loophole; retained charging with capacity curtailment ends full because the original ends full. Services themselves are unchanged within the target. Apply the replacements successively along each duty. At every step SOC at the next service is no smaller, so the induction preserves service feasibility. Purchases only decrease at existing clocks, establishing componentwise market-load dominance with unchanged fleet cost.

Canonical schedules are themselves broader target schedules, giving one inequality for each infimum. The dominance map gives the reverse physical inequality under monotone supply. Replace EACH component in any finite complete-plan convex mixture; its aggregate load weakly decreases and operating cost is unchanged, proving the convexified inequality as well. Canonical compactness, if needed for attained optima and support conclusions, follows from a finite duty/mode universe, bounded fleet, finite resource partition and closed bounded continuous charging variables. The broader finite-walk set need not itself be compact for these value equalities. No claim about negative-price value queries follows. Nonnegative prices preserve private-score values; zero prices can leave additional dominated schedules tied.

## Honest scientific consequence

If subsequently established target-valid bounds separate ALL target physical plans from a target-feasible hull mixture, the classical own-marginal-price support-failure conclusion applies to the broader modeled-walk class through this theorem, under its usual differentiability/attainment assumptions. The known binary-model gap is not such a target-valid separation. Both exact lower certificates and every upper component need fresh target admission. Keeping the original binary SUPPLY function is compatible with the theorem when it is convex and coordinatewise nondecreasing on the target load domain; changing consumption does not make its old tangent/certificate rows valid automatically.

The defensible prospective claim is therefore “canonical reduction for arbitrary finite walks over these declared paired arcs under the exact rational consumption law and common access policy.” It is not a retroactive extension of the old result, empirical road completeness, physical calibration, or robustness of the original schedules to rounding. New target evidence and its costs/outcomes must be reported separately, including failure or unresolved status.
