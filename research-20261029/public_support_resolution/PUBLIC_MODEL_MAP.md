# Public depot-15 support resolution: source map and prospective computation

Prepared 29 October 2026 from source and compact historical reviews only. No model import, solver, arithmetic replay, hashing, old-job rerun, or old-namespace edit was performed. This note is a proposal, not a new numerical result. Paths below are repository-relative to `ml-prediction-20261017`.

## The exact question

Use the same complete 37-service, depot-15, finite 30-hour physical case as `paper/latex/public_case.tex`, with the original quadratic market. Write X for every feasible complete fleet, e(x) for its 30 hourly grid loads, and c(x)=100 times its bus count. The unresolved quantities are

`D = min_{x in X} c(x)+sum_t[a_t e_t(x)+b_t e_t(x)^2/2]`

and the corresponding minimum CH over the convex hull of projected complete plans `(e,c)`. Here displayed coefficients are a=0.2 and b=1/900. The *ideal stored-input* target interprets the actual stored binary numerical coefficients as exact rationals, rather than silently replacing them by decimal fractions. The audited original coefficients are `a=3602879701896397/18014398509481984` and `b=5124095576030431/4611686018427387904`.

Price support requires an optimal physical fleet x to minimize `c(y)+gradient F(e(x)) dot e(y)` over **all** complete fleets y. Large regret at a named historical incumbent cannot settle optimal-fleet support. Flat-price equality between physical and hull values also cannot answer the quadratic question.

## Smallest rigorous new computation

Freeze one scientific cell only: full depot-15 original market, unchanged equipment/movement graph and stored-rational semantics. Begin with the passed rational witness as an upper-bound/control input, not a claimed new discovery. Do not pool depot variants, sensitivity studies, sealed data or old runs into a new empirical cohort.

Use a strong classical baseline: the compact complete-fleet MILP pricing oracle plus convex quadratic tangent outer approximation for physical planning, and a fully corrective finite-column quadratic hull master with global pricing. A direct convex MIQP on the same compact formulation is an additional useful baseline if the prospectively pinned backend supports it; availability must be checked before promising it. Compare common certified-bound targets and inclusive wall/CPU costs rather than just incumbent quality or solve-call count. Learning is unnecessary for resolving this cell.

The smallest decisive *positive-gap* certificate need not identify D and CH separately: obtain an independently replayed rational convex mixture with upper U_CH, then certify a rational physical lower L_D strictly above U_CH. Finite rational tangents under-estimate the physical quadratic objective globally. A complete branch-and-bound certificate for the resulting rational MILP (exact checked LP dual/Farkas leaves and exhaustive branch coverage) would certify L_D. Native solver bounds may guide construction but cannot replace that certificate. This requires new certificate machinery; the existing APIs alone do not provide it.

A decisive *support* certificate instead needs one exact feasible physical plan x whose exact own-price linear optimization has the same value as x. Convexity then proves x globally optimal and D=CH without a separate quadratic global solve. Rational fixed-topology convex-QP KKT reconstruction can produce/check a candidate charging schedule and its gradient, but fixed-topology optimality alone does not exclude a better routing topology. Check the resulting own-price problem over the full graph and all admitted bus counts with an independent exact global certificate.

Important fleet caveat: minimum feasible cardinality two does **not** prove nonlinear optimal fleets use two buses. For D, three-or-more buses may be excluded only using a valid nonlinear cardinality lower bound above the current physical upper. For CH, excluding high-cardinality plans by their individual nonlinear values is invalid: mixtures can improve supply cost. Instead certify their linear-price lower bound at each tested price, e.g. `300+min_t(p_t)*E_service` for nonnegative prices, against the relevant pricing upper, or retain them in the oracle. Two-bus flow floors can strengthen the other branch. No model restriction should be inferred from the historical two-bus incumbent.

Independent verification should reconstruct exact event SOC, each selected directed leg, coverage, movement-specific charge windows, connector serialization, hourly loads and objective from the frozen source coefficients. Check rational LP/KKT certificates against independently assembled constraints rather than imported author matrix objects. Corrupt coverage, terminal fullness, shared power, times, objective coefficients and a proof-tree leaf as negative controls. If the new exact certificate machinery does not close within the prospectively frozen budget, report explicit new bounds and unresolved support; do not substitute native tolerance-qualified closure or incumbent regret for the missing ideal-model proof.
