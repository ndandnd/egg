# Focused V2G primary-literature review

Reviewed 2026-10-29. Read the current related-work section and independent V2G proof review first. This bounded search assessed four primary works: two accessible full texts and two bus-specific works with abstract-level access only. No numerical work, model execution, public-data inspection, hashes or commits. Previously inspected Mukhi and Borndörfer sections were not re-audited; Hao was not used.

## Assessment

No duplicate of the exact shared-gross-throughput matching threshold, cost-labelled frontier, or paid-idle elimination proposition was identified in the two inspected full texts. This is a limited negative finding, not novelty verification. V2G scheduling, signed aggregate flexibility and optimization against posted energy prices are already established. The stronger defensible candidate remains the explicit joint load–fleet-cost projection and its consequence for physical attainment of convexified optima under a deliberately simple physical model. The two exact persistence/restoration examples illustrate that classification; they do not independently establish significance.

## Primary sources and access limits

**Blatiak, Bellizio, Badesa and Strbac (2022), “Value of Optimal Trip and Charging Scheduling of Commercial Electric Vehicle Fleets with Vehicle-to-Grid in Future Low Inertia Systems.” Full text inspected, Sections 1.2 and 2.1–2.2, equations (1)–(7), (15).** Their model chooses trip timing and charging/discharging against energy and balancing-service prices, with inventory and charger limits. Buy/sell prices are distinct, and Section 1.2 sets energy sell price to zero to avoid energy arbitrage. Each EV undertakes at most one extended daily trip; Section 2.2 chooses its start time. This directly rules out a first combination of endogenous trip scheduling and V2G market revenues. The inspected formulation does not provide a two-batch successor-cardinality threshold, an endogenous paid fleet-cost frontier, or a marginal-support classification. These are model-level distinctions, not claims that their operational problem is subsumed by ours. [Author full text](https://arxiv.org/html/2204.11565).

**Al Taha, Vincent and Bitar (arXiv v3, 2024; IEEE TSG DOI 10.1109/TSG.2024.3384871), “An Efficient Method for Quantifying the Aggregate Flexibility of Plug-in Electric Vehicle Populations.” Full text inspected, Sections II-A–II-D, Example 1, Remark 2, equations (1)–(10), and Sections III–IV.** Signed charging, initial/final energy requirements and lossless battery dynamics are explicit. Their fixed-population aggregate set is a Minkowski sum of individual convex polytopes; affine inner approximations and disaggregation address computational representation. Thus neither signed-load battery aggregation nor the lossless convex battery model is new here. Our shared connector creates a coupled gross-throughput constraint, while assignment/cardinality changes create different cost layers. I found no corresponding matching-cost threshold or paid-idle edge-deletion result in the inspected text. Their flexibility representation is much more general in time; our scalar interval is analytically simpler. [Author full text](https://arxiv.org/html/2207.07067).

**Zhou, An and Schmöcker (2025), “Optimization of charging and discharging schedules for battery electric buses under the V2G environment,” Transportmetrica B 13(1), 2506689. Abstract/repository metadata only.** The primary repository describes a multi-depot mixed-integer charging/discharging model, spare buses earning discharge revenues, and cross-line operation. This is a particularly important precedent for the idle/spare-bus interpretation: allowing such buses is not a new V2G concept. The record advertises an accepted manuscript, but the repository opened without readable file links and the publisher full-text request returned 403. I therefore cannot rule out closer assignment, fleet-cost or structural results inside it, and cannot assert a section-level distinction. [Primary repository](https://repository.kulib.kyoto-u.ac.jp/handle/2433/300766), [published DOI](https://doi.org/10.1080/21680566.2025.2506689).

**Kang, Lee and Lee (2025), “A Robust Optimization Approach for E-Bus Charging and Discharging Scheduling with Vehicle-to-Grid Integration,” Mathematics 13(9), 1380. Publisher abstract only.** It describes robust mixed-integer depot scheduling with uncertain trip consumption and discharge requests, dual-port chargers, emergency charging and demand response. This precludes novelty claims based simply on V2G buses sharing charging infrastructure or price-sensitive depot operation. The publisher HTML returned 429 and PDF/XML requests were inaccessible; the version record confirms an updated version of record but does not expose mathematical sections. No claim is made about absence of a matching theorem in the full paper. [Publisher article](https://www.mdpi.com/2227-7390/13/9/1380), [version record](https://www.mdpi.com/2227-7390/13/9/1380/notes).

## Claim boundary recommended for the draft

Describe the contribution as an exact classification for a two-batch lossless model, with fixed allowed successor graph, singleton feasibility, equal paid battery types, common windows, full terminal replenishment and signed settlement at the same posted price. State the new mathematical ingredients concretely: minimum gross throughput max(E,2R−E); threshold max(S−H,S−NB,2r_m−K); the resulting cost-labelled frontier; and removal of capped paid idle buses by deleting matching edges. Standard battery aggregation, minimum-cost matching convexity, convexification and price-support equivalence should remain attributed machinery.

The idle-bus proposition proves an exact redundancy under these assumptions, rather than discovering spare-bus V2G value. The uncapped H=S dominance statement concerns the lower frontier, not equality of the literal projection. Neither persistence nor restoration should be described as surprising empirical behavior, and neither supports a claim about lossy batteries, asymmetric export tariffs, realistic multi-window bus networks or the unchanged public 37-service case.

The narrow next literature obstacle is the inaccessible Zhou et al. full formulation; the present review cannot certify nonduplication there. Even if no exact predecessor exists, the closed form may be judged a straightforward specialization of standard matching and convex analysis. A significance case must therefore rest on the transparent physical mechanism and usable classification, with these restrictions made visible. Do not promote it to a first V2G result or a universal market-design theorem.
