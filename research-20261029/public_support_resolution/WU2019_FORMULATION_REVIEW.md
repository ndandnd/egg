## What the article itself supports

The [publisher abstract and section preview](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0306261919314850) describe bus service planning coupled with electricity market clearing through a bilevel formulation and distribution locational marginal prices. The preview identifies bus planning §2.2, market clearing §2.3, DLMP §2.4 and an MPCC implementation §2.5. Thus bus-operation/endogenous-price coupling is already a direct precedent. The text available here does not supply the complete constraints, objective derivatives or equilibrium proof. It does not justify classifying the operator as a price taker or strategic price maker solely from the term “bilevel.” No article equation/page number is asserted.

## Related primary thesis: useful lead, not a substitute article

The search-indexed [Imperial repository record](https://spiral.imperial.ac.uk/entities/publication/d6d0c721-5169-4a34-a488-741e632aa002) identifies Zongxiang Wu's *Double demand responsive electric bus operation to passengers and power system*, issued October2018, awarded March2019, DOI10.25560/82300; file `Wu-Z-2019-PhD-Thesis.pdf` (9.77MB). Its abstract explicitly calls one framework a Stackelberg model with an electricity-pool lower level producing distribution prices. It separately describes adaptive service scheduling with short-turning/interlining. This is stronger evidence that closely related strategic and endogenous-operation modeling existed, but neither establishes which thesis equations became the journal article nor verifies that the article uses identical assumptions. No thesis fulltext was inspected.
