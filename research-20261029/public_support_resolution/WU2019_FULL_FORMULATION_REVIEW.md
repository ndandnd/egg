## What can be established, and what cannot

The [publisher preview](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0306261919314850) establishes a close precedent: bus service planning is coupled with electricity clearing through a bilevel formulation and DLMP-based load management. It reports operational/numerical comparisons and congestion effects. This is sufficient to reject novelty based merely on endogenous prices, service planning, or grid–bus coupling. It is insufficient to establish which party anticipates price changes during a deviation or how integer decisions enter the pricing conditions.

The decisive comparison requires its actual bus-planning, clearing, DLMP and equilibrium/MPCC formulation.
