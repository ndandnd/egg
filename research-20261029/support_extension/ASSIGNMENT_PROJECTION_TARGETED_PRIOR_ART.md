## Direct overlap test

An actual subsumption would need to establish the following chain (possibly under different notation): endogenous service pairing changes the number of paid vehicles; for every fixed cardinality, one minimum sum of positive early requirements contains the **whole** aggregate purchase/export feasible set of every other assignment; a shared gross-time constraint and terminal refill remain exact; and every resulting polygon point admits individual SOC-feasible finite serial actions. Common conversion losses must preserve this chain, not merely appear in a generic MILP. Vehicle-cost layers must survive the projection rather than being optimized away for one tariff.

The inspected new material did not verify that chain. Equally, the accessible snippets cannot prove it is missing. The station-choice source demonstrates why “endogenous allocation plus exact aggregation” would be too broad a novelty claim: related institutions already combine those themes. The SSRN lead remains unresolved rather than being classified as merely fixed-population from its title.

## Implications for the current paper

Retain the narrow structural statement and the positive operational-overlap citations. Do not claim first assignment-aware aggregation, first joint fleet/charging optimization, generic battery convexification, or novel matching/vertex machinery. The present candidate's meaningful distinction is the scalar matching statistic's sufficiency for an objective-independent **cost-labelled full serial polygon**, under explicitly ideal common windows, efficiencies, switching and settlement.

No clear negative novelty finding was proved, but no stronger priority claim was earned either. The strongest outstanding objection remains elementary synthesis under restrictive physics; a wider scheduling/resource-allocation theorem might still subsume the reduction. This bounded audit did not systematically cover interchangeable job-machine or storage-assignment theory. More favorable numerical examples cannot settle that question. A future legitimate full text of the two most relevant new leads would warrant a precise formulation comparison; no repeated access loop or additional search is authorized by this note.
