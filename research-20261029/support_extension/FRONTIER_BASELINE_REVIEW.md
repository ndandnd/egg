# Prospective frontier-versus-MIQP baseline: conceptual review

**Research value is bounded but real:** a careful comparison could show the computational consequence of the common-loss structural reduction within its exact two-batch class. It would be a classical algorithm/formulation comparison, not a new ML benefit, public-timetable scalability claim or evidence of practical prevalence. The existing theorem and finite implementation qualification do not themselves establish a runtime advantage, and a runtime experiment is unnecessary to prove the reduction.

Both methods must optimize the same physical D on the same allowed graph, singleton availability, common battery/efficiencies, positive bus fee, zero reserve/travel, shared grid-side gross early connector and terminal refill resources. All service-assigned buses share the charging windows; per-bus acceptance equals connector power. Optional identical idle buses require the same explicit total cap or a separately justified exclusion on both sides. No fixed favorable pairing, extra resource scaling, omitted infeasible cardinality, net-only connector budget, free initial depletion or unlisted charging access may enter either formulation.

The finite-frontier algorithm may use r_m and at most two affine pieces per cardinality because every physical point is dominated by an attainable frontier point. This gives equal **optimal values** for coordinatewise nondecreasing supply on the entire admitted signed domain. It does not give equality of complete physical projections or every zero-price response. A direct formulation can retain within-bus cycles, or remove them through the same proved dominance; cycle-free complementarity must not be dropped for arbitrary objectives and then called an equivalent physical set. Convexity alone, equal export tariffs alone, or monotonicity only near a selected optimum is insufficient. Lossless scalar-support formulas must not be substituted for the lossy two-load frontier.

A strong direct baseline should use a compact allowed-matching/flow formulation and reasonable symmetry reduction, rather than deliberately label 2n interchangeable buses and leave massive avoidable symmetry. State its sign/cycle handling, valid dominance cuts, presolve, tolerances, hardware, threads and cold/warm policy. Give both methods the same input/preprocessing opportunity; either may exploit established consequences of the declared model. Computing all r_m, constructing breakpoints, exact arithmetic, admission and publication count in the frontier method's cost. Charging-model construction, native startup and any exact post-check count for MIQP. Reusing an analytically supplied optimum or frontier as a one-sided warm start would require a separate, clearly charged experimental condition.

Compare physical D with physical D. CH from conv(Z) is a different task: a direct MIQP physical optimum or generic continuous relaxation is not automatically the complete-plan hull optimum. Native floating OPTIMAL/objective equality is also a different guarantee from an exact rational optimum with an attainable witness. Report matched values and verification boundaries first; timeouts, failed admission and preparation costs remain in denominators. Exact certification work must be charged or explicitly excluded for both, rather than describing unlike guarantees as a speed comparison.

A prospectively fixed, balanced input policy could measure runtime, memory, completion and exact/native agreement across declared sizes and graph/resource regimes. Do not select unusually scarce, symmetric or infeasible instances after observing which solver loses; fixed K/H scaling must be stated because it changes feasibility as n grows. Results could support “this implementation was faster than this strong baseline on this declared suite.” They could not establish asymptotic superiority, universal solver dominance, unrestricted EVSP performance, calibrated savings, or a neural-learning contribution. If a strong compact baseline already solves the class cheaply, reporting that would be useful evidence that further computational benchmarking adds little beyond the structural theorem.

Source-only conceptual assessment; no case selection, implementation, data, computation, imports, tests, hashes, native work or commits.
