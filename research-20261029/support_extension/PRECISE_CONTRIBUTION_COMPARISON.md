## The claim worth leading with

**A physical reduction that retains vehicle cost while assignments change.** For the declared two simultaneous service batches, allowed successor graph, full start/end batteries and fixed single early/terminal connectors, the charge-only theorem gives the complete attainable joint image as cardinality-labelled intervals. Classical minimum-cost matching values r_m determine their lower endpoints; vehicle fees determine their distinct cost layers. The load-only set is an interval, so its nonconvexity is not the contribution. The relevant object is the cheapest physical vehicle cost at each load versus its convexified interpolation.

This representation answers an entire family of planning/support questions, rather than returning only one optimum for one chosen objective. For strictly convex supply restricted to the lossless constant-total-energy line, its hull minimizer is unique; physical attainment at the retained thresholds or final horizontal segment is exactly the zero-gap condition. Interior locations between those retained thresholds give a positive gap, subject to the stated marginal-price admissibility qualifications. Clipped boundaries, collinear physical thresholds, zero fee and infeasible cardinalities require the paper's explicit exceptions.

With common bidirectional losses the honest claim changes: **the efficient image**, not the full physical image, is obtained by Pareto-pruning attainable curves with at most two affine segments per matching cardinality. The same r_m suffices despite sign choices, gross connector use and terminal conversion losses. Dominated cycles/allocations can be removed only for coordinatewise monotone planning or nonnegative-price response values. This gives a finite hull program and supporting-score checks; it does **not** give the lossless scalar threshold-location support classification unchanged.

## What a reader can decide, and what remains classical

Once r_m is known, the charge-only representation identifies feasible vehicle-count/load layers, their hull boundary and the physical attainment of any strictly convex hull optimum without enumerating complete assignments again. The lossless V2G thresholds explain when gross discharge flexibility preserves or restores support. Common losses preserve a finite efficient decision representation: at most two one-dimensional physical subproblems per cardinality, a hull program over finitely many endpoints/kinks, and a certificate consisting of feasible mixture weights and private-score inequalities. At zero prices this decides response **value**, not every tied physical response.

A generic MILP or global oracle can also answer a specified instance/objective, potentially with richer physics. The difference is the explicit representation and parameter explanation, not that optimization models cannot answer support questions. Min-cost cardinality matching, its convex value sequence, ordered exchange arguments, convex segment minimization, finite hull generation and supporting-plane certificates are classical. Rational exactness additionally requires rational generators/coefficients and gives existence of a rational optimum, not rationality of every flat optimum.

## Remaining significance and novelty uncertainty

No equivalent assignment-cardinality/shared-gross-connector lossy frontier theorem was identified in the previously inspected sections. That bounded observation does not clear the wider fixed-charge scheduling/aggregation literature. The strongest rejection is still that the restricted theorem may be viewed as an elementary combination of box aggregation, positive-part algebra, matching and classical nonconvex pricing. The proof's real burden is exhaustive physical attainment/dominance with fixed resources; repeated solved examples cannot establish its originality.

The useful presentation is therefore one clear complete-projection theorem, the lossless support regimes it enables, and the distinct common-loss efficient reduction, with operational assumptions visible. Staggered access, transition energy/time, heterogeneous efficiencies or a different terminal/access policy do not follow automatically. The separate public certificate adds existence within its declared charge-only graph, not empirical validity or a theorem for arbitrary bus timetables. These precise boundaries make a plausible structural contribution legible while leaving its originality and broader significance open to expert judgment.
